An undirected graph is \emph{planar} if it has an embedding in the plane in which vertices are distinct points and each edge is drawn as a curve joining its endpoints, with distinct edge curves intersecting only at common endpoints. Planarity refers to the existence of such a drawing, not to whether a particular drawing happens to have crossings.
