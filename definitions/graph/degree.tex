For a vertex $v$ of a finite simple undirected graph, its \emph{degree} $\deg(v)$ is the number of edges incident with $v$, equivalently the number of its neighbors. In a graph with loops, the usual undirected-degree convention counts each loop twice; directed graphs instead have in-degrees and out-degrees.
