An undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ is \emph{bipartite} if its vertex set can be partitioned as $V=V_1\sqcup V_2$ with every edge joining a vertex of $V_1$ to one of $V_2$. Equivalently, its vertices admit a proper two-coloring; for a finite graph this is also equivalent to having no odd cycle. The parts need not have equal sizes.
