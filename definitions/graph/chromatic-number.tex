The \emph{chromatic number} $\chi(G)$ of a simple undirected graph $G$ is the least number of colors needed to assign colors to its vertices so that adjacent vertices receive different colors. The empty graph has chromatic number $0$ under this convention; a nonempty edgeless graph has chromatic number $1$.
