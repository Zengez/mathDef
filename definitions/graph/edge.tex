For a simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ with $E\subseteq\{\{u,v\}:u,v\in V,\ u\ne v\}$, an \emph{edge} is a two-element subset $\{u,v\}\in E$ of the vertex set. It records adjacency without orientation. In multigraphs or graphs allowing loops, an edge may need a different formal representation.
