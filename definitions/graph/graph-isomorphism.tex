An \emph{isomorphism} between simple undirected graphs $G=(V,E)$ and $H=(W,F)$ is a bijection $\phi:V\to W$ such that
\[
\{u,v\}\in E\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\{\phi(u),\phi(v)\}\in F
\]
for every $u,v\in V$. Thus both adjacency and nonadjacency are preserved, and the graphs differ only by vertex labels.
