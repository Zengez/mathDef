In a simple undirected graph, a \emph{cycle} of length $k\ge3$ is a sequence $v_0,v_1,\ldots,v_{k-1},v_0$ with $v_0,\ldots,v_{k-1}$ pairwise distinct and an edge between each consecutive pair, including $v_{k-1}$ and $v_0$. Multigraphs may allow cycles of length $1$ or $2$, but not under this simple-graph convention.
