A \emph{(simple) path} in an undirected graph is a finite sequence $v_0,\ldots,v_k$ of pairwise distinct vertices, where $k\ge0$ and $\{v_{i-1},v_i\}$ is an edge for $1\le i\le k$. Its length is $k$; the case $k=0$ is a single-vertex path. A walk, by contrast, may revisit vertices.
