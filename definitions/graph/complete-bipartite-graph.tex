The \emph{complete bipartite graph} $K_{m,n}$, for integers $m,n\ge0$, is a simple graph whose vertices are divided into two parts of sizes $m$ and $n$, with an edge between every vertex of the first part and every vertex of the second and no edges within a part. It has $m+n$ vertices and $mn$ edges.
