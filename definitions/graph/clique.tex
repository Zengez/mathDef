A \emph{clique} in a simple undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ is a set $C\subseteq V$ such that every pair of distinct vertices of $C$ is joined by an edge. Equivalently, the induced subgraph $G[C]$ is complete. A \emph{maximal clique} cannot be enlarged, while a \emph{maximum clique} has largest cardinality; these notions differ.
