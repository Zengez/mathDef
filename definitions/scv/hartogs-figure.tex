For $0<r,s<1$ and $\mathbb D_a=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<a\}$, the \emph{Hartogs figure} in $\mathbb C^2$ is $H_{r,s}=(\mathbb D_r\times\mathbb D)\cup(\mathbb D\times(\mathbb D\setminus\overline{\mathbb D_s}))$. It is a connected open subset of the bidisc used in holomorphic-extension problems.
