% term: Standard Hartogs figure
% related: scv.complex-polydisc, scv.envelope-of-holomorphy
% source: https://alphabetamath.cc/item/thm-hartogs-figure-extension
% status: draft

For $0<r,s<1$ and $\mathbb D_t=\{z\in\mathbb C:|z|<t\}$, a \emph{standard Hartogs figure} in $\mathbb C^2$ is
\[
H_{r,s}=(\mathbb D_r\times\mathbb D)\,\cup\,\bigl(\mathbb D\times(\mathbb D\setminus\overline{\mathbb D_s})\bigr).
\]
It is a connected open subset of the unit bidisc. Every holomorphic function on this figure extends uniquely to the full bidisc; the precise arrangement of the coordinates is one of several equivalent standard conventions.
