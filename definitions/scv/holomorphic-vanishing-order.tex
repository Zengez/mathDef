% term: Vanishing order of a holomorphic germ
% related: scv.holomorphic-function-germ, scv.weierstrass-polynomial
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weierstrass_theorem
% status: draft

For a holomorphic function $f$ near $p\in\mathbb C^n$ not identically zero near $p$, its \emph{order of vanishing at $p$} is the minimum $|\alpha|$ over nonzero Taylor coefficients $c_\alpha$ in $f(p+z)=\sum_\alpha c_\alpha z^\alpha$. The order of the zero germ is conventionally $+\infty$.
