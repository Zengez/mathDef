% id: scv.holomorphic-vanishing-order
% term: Vanishing order of a holomorphic germ
% related: scv.holomorphic-function-germ, scv.weierstrass-polynomial
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weierstrass_theorem
% status: draft

For a holomorphic germ $f$ at $p\in\mathbb C^n$, with convergent Taylor expansion $f(p+z)=\sum_{\alpha\in\mathbb N_0^n}c_\alpha z^\alpha$, the \emph{order of vanishing} is
\[
\operatorname{ord}_p(f)=\min\{|\alpha|:c_\alpha\ne0\}
\]
when $f$ is not the zero germ. It is $0$ if $f(p)\ne0$, and by convention $\operatorname{ord}_p(0)=+\infty$. This is the order in the maximal ideal of the regular local ring $\mathcal O_{\mathbb C^n,p}$.
