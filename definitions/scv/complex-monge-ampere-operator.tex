For a real-valued $C^2$ function $u$ on a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{complex Monge--Ampère operator in determinant normalization} is
\[
\operatorname{MA}_{\mathbb C}(u)=\det\left(\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial z_j\partial\overline z_k}\right)_{j,k=1}^{n}.
\]
For plurisubharmonic $u$ the complex Hessian is positive semidefinite, so this determinant is nonnegative. The associated Monge--Ampère measure $(dd^c u)^n$ differs by a fixed constant depending on the normalization of $d^c$; extension to nonsmooth functions requires additional pluripotential theory.
