% term: Complex Monge--Ampère operator (smooth case)
% related: scv.levi-form, scv.plurisubharmonic-function
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Green_function
% status: draft

For a real $C^2$ function $u$ on a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, its \emph{complex Monge--Ampère operator} in determinant normalization is $\operatorname{MA}_{\mathbb C}(u)=\det\bigl(u_{z_j\bar z_k}\bigr)_{j,k=1}^n$. If $u$ is plurisubharmonic, the determinant is nonnegative; the associated measure $(dd^cu)^n$ differs by a convention-dependent constant factor.
