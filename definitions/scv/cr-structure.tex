An \emph{integrable CR structure} on a smooth real manifold $M$ is a constant-rank complex subbundle $T^{0,1}M\subseteq TM\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$ satisfying
\[
T^{0,1}M\cap\overline{T^{0,1}M}=\{0\},\qquad
[\Gamma(T^{0,1}M),\Gamma(T^{0,1}M)]\subseteq\Gamma(T^{0,1}M).
\]
The complex rank of $T^{0,1}M$ is its CR dimension. The bracket-involutivity requirement is the formal integrability condition.
