An \emph{integrable CR structure} on a smooth real manifold $M$ is a constant-rank complex subbundle $T^{0,1}M\subseteq TM\otimes_{\mathbb R}\mathbb C$ satisfying $T^{0,1}M\cap\overline{T^{0,1}M}=\{0\}$ and $[\Gamma(T^{0,1}M),\Gamma(T^{0,1}M)]\subseteq\Gamma(T^{0,1}M)$. The rank is its CR dimension.
