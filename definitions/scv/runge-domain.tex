% status: draft

A domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{Runge in $\mathbb C^n$} if each $f\in\mathcal O(\Omega)$ can be approximated uniformly on every compact subset of $\Omega$ by polynomials in $z_1,\ldots,z_n$. Equivalently, polynomial restrictions are dense in $\mathcal O(\Omega)$ with its compact-open topology.
