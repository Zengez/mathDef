% term: Runge domain (polynomial approximation)
% related: scv.domain-of-holomorphy, scv.runge-pair
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Runge_domain
% status: draft

A domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{Runge in $\mathbb C^n$} if every $f\in\mathcal O(\Omega)$ can be approximated uniformly on compact subsets of $\Omega$ by complex polynomials in $n$ variables.
