% term: Bergman--Shilov boundary of a bounded domain

% status: draft

For a bounded domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, form the uniform algebra on $\overline\Omega$ generated by functions holomorphic on neighborhoods of $\overline\Omega$ and their uniform limits. Its \emph{Bergman--Shilov boundary} is the Shilov boundary of this algebra; by the maximum principle it is contained in $\partial\Omega$. This notion is distinct from the Bergman kernel.
