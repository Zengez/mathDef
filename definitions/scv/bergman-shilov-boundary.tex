% term: Bergman--Shilov boundary of a bounded domain

% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Bergman-Shilov_boundary
% status: draft

For a bounded domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, consider the uniform algebra on $\overline\Omega$ obtained by uniformly closing functions holomorphic on a neighborhood of $\overline\Omega$. Its \emph{Bergman--Shilov boundary} is the smallest closed subset $S\subseteq\overline\Omega$ on which every function in this algebra attains its supremum modulus. By the maximum-modulus principle, $S\subseteq\partial\Omega$. The boundary depends on the function algebra and is distinct from the Bergman reproducing kernel.
