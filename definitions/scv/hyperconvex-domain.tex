A bounded domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{hyperconvex under the continuous-exhaustion convention} if there exists a continuous negative plurisubharmonic function $\rho$ on $\Omega$ such that $\{\rho<c\}$ is relatively compact in $\Omega$ for every $c<0$. The negative-level restriction is essential; some related definitions weaken continuity.
