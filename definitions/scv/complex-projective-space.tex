The \emph{complex projective $n$-space} is
\[
\mathbb CP^n=(\mathbb C^{n+1}\setminus\{0\})/\mathbb C^\times,
\]
the space of one-dimensional complex linear subspaces of $\mathbb C^{n+1}$, with its natural complex-manifold structure; the quotient identifies nonzero scalar multiples.
