In the affine chart $[1:z_1:\cdots:z_n]$ of $\mathbb CP^n$, one normalization of the \emph{Fubini–Study form} is
\[
\omega_{\mathrm{FS}}=i\,\partial\bar\partial\log\left(1+\sum_{j=1}^n|z_j|^2\right).
\]
It defines a positive Kähler metric on projective space; constant normalizations vary.
