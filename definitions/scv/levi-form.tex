% id: scv.levi-form
% term: Levi form of a real-valued function
% related: scv.plurisubharmonic-function, scv.strictly-plurisubharmonic

% status: draft

For real-valued $u\in C^2(\Omega)$ on open $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, its \emph{Levi form} at $z$ is the Hermitian form
\[
\mathcal L_u(z;v)=\sum_{j,k=1}^n\frac{\partial^2 u}{\partial z_j\,\partial\bar z_k}(z)\,v_j\bar v_k,\qquad v\in\mathbb C^n.
\]
