% term: Envelope of holomorphy
% related: scv.domain-of-holomorphy, scv.complex-manifold
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Holomorphic_envelope
% status: draft

For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, its \emph{envelope of holomorphy} is a Riemann domain $\pi:E\to\mathbb C^n$ with an embedding of $\Omega$ over $\mathbb C^n$ such that every holomorphic function on $\Omega$ extends holomorphically to $E$, satisfying the maximal universal extension property. The projection $\pi$ need not be injective, so the envelope may be multisheeted.
