% term: Envelope of holomorphy

% status: draft

For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, its \emph{envelope of holomorphy} is the maximal Riemann domain $E$ over $\mathbb C^n$ into which $\Omega$ embeds compatibly with the projection and to which every holomorphic function on $\Omega$ extends. The envelope need not be a single-sheeted domain in $\mathbb C^n$.
