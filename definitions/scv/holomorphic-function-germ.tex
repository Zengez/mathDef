% id: scv.holomorphic-function-germ
% term: Germ of a holomorphic function
% related: scv.complex-manifold, scv.analytic-set-germ
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Germ
% status: draft

For a complex manifold $X$ and $p\in X$, the \emph{germ at $p$} of a holomorphic function is an equivalence class of pairs $(U,f)$ with $p\in U\subseteq X$ open and $f\in\mathcal O(U)$. Two representatives agree as germs when their restrictions agree on some neighborhood of $p$.
