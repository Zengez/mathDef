% term: Germ of a holomorphic function
% related: scv.complex-manifold, scv.analytic-set-germ
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Germ
% status: draft

For a complex manifold $X$ and $p\in X$, a \emph{germ of a holomorphic function at $p$} is an equivalence class of pairs $(U,f)$, where $U$ is an open neighborhood of $p$ and $f\in\mathcal O(U)$. Two pairs represent the same germ if their functions agree on some possibly smaller neighborhood of $p$. The collection $\mathcal O_{X,p}$ of such germs forms a local ring, whose maximal ideal consists of germs vanishing at $p$.
