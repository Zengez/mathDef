% term: Differential form of type (p,q)

% status: draft

For $0\le p,q\le n$, on a complex $n$-manifold $X$, a smooth \emph{$(p,q)$-form} is a smooth section of $\bigwedge^p(T^{*1,0}X)\otimes\bigwedge^q(T^{*0,1}X)$. In holomorphic coordinates it is a sum of $f_{I,J}\,dz_I\wedge d\bar z_J$.
