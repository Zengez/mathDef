A \emph{meromorphic function} on a complex manifold $X$ is locally representable as a quotient $g/h$ of holomorphic functions with $h$ not identically zero on a connected chart, with the local quotients agreeing wherever jointly defined. Unlike in one variable, a meromorphic function in several variables can have points of indeterminacy.
