% term: Meromorphic function on a complex manifold

% status: draft

A \emph{meromorphic function on a complex manifold $X$} is a global section of the sheaf of fields of fractions of the local rings $\mathcal O_{X,p}$: locally it is expressible as $g/h$ with holomorphic $g,h$ and $h$ not identically zero on any connected component of the neighborhood, and these fractions agree on overlaps as meromorphic germs. In complex dimension greater than one, meromorphic functions need not define everywhere-defined maps to the Riemann sphere, because points of indeterminacy may occur.
