% id: scv.holomorphic-separability
% term: Holomorphic separability
% related: scv.complex-manifold, scv.stein-manifold
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Stein_manifold
% status: draft

A complex manifold $X$ is \emph{holomorphically separable} if for every pair $x\ne y$ there exists $f\in\mathcal O(X)$ with $f(x)\ne f(y)$.
