% term: Complex tangent space to a real hypersurface
% related: scv.defining-function, scv.levi-form
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/CR_manifold#Embedded_CR_manifolds
% status: draft

Let $\rho$ be a $C^1$ local defining function for a real hypersurface at $p\in\mathbb C^n$. Its \emph{complex tangent hyperplane} (the $(1,0)$ tangent vectors) is
\[
T_p^{1,0}(\partial\Omega)=\{v\in\mathbb C^n:\sum_{j=1}^n\rho_{z_j}(p)v_j=0\}.
\]
