A domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is a \emph{domain of holomorphy} if some $f\in\mathcal O(\Omega)$ admits no holomorphic extension to any strictly larger domain containing $\Omega$. For domains in $\mathbb C^n$, this is equivalent to pseudoconvexity (the solution of the Levi problem); such equivalences need not extend verbatim to arbitrary infinite-dimensional complex spaces.
