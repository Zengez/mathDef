% term: Germ of a complex analytic set

% status: draft

At a point $p$ of a complex manifold $X$, an \emph{analytic-set germ} is an equivalence class of complex analytic subsets defined on neighborhoods of $p$, where two representatives are equivalent if their intersections with some smaller neighborhood of $p$ coincide.
