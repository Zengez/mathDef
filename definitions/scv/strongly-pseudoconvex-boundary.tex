% term: Strongly pseudoconvex boundary

% status: draft

A $C^2$ domain is \emph{strongly pseudoconvex} at $p\in\partial\Omega$ if a local defining function $\rho$ has strictly positive Levi form on the nonzero complex tangent vectors at $p$.
