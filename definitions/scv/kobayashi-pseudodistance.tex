For a complex manifold $X$, the \emph{Kobayashi pseudodistance} $d_X$ is the largest pseudodistance on $X$ such that every holomorphic map $f:\mathbb D\to X$ is distance nonincreasing with respect to the Poincaré distance on $\mathbb D$. It may vanish between distinct points.
