A \emph{Kähler manifold} is a complex manifold $(X,J)$ with a Riemannian metric $g$ satisfying $g(Ju,Jv)=g(u,v)$ and such that its fundamental real $2$-form $\omega(u,v)=g(Ju,v)$ is closed: $d\omega=0$.
