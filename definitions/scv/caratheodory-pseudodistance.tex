For a connected complex manifold $X$, its \emph{Carathéodory pseudodistance} is
\[
c_X(x,y)=\sup\{\rho_{\mathbb D}(f(x),f(y)):f\in\mathcal O(X,\mathbb D)\},
\]
where $\rho_{\mathbb D}$ is the chosen Poincaré distance on the unit disk. It is biholomorphically invariant and satisfies the triangle inequality, but may vanish at distinct points when disc-valued holomorphic functions fail to separate them. The factor in $\rho_{\mathbb D}$ depends on a curvature convention.
