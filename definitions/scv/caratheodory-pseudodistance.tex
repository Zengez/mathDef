For a complex manifold $X$, the \emph{Carathéodory pseudodistance} is $c_X(x,y)=\sup_{f\in\mathcal O(X,\mathbb D)}\rho_{\mathbb D}(f(x),f(y))$, where $\rho_{\mathbb D}$ is a fixed Poincaré distance on the unit disc. It is a pseudodistance and may vanish for distinct $x,y$.
