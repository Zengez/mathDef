A real-valued $C^2$ function $u$ on $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{strictly plurisubharmonic} if its Levi form is positive definite at every point: $\mathcal L_u(z;v)>0$ for every nonzero $v\in\mathbb C^n$ at every $z\in\Omega$. This is the $C^2$ Levi-form convention.
