Near $0\in\mathbb C^{n-1}$, a \emph{Weierstrass polynomial} of degree $m\ge1$ in $z_n$ has the form $P(z',z_n)=z_n^m+a_1(z')z_n^{m-1}+\cdots+a_m(z')$, where each $a_j$ is holomorphic near $0$ and $a_j(0)=0$. The distinguished variable is $z_n$.
