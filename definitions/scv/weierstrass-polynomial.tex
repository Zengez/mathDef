% term: Weierstrass polynomial in a distinguished variable

Near the origin in $\mathbb C^{n-1}$, a \emph{Weierstrass polynomial of degree $m\ge1$ in the distinguished variable $z_n$} is
\[
P(z',z_n)=z_n^m+a_1(z')z_n^{m-1}+\cdots+a_m(z'),
\]
where $z'=(z_1,\ldots,z_{n-1})$ and each coefficient $a_j$ is holomorphic near $0$ with $a_j(0)=0$. Its coefficients are germs in the base variables; the monic and vanishing conditions are those appearing in the Weierstrass preparation theorem.
