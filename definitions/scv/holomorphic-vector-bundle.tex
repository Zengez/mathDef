A \emph{holomorphic vector bundle} of rank $r$ over a complex manifold $X$ is a complex vector bundle $E\to X$ with local holomorphic trivializations $E|_U\cong U\times\mathbb C^r$ whose transition functions are holomorphic maps to $\mathrm{GL}_r(\mathbb C)$.
