% status: draft

For domains $\Omega\subseteq D\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, $(\Omega,D)$ is a \emph{Runge pair} if the restrictions to $\Omega$ of functions from $\mathcal O(D)$ are dense in $\mathcal O(\Omega)$ for the topology of uniform convergence on compact subsets.
