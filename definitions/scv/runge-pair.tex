For domains $\Omega\subseteq D\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, $(\Omega,D)$ is a \emph{Runge pair} if restrictions of functions in $\mathcal O(D)$ are dense in $\mathcal O(\Omega)$ for uniform convergence on compact subsets. The special case $D=\mathbb C^n$ is precisely polynomial Runge approximation, since entire functions are compact-locally approximated by polynomials.
