% id: scv.kobayashi-hyperbolicity
% term: Kobayashi hyperbolic manifold
% related: scv.kobayashi-pseudodistance, scv.complex-manifold
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Kobayashi_hyperbolicity
% status: draft

A complex manifold $X$ is \emph{Kobayashi hyperbolic} if its Kobayashi pseudodistance separates points, i.e. $d_X(x,y)>0$ whenever $x\ne y$.
