% term: Holomorphic convex hull of a compact set
% related: scv.holomorphically-convex, scv.holomorphic-separability
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Analytic_function
% status: draft

For a complex manifold $X$ and compact $K\subset X$, the \emph{holomorphic hull relative to the global holomorphic functions $\mathcal O(X)$} is
\[
\widehat K_{\mathcal O(X)}=\{x\in X:|f(x)|\le\sup_{z\in K}|f(z)|\ \text{for every }f\in\mathcal O(X)\}.
\]
