% id: scv.levi-flat-hypersurface
% term: Levi-flat real hypersurface
% related: scv.levi-form, scv.complex-tangent-hyperplane
% source: https://planetmath.org/leviflat
% status: draft

A $C^2$ real hypersurface $M$ in a complex manifold is \emph{Levi-flat} if its Levi form vanishes identically on its complex tangent distribution $TM\cap J(TM)$. Equivalently, under the usual regularity hypotheses this real distribution is involutive and locally foliates $M$ by complex hypersurfaces, called its \emph{Levi foliation}. This is a vanishing condition, not merely semipositivity of the Levi form.
