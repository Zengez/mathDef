A domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{pseudoconvex} if it admits a continuous plurisubharmonic exhaustion function, that is, a continuous plurisubharmonic $\rho:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ whose closed sublevel sets $\{\rho\le c\}$ are compact in $\Omega$ for every real $c$. By the Levi problem this is equivalent, for domains in $\mathbb C^n$, to being a domain of holomorphy.
