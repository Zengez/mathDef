A domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ is \emph{pseudoconvex} if it admits a continuous plurisubharmonic exhaustion function, that is, a continuous plurisubharmonic $\rho:\Omega\to\mathbb R$ whose sublevel sets $\{\rho\le c\}$ are relatively compact in $\Omega$ for every real $c$.
