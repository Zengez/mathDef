For compact $K\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{polynomial hull} is
\[
\widehat K=\{z\in\mathbb C^n:|p(z)|\le\sup_{w\in K}|p(w)|\quad\forall p\in\mathbb C[z_1,\ldots,z_n]\}.
\]
