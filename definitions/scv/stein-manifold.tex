% id: scv.stein-manifold
% term: Stein manifold
% related: scv.holomorphically-convex, scv.holomorphic-separability, scv.complex-manifold
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stein_manifold
% status: draft

A complex manifold $X$ is \emph{Stein} if it is holomorphically convex and holomorphically separable: compact subsets have compact holomorphic hulls, and global holomorphic functions separate any two distinct points.
