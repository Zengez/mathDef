A complex manifold $X$ is \emph{Stein} if it is holomorphically convex, holomorphically separable, and has local holomorphic coordinate systems given by restrictions of global holomorphic functions near every point. Concretely, holomorphic hulls of compact sets are compact and global holomorphic functions separate distinct points. Equivalent formulations use proper holomorphic embeddings into affine space.
