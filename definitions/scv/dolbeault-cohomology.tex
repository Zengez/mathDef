For a complex manifold $X$ and integers $p,q\ge0$, the \emph{Dolbeault cohomology} is
\[
H_{\bar\partial}^{p,q}(X)=\frac{\ker(\bar\partial:\mathcal A^{p,q}(X)\to\mathcal A^{p,q+1}(X))}{\operatorname{im}(\bar\partial:\mathcal A^{p,q-1}(X)\to\mathcal A^{p,q}(X))},
\]
where $\mathcal A^{p,q}(X)$ denotes smooth forms of type $(p,q)$ and $\mathcal A^{p,-1}=0$.
