A \emph{complex torus} of complex dimension $n$ is a quotient $\mathbb C^n/\Lambda$, where $\Lambda$ is a discrete additive subgroup of full real rank $2n$. Its inherited addition gives a compact complex Lie group, without any assumption that the torus is algebraic.
