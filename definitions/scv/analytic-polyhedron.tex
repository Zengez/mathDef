An \emph{analytic polyhedron} in $\mathbb C^n$ is a relatively compact connected open set $P\Subset D$, for a domain $D\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, of the form
\[
P=\{z\in D:|f_j(z)|<1,\quad j=1,\ldots,m\},
\]
where $f_j\in\mathcal O(D)$. Requiring $P\Subset D$ distinguishes this compactly contained convention from more general sets cut out by holomorphic inequalities.
