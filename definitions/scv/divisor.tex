On a complex manifold $X$, a \emph{divisor} is a locally finite formal integer sum $D=\sum_j n_jV_j$ of distinct irreducible analytic hypersurfaces $V_j$ (complex codimension one). It is \emph{effective} when every coefficient $n_j$ is nonnegative. On a complex manifold, Weil and Cartier divisors correspond.
