A \emph{Weil divisor} on a complex manifold $X$ is a locally finite formal integer sum
\[
D=\sum_j n_jV_j,
\]
where each $V_j$ is an irreducible analytic hypersurface (complex codimension one) and $n_j\in\mathbb Z$. It is \emph{effective} if every $n_j\ge0$. Because a complex manifold has regular, hence locally factorial, analytic local rings, Weil divisors correspond to Cartier divisors on $X$; this equivalence can fail on singular complex spaces.
