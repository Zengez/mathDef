A complex manifold $X$ is \emph{Carathéodory hyperbolic} if its Carathéodory pseudodistance $c_X$ is a genuine distance, i.e. $c_X(x,y)>0$ whenever $x\ne y$. Equivalently, bounded disc-valued holomorphic functions separate points of $X$.
