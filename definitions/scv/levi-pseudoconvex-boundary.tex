A $C^2$ domain $\Omega=\{\rho<0\}$ is \emph{Levi pseudoconvex} at a boundary point $p$ if $\mathcal L_\rho(p;v)\ge0$ for every complex tangent vector $v\in T_p^{1,0}(\partial\Omega)$.
