% term: Uniformly elliptic second-order operator
% related: pde.poisson-equation, pde.laplacian

% status: draft

A second-order operator $Lu=-\sum_{i,j}a^{ij}(x)\partial_{ij}u+\text{lower order terms}$ is \emph{uniformly elliptic} (in the real, nondivergence-form convention) if the symmetric part of its real coefficient matrix $(a^{ij}(x))$ satisfies $\sum_{i,j}a^{ij}(x)\xi_i\xi_j\ge\lambda|\xi|^2$ for some $\lambda>0$ uniformly in $x$.
