% term: Principal symbol

% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Symbol_of_a_differential_operator
% status: draft

For a differential operator $P=\sum_{|\alpha|\le m}a_\alpha(x)D^\alpha$, its \emph{principal symbol} (in the convention $D_j=\partial_{x_j}$) is $p_m(x,\xi)=\sum_{|\alpha|=m}a_\alpha(x)\xi^\alpha$ (up to powers of $i$ by convention).
