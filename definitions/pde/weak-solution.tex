% term: Weak solution of Poisson's equation
% related: pde.poisson-equation, pde.h10-space
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weak_solution
% status: draft

For an open domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $f\in L^2(\Omega)$, a \emph{weak homogeneous-Dirichlet solution} of $-\Delta u=f$ is $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ with $\int_\Omega\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,dx=\int_\Omega fv\,dx$ for every $v\in H_0^1(\Omega)$. On unbounded domains this $L^2$ pairing need not yield a continuous functional on $H_0^1$ unless a suitable Poincaré-type or other hypothesis holds; the displayed equality is the definition wherever both sides are meaningful.
