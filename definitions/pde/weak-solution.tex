For $f\in L^2(\Omega)$, a \emph{weak homogeneous-Dirichlet solution} of $-\Delta u=f$ is $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ with $\int_\Omega\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,dx=\int_\Omega fv\,dx$ for every $v\in H_0^1(\Omega)$.
