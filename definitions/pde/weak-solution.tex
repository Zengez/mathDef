% term: Weak solution of Poisson's equation
% related: pde.poisson-equation, pde.h10-space
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weak_solution
% status: draft

For an open domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $f\in L^2(\Omega)$, a \emph{weak homogeneous-Dirichlet solution} of $-\Delta u=f$ is $u\in H_0^1(\Omega)$ with $\int_\Omega\nabla u\cdot\nabla v\,dx=\int_\Omega fv\,dx$ for every $v\in H_0^1(\Omega)$. The right-hand side is a continuous functional on $H_0^1(\Omega)$ with its full $H^1$ norm, by the Cauchy--Schwarz inequality. Existence and uniqueness of a solution require separate hypotheses.
