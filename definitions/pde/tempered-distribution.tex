A \emph{tempered distribution} on $\mathbb R^n$ is a continuous linear functional $T:\mathcal S(\mathbb R^n)\to\mathbb C$, where the Schwartz topology is generated by the seminorms $\sup_x|x^\alpha D^\beta\varphi(x)|$ for all multi-indices $\alpha,\beta$.
