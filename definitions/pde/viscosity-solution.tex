% term: Viscosity solution of a degenerate elliptic PDE
% related: pde.weak-solution, pde.elliptic-equation

% status: draft

Suppose $F(x,r,p,X)$ is continuous and nonincreasing in the symmetric-matrix variable $X$. A continuous function $u$ is a \emph{viscosity subsolution} of $F(x,u,Du,D^2u)=0$ if $F(x,u(x),D\phi(x),D^2\phi(x))\le0$ whenever $\phi\in C^2$ touches $u$ from above at $x$. A \emph{supersolution} has the reversed inequality for test functions touching from below; a \emph{viscosity solution} is both.
