A \emph{distribution} on open $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is a continuous linear functional $T:C_c^\infty(\Omega)\to\mathbb C$ for the usual test-function topology: $\varphi_j\to0$ when all their supports lie in one compact subset and all derivatives converge uniformly to zero there. This is the standard distribution-space convention.
