For open $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, $k\in\mathbb N_0$ and $1\le p\le\infty$, the \emph{Sobolev space} $W^{k,p}(\Omega)$ consists of $L^p$ functions whose weak derivatives $D^\alpha u$ (defined distributionally) lie in $L^p(\Omega)$ for every multi-index $\alpha$ with $|\alpha|\le k$. Here functions agreeing almost everywhere are identified.
