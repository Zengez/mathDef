For open $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, $k\in\mathbb N_0$ and $1\le p\le\infty$, the \emph{Sobolev space} $W^{k,p}(\Omega)$ consists of $L^p$ functions whose weak derivatives $D^\alpha u$ lie in $L^p$ for every $|\alpha|\le k$.
