For diffusivity $1$, the \emph{Euclidean heat kernel} on $\mathbb R^n$ is
\[
K_t(x)=(4\pi t)^{-n/2}\exp\!\left(-\frac{|x|^2}{4t}\right),\qquad t>0.
\]
It has total integral $1$, satisfies $(\partial_t-\Delta)K_t=0$, and tends to the Dirac distribution $\delta_0$ as $t\downarrow0$. For suitable initial data $u_0$, convolution $K_t*u_0$ solves the heat equation with initial value $u_0$.
