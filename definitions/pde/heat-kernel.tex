For $t>0$ and $x\in\mathbb R^n$, the \emph{Euclidean heat kernel} for diffusivity $1$ is $K_t(x)=(4\pi t)^{-n/2}\exp(-|x|^2/(4t))$. It satisfies $(\partial_t-\Delta)K_t=0$ for $t>0$ and converges to $\delta_0$ in distributions as $t\downarrow0$.
