For a measurable essentially bounded symbol $m:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb C$, the \emph{$L^2$ Fourier multiplier} is the bounded operator
\[
T_mf=\mathcal F^{-1}(m\,\widehat f)
\]
on $L^2(\mathbb R^n)$. By Plancherel's theorem its operator norm is $\|T_m\|_{2\to2}=\|m\|_{L^\infty}$. This equality holds for the fixed consistent Fourier-transform normalization; multiplier boundedness on $L^p$ for $p\ne2$ is a separate question.
