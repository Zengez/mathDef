For an essentially bounded measurable function $m:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb C$, the associated \emph{Fourier multiplier on $L^2(\mathbb R^n)$} is $T_mf=\mathcal F^{-1}(m\widehat f)$, initially on Schwartz functions and extended by Plancherel's theorem. Its $L^2$ operator norm is $\|m\|_{L^\infty}$. Other multiplier classes specify different domain and target spaces.
