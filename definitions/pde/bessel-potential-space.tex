For $s\in\mathbb R$, the \emph{Bessel-potential Sobolev space} $H^s(\mathbb R^n)$ consists of tempered distributions $u$ whose Fourier transforms are representable by functions for which $\int_{\mathbb R^n}(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi<\infty$, using a fixed Fourier normalization.
