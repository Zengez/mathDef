% term: Bessel potential Sobolev space Hs
% related: pde.tempered-distribution, pde.fourier-transform, pde.fourier-multiplier

For $s\in\mathbb R$, the \emph{Bessel-potential Sobolev space} $H^s(\mathbb R^n)$ consists of tempered distributions $u$ whose Fourier transforms are represented by functions satisfying
\[
\int_{\mathbb R^n}(1+|\xi|^2)^s|\widehat u(\xi)|^2\,d\xi<\infty.
\]
Using a unitary Fourier transform, the square root of this integral is its standard Hilbert norm. With a nonunitary Fourier convention a constant normalization factor must be inserted.
