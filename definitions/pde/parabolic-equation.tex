A standard \emph{linear parabolic equation} has the form $\partial_tu-\sum_{i,j}a^{ij}(t,x)\partial_{ij}u+\text{lower-order terms}=f$, with a positive-definite symmetric part of the principal spatial coefficient matrix. The adjective \emph{uniformly parabolic} additionally requires an ellipticity constant bounded below uniformly over the space-time region.
