% term: Elliptic classical scalar symbol
% related: pde.classical-symbol, pde.parametrix
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Microlocal_analysis#Pseudodifferential_operators
% status: draft

For a scalar classical symbol $a$ of order $m$ on $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, write $a_m$ for its leading positively homogeneous symbol. The symbol is \emph{elliptic} if $a_m(x,\xi)\ne0$ for every $x\in\Omega$ and $\xi\ne0$. Equivalently, for every compact $K\Subset\Omega$ there exists $c_K>0$ such that $|a_m(x,\xi)|\ge c_K|\xi|^m$ for $x\in K$ and $\xi\ne0$.
