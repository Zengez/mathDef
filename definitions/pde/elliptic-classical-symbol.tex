% term: Elliptic classical scalar symbol
% related: pde.classical-symbol, pde.parametrix
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Pseudo-differential_operator
% status: draft

A scalar classical symbol $a$ of order $m$ on an open set $\Omega$ is \emph{elliptic} if its leading homogeneous term $a_m(x,\xi)$ is nonzero for every $x\in\Omega$ and $\xi\ne0$. Equivalently, locally uniformly in $x$ on compact subsets, $|a_m(x,\xi)|\ge c_K|\xi|^m$ for $\xi\ne0$.
