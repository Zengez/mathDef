The \emph{Schwartz space} $\mathcal S(\mathbb R^n)$ consists of smooth $\varphi$ such that $\sup_x|x^\alpha D^\beta\varphi(x)|<\infty$ for every pair of multi-indices $\alpha,\beta$.
