% status: draft

For a distribution $u\in\mathcal D'(\Omega)$, its \emph{singular support} $\operatorname{sing\,supp}u$ is the complement of the largest open subset of $\Omega$ on which $u$ agrees with a $C^\infty$ function as a distribution.
