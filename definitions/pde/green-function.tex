Given a linear differential operator $L$ on a domain $\Omega$ with specified homogeneous boundary conditions, a \emph{Green function} is a kernel $G(x,y)$ that, for each fixed $y\in\Omega$, satisfies $L_xG(x,y)=\delta_y$ distributionally in $x$ and satisfies those boundary conditions in the relevant trace sense. Existence and uniqueness depend on the operator and boundary-value problem.
