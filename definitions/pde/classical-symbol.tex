A symbol $a\in S^m_{1,0}$ is \emph{classical of order $m$} if it has an asymptotic expansion $a(x,\xi)\sim\sum_{j\ge0}a_{m-j}(x,\xi)$ as $|\xi|\to\infty$, where each $a_{m-j}$ is positively homogeneous of degree $m-j$ in $\xi$ for large $|\xi|$, and subtracting the first $N$ terms leaves a symbol of order $m-N$.
