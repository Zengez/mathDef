A symbol $a\in S^m_{1,0}(\Omega\times\mathbb R^n)$ is \emph{classical} or \emph{polyhomogeneous of order $m$} if, as $|\xi|\to\infty$,
\[
a(x,\xi)\sim\sum_{j=0}^\infty a_{m-j}(x,\xi),
\]
where each $a_{m-j}$ is smooth and positively homogeneous of degree $m-j$ in $\xi$ for sufficiently large $|\xi|$, and subtracting the first $N$ homogeneous terms (with a cutoff near $\xi=0$) leaves an element of $S^{m-N}_{1,0}$.
