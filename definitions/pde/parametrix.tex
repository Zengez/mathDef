% term: Parametrix modulo smoothing operators
% related: pde.pseudodifferential-operator, pde.smoothing-operator, pde.elliptic-classical-symbol
% source: https://www.mat.univie.ac.at/~stefan/files/ASIT/ASIT.pdf#page=119
% status: draft

For a pseudodifferential operator $P$, a \emph{two-sided parametrix modulo smoothing operators} is another operator $Q$ for which
\[
QP=I+R_1,\qquad PQ=I+R_2,
\]
where $R_1,R_2$ are smoothing (order $-\infty$) operators. The choice of plus or minus signs is immaterial because the remainders form a vector space. A parametrix is a symbolic inverse up to regularizing errors, not necessarily an actual inverse.
