For a pseudodifferential operator $P$, a \emph{two-sided parametrix modulo smoothing operators} is an operator $Q$ such that $QP=I+R_1$ and $PQ=I+R_2$, where $R_1,R_2$ are smoothing operators. It is an inverse only modulo regularizing errors.
