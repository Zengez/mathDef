For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ with sufficiently regular boundary, a \emph{Neumann boundary condition} prescribes the outward normal derivative of an unknown function $u$: $\partial_\nu u=\nabla u\cdot\nu=g$ on $\partial\Omega$, interpreted through boundary traces when necessary.
