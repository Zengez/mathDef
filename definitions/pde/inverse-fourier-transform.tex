% term: Inverse Fourier transform

% status: draft

For suitable $g$, the inverse transform in the convention $\widehat f(\xi)=\int f(x)e^{-ix\cdot\xi}\,dx$ is $f(x)=(2\pi)^{-n}\int_{\mathbb R^n}g(\xi)e^{ix\cdot\xi}\,d\xi$.
