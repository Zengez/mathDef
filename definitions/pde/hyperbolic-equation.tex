A real second-order scalar PDE is \emph{hyperbolic relative to a chosen time covector} when its real homogeneous principal symbol has real characteristic roots in the frequency associated with that covector; a stricter hyperbolicity convention also requires distinct roots. For example, $u_{tt}-c^2\Delta_x u=0$ is hyperbolic in the $t$ direction for $c>0$.
