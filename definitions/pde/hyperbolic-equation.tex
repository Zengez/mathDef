Let $p_2(x,\xi)$ be the real homogeneous principal symbol of a second-order scalar differential operator. It is \emph{hyperbolic at $x$ with respect to a time covector} $\tau\ne0$ if $p_2(x,\tau)\ne0$ and, for every real covector $\eta$, all roots in $\lambda$ of $p_2(x,\eta+\lambda\tau)=0$ are real. It is \emph{strictly hyperbolic} if these roots are distinct whenever $\eta$ is not proportional to $\tau$. For example, $u_{tt}-c^2\Delta_xu=0$ is hyperbolic in the $t$ direction for $c>0$.
