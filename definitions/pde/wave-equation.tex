For $c>0$, the \emph{homogeneous linear wave equation} for $u:I\times\Omega\to\mathbb R$ ($I\subseteq\mathbb R$ an interval, $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ open) is $\partial_t^2u-c^2\Delta_xu=0$. Boundary and initial conditions specify particular evolution problems.
