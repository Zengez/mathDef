For $\varepsilon>0$, a standard \emph{mollifier family} is $\rho_\varepsilon(x)=\varepsilon^{-n}\rho(x/\varepsilon)$ where $\rho\in C_c^\infty(\mathbb R^n)$, $\rho\ge0$, and $\int\rho=1$.
