For a specified class of admissible data $D$ and a topologized solution space $S$, a mathematical problem is \emph{well posed in the sense of Hadamard} if (i) every data item has a solution in $S$, (ii) that solution is unique in $S$, and (iii) the solution map $D\to S$ is continuous. Each clause depends on the chosen spaces and topologies.
