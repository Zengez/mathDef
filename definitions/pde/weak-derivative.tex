% status: draft

For $u,v\in L^1_{\rm loc}(\Omega)$, $v$ is a \emph{weak derivative} $D^\alpha u$ if $\int_\Omega uD^\alpha\varphi\,dx=(-1)^{|\alpha|}\int_\Omega v\varphi\,dx$ for every $\varphi\in C_c^\infty(\Omega)$.
