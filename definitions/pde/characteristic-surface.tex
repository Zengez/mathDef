% term: Characteristic hypersurface

% status: draft

Let $P$ be a scalar differential operator of order $m$ with principal symbol $p_m(x,\xi)$. A smooth hypersurface locally given by $\phi(x)=0$, where $d\phi\ne0$ on the hypersurface, is \emph{characteristic at $x$} if $p_m(x,d\phi_x)=0$. The nonvanishing differential makes $\phi$ a valid local defining function.
