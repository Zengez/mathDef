A time-homogeneous Markov process on a locally compact state space $S$ is a \emph{Feller process} if its transition operators $P_tf(x)=\mathbb E_x[f(X_t)]$ map $C_0(S)$ to itself and form a strongly continuous semigroup: $\|P_tf-f\|_\infty\to0$ as $t\downarrow0$ for all $f\in C_0(S)$.
