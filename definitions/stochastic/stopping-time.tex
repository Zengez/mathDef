% id: stochastic.stopping-time
% term: Stopping time
% related: stochastic.filtration, stochastic.stopped-process
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stopping_time
% status: draft

On a filtered probability space with filtration $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in T}$, where $T=\mathbb N_0$ or $T=[0,\infty)$, a random time $\tau:\Omega\to T\cup\{\infty\}$ is a \emph{stopping time} if
\[
\{\tau\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t\qquad\text{for every }t\in T.
\]
Thus, whether stopping has occurred by time $t$ is determined by the information available at time $t$. The same condition covers discrete and continuous time, and $\tau=\infty$ is permitted.
