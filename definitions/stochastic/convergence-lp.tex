% id: stochastic.convergence-lp
% term: Convergence in Lp
% related: measure.lp-space, stochastic.convergence-probability
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergence_of_random_variables
% status: draft

For random variables $X_n,X$ on a common probability space and $1\le p<\infty$, $X_n$ \emph{converges in $L^p$} to $X$ if $E[|X_n-X|^p]\to0$; equivalently, the $L^p$-norm of $X_n-X$ tends to zero.
