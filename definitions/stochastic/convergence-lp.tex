% term: Convergence in Lp
% related: measure.lp-space, stochastic.convergence-probability
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Convergence_of_random_variables
% status: draft

For random variables $X_n,X\in L^p(P)$ on a common probability space and $1\le p<\infty$, one says that $X_n$ \emph{converges in $L^p$} (or in $p$-th mean) to $X$ if
\[
E[|X_n-X|^p]\longrightarrow0.
\]
Equivalently, $\|X_n-X\|_{L^p(P)}\to0$. The $L^p$ membership assumption ensures the moments and norm are finite; this differs from convergence in probability, which requires no such moment bounds.
