% term: Ergodic invariant probability measure

For a measurable transformation $T$ of $(X,\mathcal F)$, a $T$-invariant probability measure $\mu$ is \emph{ergodic} if every measurable $A$ satisfying $\mu(A\mathbin\triangle T^{-1}A)=0$ has $\mu(A)\in\{0,1\}$. Here $\triangle$ denotes symmetric difference.
