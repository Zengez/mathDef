For a continuous semimartingale $X$, its \emph{quadratic variation} $[X]_t$ is the limit in probability of sums $\sum_i(X_{t_{i+1}}-X_{t_i})^2$ over partitions of $[0,t]$ whose mesh tends to zero. For continuous semimartingales this limit is independent of the choice of deterministic partitions (in probability).
