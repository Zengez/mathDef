% term: Renewal process
% related: prob.iid, stochastic.poisson-process
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Renewal_theory
% status: draft

For independent identically distributed strictly positive waiting times $(W_n)_{n\ge1}$, let $S_0=0$ and $S_n=\sum_{k=1}^n W_k$. The \emph{renewal counting process} is $N(t)=\max\{n\ge0:S_n\le t\}$ for $t\ge0$. By positivity and independence, $S_n\to\infty$ almost surely, so $N(t)$ is finite almost surely.
