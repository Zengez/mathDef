% term: Strictly stationary process

% status: draft

A process $(X_t)$ is \emph{strictly stationary} if for all finite time lists and shifts $h$ the joint distributions of $(X_{t_1+h},\ldots,X_{t_n+h})$ and $(X_{t_1},\ldots,X_{t_n})$ agree.
