A \emph{Markov kernel} $K:X\times\mathcal G\to[0,1]$ has $K(x,\cdot)$ a probability measure on $(Y,\mathcal G)$ for every $x$ and $K(\cdot,B)$ measurable on $X$ for every $B\in\mathcal G$.
