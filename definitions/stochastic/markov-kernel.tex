For measurable spaces $(X,\mathcal F)$ and $(Y,\mathcal G)$, a \emph{Markov kernel} $K:X\times\mathcal G\to[0,1]$ has $K(x,\cdot)$ a probability measure on $(Y,\mathcal G)$ for every $x\in X$ and $K(\cdot,B)$ an $\mathcal F$-measurable function for every $B\in\mathcal G$.
