% term: Weak convergence of probability measures
% related: stochastic.convergence-distribution, stochastic.tightness
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weak_convergence_of_probability_measures
% status: draft

Probability measures $\mu_n$ on a metric space $S$ \emph{converge weakly} to $\mu$ if $\int f\,d\mu_n\to\int f\,d\mu$ for every bounded continuous real function $f:S\to\mathbb R$.
