% id: stochastic.martingale-transform
% term: Discrete martingale transform
% related: stochastic.predictable-process, stochastic.martingale
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Martingale
% status: draft

For martingale $M$ and predictable $H$, their \emph{martingale transform}, when defined, is $(H\cdot M)_n=\sum_{k=1}^nH_k(M_k-M_{k-1})$.
