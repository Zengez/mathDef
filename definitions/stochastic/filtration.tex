A \emph{filtration} on $(\Omega,\mathcal F)$ is an increasing family $(\mathcal F_t)_{t\in T}$ of sub-$\sigma$-algebras, with $\mathcal F_s\subseteq\mathcal F_t$ when $s\le t$.
