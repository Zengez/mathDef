A real-valued \emph{Lévy process} $(X_t)_{t\ge0}$ has $X_0=0$ almost surely, independent stationary increments, and stochastic continuity ($X_t\to X_s$ in probability as $t\to s$). It is ordinarily represented by a càdlàg modification.
