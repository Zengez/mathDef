% id: stochastic.tightness
% term: Tight family of probability measures
% related: stochastic.weak-convergence-measures, topology.compactness
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Weak_convergence_of_probability_measures
% status: draft

A family $\mathcal P$ of Borel probability measures on a metric space $S$ is \emph{uniformly tight} if for every $\varepsilon>0$ some compact $K\subseteq S$ satisfies $\mu(K)\ge1-\varepsilon$ for all $\mu\in\mathcal P$.
