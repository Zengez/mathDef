% term: Stopped process
% related: stochastic.stopping-time, stochastic.martingale

% status: draft

Let $(X_t)_{t\in T}$ be a stochastic process, where $T=\mathbb N_0$ or $T=[0,\infty)$, and let $\tau$ be a stopping time for the same time index and filtration. The \emph{stopped process} is
\[
X_t^\tau(\omega)=X_{t\wedge\tau(\omega)}(\omega)\qquad(t\in T).
\]
After a finite stopping time the sample path remains at its value at the stopping time; on paths where $\tau=\infty$, the stopped process agrees with $X$ at every finite time. This construction applies in both discrete and continuous time.
