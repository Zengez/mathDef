A real-valued adapted càdlàg process $X$ is a \emph{semimartingale} if it admits a decomposition $X=M+A$, where $M$ is a local martingale and $A$ is an adapted càdlàg process whose sample paths have finite variation on every compact time interval. This is a continuous-time definition; no independent-increment or Markov property is required.
