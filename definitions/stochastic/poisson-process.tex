A \emph{Poisson process} of rate $\lambda>0$ is a counting process $(N_t)_{t\ge0}$ with $N_0=0$, independent stationary increments, and $N_t-N_s\sim\operatorname{Poisson}(\lambda(t-s))$ for $0\le s<t$.
