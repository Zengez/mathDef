For a Fréchet-differentiable map $f:U\subseteq X\to Y$ of finite-dimensional normed spaces, with $x\in U$, $x\ne0$, and $f(x)\ne0$, the local normwise \emph{relative condition number} is $\kappa_f(x)=\|Df(x)\|_{X\to Y}\,\|x\|_X/\|f(x)\|_Y$. It describes first-order sensitivity and depends on the chosen norms.
