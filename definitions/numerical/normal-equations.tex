% term: Normal equations
% related: numerical.least-squares, linear.transpose
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Linear_least_squares
% status: draft

For $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ and $b\in\mathbb R^m$, the \emph{normal equations} for least squares are $A^{\mathsf T}Ax=A^{\mathsf T}b$. Every least-squares minimizer solves these equations, and conversely. In complex vector spaces use the conjugate transpose $A^*$ instead.
