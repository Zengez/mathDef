An \emph{LU factorization} of a square matrix over a field is $A=LU$, with $L$ lower triangular and $U$ upper triangular. Such a factorization without row permutations need not exist for every matrix; partial pivoting typically yields $PA=LU$ for some permutation matrix $P$.
