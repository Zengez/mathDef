A \emph{truncation error} is the discrepancy caused by replacing an exact or infinite mathematical operation with a truncated finite approximation, assuming exact arithmetic. In numerical ODE/PDE methods, \emph{local truncation error} is defined by inserting the exact solution into one discrete update and measuring the resulting defect; normalization by powers of the step size varies by convention.
