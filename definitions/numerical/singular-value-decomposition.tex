A \emph{singular value decomposition} of $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$ is $A=U\Sigma V^*$ with $U\in\mathbb C^{m\times m}$ and $V\in\mathbb C^{n\times n}$ unitary and $\Sigma\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ rectangular diagonal with nonnegative diagonal entries (usually arranged in nonincreasing order).
