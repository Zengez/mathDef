For any $A\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ with $m\ge n$, a \emph{thin QR factorization} is $A=QR$, where $Q\in\mathbb R^{m\times n}$ has orthonormal columns and $R\in\mathbb R^{n\times n}$ is upper triangular. Such a factorization also exists for rank-deficient $A$, although $R$ is then singular.
