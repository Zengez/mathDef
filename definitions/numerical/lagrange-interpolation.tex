% id: numerical.lagrange-interpolation
% term: Lagrange interpolating polynomial
% related: numerical.interpolation, numerical.divided-difference

% status: draft

For distinct nodes $x_0,\ldots,x_n$ in a field $F$ and prescribed scalars $y_0,\ldots,y_n\in F$, the unique polynomial interpolant of degree at most $n$ is $p(x)=\sum_{j=0}^ny_jL_j(x)$, where $L_j(x)=\prod_{k\ne j}(x-x_k)/(x_j-x_k)$.
