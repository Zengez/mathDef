A \emph{conforming Galerkin finite-element method} selects a finite-dimensional space $V_h\subseteq V$ of typically piecewise-polynomial functions associated with a mesh, and seeks $u_h\in V_h$ satisfying $a(u_h,v_h)=\ell(v_h)$ for every $v_h\in V_h$. Nonconforming, mixed, and Petrov--Galerkin finite-element variants employ different trial and test spaces.
