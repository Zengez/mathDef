For $A\in\mathbb C^{m\times n}$, its \emph{singular values} in the $n$-value convention are the nonnegative square roots of the eigenvalues of $A^*A$ (including zeros), counted with multiplicity. Some authors list only $\min(m,n)$ singular values, as in a rectangular diagonal SVD; the positive singular values coincide.
