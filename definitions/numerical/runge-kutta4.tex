For $y'=f(t,y)$ and step $h$, define $k_1=f(t,y)$, $k_2=f(t+h/2,y+hk_1/2)$, $k_3=f(t+h/2,y+hk_2/2)$, $k_4=f(t+h,y+hk_3)$; then $y_{\rm next}=y+h(k_1+2k_2+2k_3+k_4)/6$.
