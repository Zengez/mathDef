Given a partition $a=x_0<x_1<\cdots<x_m=b$, a \emph{cubic spline} is an $s\in C^2([a,b])$ whose restriction to each $[x_{j-1},x_j]$ is a polynomial of degree at most $3$. Boundary constraints such as natural or clamped conditions are extra data, not part of the general definition.
