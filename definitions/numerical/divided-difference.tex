% id: numerical.divided-difference
% term: Divided difference
% related: numerical.interpolation, numerical.lagrange-interpolation
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Numerical_analysis
% status: draft

For distinct nodes, divided differences are defined recursively by $f[x_i]=f(x_i)$ and $f[x_i,\ldots,x_j]=(f[x_{i+1},\ldots,x_j]-f[x_i,\ldots,x_{j-1}])/(x_j-x_i)$.
