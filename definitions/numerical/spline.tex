For a knot partition $a=x_0<\cdots<x_m=b$, a \emph{piecewise polynomial spline of degree at most $d$ and smoothness $C^k$} is $s\in C^k([a,b])$ such that each restriction $s|_{[x_{j-1},x_j]}$ is a polynomial of degree at most $d$. The smoothness $k$ and knot multiplicities are convention-dependent.
