An \emph{equivalence relation} $\sim$ on $X$ is a relation that is reflexive, symmetric, and transitive:
\[
x\sim x,\quad x\sim y\implies y\sim x,\quad
(x\sim y\land y\sim z)\implies x\sim z.
\]
