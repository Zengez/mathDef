An \emph{equivalence relation} $\sim$ on $X$ is a binary relation that is reflexive, symmetric and transitive:
\[
x\sim x,\qquad x\sim y\Rightarrow y\sim x,\qquad
(x\sim y\land y\sim z)\Rightarrow x\sim z.
\]
Its equivalence classes partition $X$.
