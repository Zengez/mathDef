A \emph{total order} (linear order) on $X$ is a partial order $\le$ in which every pair $x,y\in X$ is comparable: $x\le y$ or $y\le x$. It need not be a well-order, since a nonempty subset may fail to have a least element (as with the usual order on $\mathbb Z$).
