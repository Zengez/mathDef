For posets $(P,\le_P)$ and $(Q,\le_Q)$, an \emph{order embedding} is a map $f:P\to Q$ such that for all $x,y\in P$,
\[
x\le_P y\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad f(x)\le_Q f(y).
\]
Such a map preserves and reflects order and is necessarily injective by antisymmetry. An order-preserving map without reflection need not be an embedding.
