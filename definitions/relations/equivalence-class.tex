Given an equivalence relation $\sim$ on $X$ and an element $x\in X$, its \emph{equivalence class} is $[x]_\sim=\{y\in X:y\sim x\}$. The classes are nonempty and form a pairwise disjoint partition of $X$; two classes coincide exactly when their representatives are equivalent.
