A \emph{partial order} $\le$ on a set $P$ is a binary relation that is reflexive ($x\le x$), antisymmetric ($(x\le y\land y\le x)\Rightarrow x=y$), and transitive ($(x\le y\land y\le z)\Rightarrow x\le z$). The pair $(P,\le)$ is a \emph{partially ordered set}, or poset. Unlike a total order, not every pair must be comparable.
