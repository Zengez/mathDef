% term: Meet of a subset

% status: draft

For a subset $A$ of a poset $(P,\le)$, a \emph{meet} (infimum, or greatest lower bound) $\bigwedge A$, if it exists, is an element $m\in P$ satisfying $m\le a$ for every $a\in A$ and $l\le m$ for every other lower bound $l$ of $A$. It is unique when it exists; an arbitrary poset need not have meets of all subsets.
