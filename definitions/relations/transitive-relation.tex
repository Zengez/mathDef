A binary relation $R$ on $X$ is \emph{transitive} if for all $x,y,z\in X$, $(xRy\land yRz)\Rightarrow xRz$. The requirement concerns all composable pairs of relations; it does not imply reflexivity, symmetry or antisymmetry.
