A \emph{chain} in a partially ordered set $(P,\le)$ is a subset $C\subseteq P$ in which any two elements are comparable: for every $x,y\in C$, either $x\le y$ or $y\le x$. Thus the restriction of the partial order to $C$ is a total order. Chains may be empty unless a nonempty convention is imposed.
