A \emph{preorder} on a set $X$ is a binary relation $\preceq$ that is reflexive ($x\preceq x$) and transitive ($(x\preceq y\land y\preceq z)\Rightarrow x\preceq z$). Antisymmetry is not required. Identifying $x,y$ whenever $x\preceq y$ and $y\preceq x$ gives a natural partially ordered quotient.
