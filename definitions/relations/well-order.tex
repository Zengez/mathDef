A \emph{well-order} on a set $X$ is a total order $\le$ such that every nonempty subset $A\subseteq X$ has a least element. In particular, it forbids infinite strictly descending sequences when ordinary dependent choice is available. Every ordinal carries a canonical well-order given by membership.
