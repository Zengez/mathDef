A binary relation $R$ on a set $X$ is \emph{antisymmetric} if $(xRy\land yRx)\Rightarrow x=y$ for every $x,y\in X$. Antisymmetry does not mean that $R$ is the negation of a symmetric relation: a relation can be both symmetric and antisymmetric.
