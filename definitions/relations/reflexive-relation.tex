A binary relation $R\subseteq X\times X$ is \emph{reflexive} if every $x\in X$ satisfies $xRx$, equivalently $(x,x)\in R$ for all $x$. On the empty set the condition holds vacuously. Reflexivity alone does not imply symmetry or transitivity.
