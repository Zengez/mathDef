A \emph{lattice} is a partially ordered set $(L,\le)$ such that every pair $x,y\in L$ has a join $x\vee y$ (least upper bound) and meet $x\wedge y$ (greatest lower bound). A lattice need not have suprema or infima of arbitrary infinite subsets; that stronger condition defines a complete lattice.
