% source: https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.16503

% source_title: Quasi-metric spaces on which real-valued continuous functions are uniformly continuous
% status: draft

A quasi-metric space is \emph{left $K$-complete} if each left $K$-Cauchy sequence has a forward limit in that space.
