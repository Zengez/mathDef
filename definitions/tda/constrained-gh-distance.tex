% source_title: Gromov-Hausdorff distance between chromatic metric pairs and stability of the six-pack
% status: draft

For chromatic metric pairs $(A_1,\chi_1)$, $(A_2,\chi_2)$ and $\mathcal C\subseteq\mathcal P(\mathbb N)$, set
\[
d_{\rm GH}^{\mathcal C}=\frac12\inf_{f,g\ {\mathcal C}\text{-constrained}}\max\{\operatorname{dis}f,\operatorname{dis}g,\operatorname{codis}(f,g)\}.
\]
Here $f:A_1\to A_2$, $g:A_2\to A_1$, with $\operatorname{dis}f=\sup_{u,v\in A_1}|d_1(u,v)-d_2(fu,fv)|$ and $\operatorname{codis}(f,g)=\sup_{u\in A_1,v\in A_2}|d_1(u,gv)-d_2(fu,v)|$.
