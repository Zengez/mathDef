A map $f:A_1\to A_2$ between chromatic pairs is \emph{$\sigma$-constrained}, for $\sigma\subseteq\mathbb N$, when $f(\chi_1^{-1}(\sigma))\subseteq\chi_2^{-1}(\sigma)$.
