A \emph{projection} on a vector space $X$ is a linear endomorphism $P:X\to X$ satisfying
\[
P^2=P.
\]
It acts as the identity on $\operatorname{ran}P$, and $X=\operatorname{ran}P\oplus\ker P$. In an inner-product space, a projection need not be orthogonal unless additional adjoint conditions are imposed.
