The \emph{resolvent set} $\rho(T)$ of a bounded operator $T$ on a complex Banach space is $\mathbb C\setminus\sigma(T)$. For $\lambda\in\rho(T)$, $R(\lambda,T)=(\lambda I-T)^{-1}$.
