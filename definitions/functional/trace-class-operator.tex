A bounded operator $T:H\to H$ on a Hilbert space is \emph{trace class} if the positive operator $|T|=(T^*T)^{1/2}$ has finite trace:
\[
\|T\|_1=\operatorname{Tr}|T|<\infty.
\]
Equivalently, its singular values are summable, counting multiplicity. Trace-class operators are compact and Hilbert--Schmidt, and $\operatorname{Tr}(T)$ is well-defined and independent of the orthonormal basis.
