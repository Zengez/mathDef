% id: functional.spectral-measure
% term: Projection-valued spectral measure
% related: functional.hilbert-space, functional.orthogonal-projection
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Projection-valued_measure
% status: draft

On a measurable space $(S,\Sigma)$ and a Hilbert space $H$, a \emph{projection-valued measure} is a map $E:\Sigma\to\mathcal B(H)$ such that each $E(A)$ is an orthogonal projection, $E(S)=I$, and for pairwise disjoint $A_n$, $E(\bigcup_nA_n)x=\sum_nE(A_n)x$ for every $x\in H$, with convergence in Hilbert-space norm.
