% term: Bounded sequence in a normed space
% related: functional.normed-space, analysis.sequence
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bounded_set_(topological_vector_space)
% status: draft

A sequence $(x_n)$ in a normed space $X$ is \emph{bounded} if its range $\{x_n:n\in\mathbb N\}$ is norm-bounded; equivalently, there exists $M<\infty$ such that
\[
\|x_n\|\le M\qquad\text{for every }n,
\]
or, equivalently, $\sup_n\|x_n\|<\infty$.
