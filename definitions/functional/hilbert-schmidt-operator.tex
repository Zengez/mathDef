For Hilbert spaces $H,K$, a bounded linear map $T:H\to K$ is \emph{Hilbert--Schmidt} if
\[
\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2=\sum_{j\in J}\|Te_j\|_K^2<\infty
\]
for an orthonormal basis $(e_j)_{j\in J}$ of $H$. This value is independent of the orthonormal basis, even for nonseparable $H$, with the sum of nonnegative terms defined as the supremum of finite partial sums. Every Hilbert--Schmidt operator is compact.
