For Hilbert spaces $H,K$, a bounded operator $T:H\to K$ is \emph{Hilbert--Schmidt} if $\sum_{j\in J}\|Te_j\|_K^2<\infty$ for an orthonormal basis $(e_j)_{j\in J}$ of $H$. The sum is basis-independent and defines $\|T\|_{\mathrm{HS}}^2$.
