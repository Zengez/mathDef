A \emph{uniform algebra on $X$}, where $X$ is compact Hausdorff, is a uniformly closed complex subalgebra $A\subseteq C(X)$ that contains the constant functions and separates points of $X$. It is a unital commutative Banach algebra in the supremum norm $\|f\|_\infty=\max_X|f|$. A uniform algebra need not be closed under complex conjugation, and need not equal all of $C(X)$.
