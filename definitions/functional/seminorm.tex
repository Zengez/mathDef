On a real or complex vector space $V$, a \emph{seminorm} $p:V\to[0,\infty)$ satisfies $p(ax)=|a|p(x)$ and $p(x+y)\le p(x)+p(y)$; unlike a norm, $p(x)=0$ need not imply $x=0$.
