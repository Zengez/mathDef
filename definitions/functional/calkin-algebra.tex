For an infinite-dimensional complex Hilbert space $H$, the \emph{Calkin algebra} is the quotient
\[
\mathcal Q(H)=\mathcal B(H)/\mathcal K(H),
\]
where $\mathcal B(H)$ is the $C^*$-algebra of bounded operators on $H$ and $\mathcal K(H)$ is its norm-closed two-sided ideal of compact operators. It is itself a unital $C^*$-algebra, and the coset of an operator records its behavior modulo compact perturbations.
