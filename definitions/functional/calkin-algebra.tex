For an infinite-dimensional Hilbert space $H$, the \emph{Calkin algebra} is the quotient $\mathcal Q(H)=\mathcal B(H)/\mathcal K(H)$ of the bounded-operator $C^*$-algebra by its closed two-sided ideal of compact operators.
