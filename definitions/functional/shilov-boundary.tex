% term: Shilov boundary of a uniform algebra
% related: functional.uniform-algebra, functional.peak-point
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Boundary_(in_the_theory_of_uniform_algebras)
% status: draft

For a uniform algebra $A\subseteq C(X)$ on a compact Hausdorff space $X$, its \emph{Shilov boundary} $\partial_S A$ is the unique smallest closed set $F\subseteq X$ such that
\[
\max_{x\in X}|f(x)|=\max_{x\in F}|f(x)|\qquad(f\in A).
\]
Equivalently, it is contained in every closed $A$-boundary and itself is such a boundary. It is a function-algebra boundary, not the Bergman reproducing kernel or a metric boundary.
