% term: Shilov boundary of a uniform algebra
% related: functional.uniform-algebra, functional.peak-point
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Bergman-Shilov_boundary
% status: draft

For a uniform algebra $A\subseteq C(X)$, its \emph{Shilov boundary} $\partial_S A$ is the unique smallest closed subset $F\subseteq X$ such that $\max_{x\in X}|f(x)|=\max_{x\in F}|f(x)|$ for every $f\in A$. Equivalently, it is the intersection of all closed $A$-boundaries.
