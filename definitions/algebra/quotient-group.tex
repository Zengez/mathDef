If $N$ is a normal subgroup of a group $G$, the \emph{quotient group} $G/N$ has elements the cosets $gN$ and multiplication $(gN)(hN)=(gh)N$. Normality makes the operation well-defined; the identity is $N$ and the inverse of $gN$ is $g^{-1}N$.
