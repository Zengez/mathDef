% term: Kernel of a group homomorphism
% related: algebra.group-homomorphism, algebra.normal-subgroup
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kernel_(algebra)
% status: draft

For a group homomorphism $\varphi:G\to H$, its \emph{kernel} is $\ker\varphi=\{g\in G:\varphi(g)=e_H\}$. It is a normal subgroup of $G$, and $\varphi$ is injective if and only if $\ker\varphi=\{e_G\}$. This is the group-theoretic kernel, not the kernel of a linear map.
