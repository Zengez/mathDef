An ideal $P$ of a commutative unital ring $R$ is \emph{prime} if $P\ne R$ and, for all $a,b\in R$, $ab\in P$ implies $a\in P$ or $b\in P$. Equivalently, $R/P$ is an integral domain. For noncommutative rings, prime ideals are defined differently.
