For groups $(G,\cdot)$ and $(H,\ast)$, a \emph{group homomorphism} is a map $\varphi:G\to H$ satisfying $\varphi(ab)=\varphi(a)\ast\varphi(b)$ for all $a,b\in G$. It automatically preserves the identity and inverses: $\varphi(e_G)=e_H$ and $\varphi(a^{-1})=\varphi(a)^{-1}$.
