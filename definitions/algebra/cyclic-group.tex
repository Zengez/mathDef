A group $G$ is \emph{cyclic} if some element $g\in G$ generates it: $G=\langle g\rangle=\{g^n:n\in\mathbb Z\}$. The group may be finite or infinite, and its operation need not initially be written multiplicatively. Every cyclic group is abelian.
