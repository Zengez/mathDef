For a subgroup $H\le G$ of a group $G$ and $g\in G$, the \emph{left coset} is $gH=\{gh:h\in H\}$ and the \emph{right coset} is $Hg=\{hg:h\in H\}$. Left cosets partition $G$, as do right cosets. Their coincidence for every $g$ is equivalent to $H$ being normal.
