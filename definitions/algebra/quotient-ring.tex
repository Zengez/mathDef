For a two-sided ideal $I$ of a unital ring $R$, the \emph{quotient ring} $R/I$ consists of additive cosets $r+I$ with $(r+I)+(s+I)=(r+s)+I$ and $(r+I)(s+I)=rs+I$. The ideal property makes multiplication well-defined; its identity is $1_R+I$, possibly equal to zero when $I=R$.
