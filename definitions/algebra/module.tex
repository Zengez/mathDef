A \emph{unital left module} $M$ over an associative ring $R$ with identity is an abelian group $(M,+)$ equipped with an action $R\times M\to M$, $(r,m)\mapsto rm$, such that $r(m+n)=rm+rn$, $(r+s)m=rm+sm$, $(rs)m=r(sm)$, and $1_Rm=m$ for all $r,s\in R$ and $m,n\in M$. Right modules use an action $M\times R\to M$.
