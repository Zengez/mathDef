A \emph{unital ring homomorphism} $f:R\to S$ between rings with identity is a map such that $f(a+b)=f(a)+f(b)$, $f(ab)=f(a)f(b)$, and $f(1_R)=1_S$. These identities also imply $f(0_R)=0_S$ and $f(-a)=-f(a)$. Some authors omit the identity-preservation requirement.
