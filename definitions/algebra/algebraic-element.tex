Let $L/K$ be a field extension. An element $\alpha\in L$ is \emph{algebraic over $K$} if there exists a nonzero polynomial $p(t)\in K[t]$ with $p(\alpha)=0$. Otherwise $\alpha$ is transcendental over $K$. An algebraic element has a unique monic irreducible minimal polynomial over $K$.
