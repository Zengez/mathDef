A \emph{left action} of a group $G$ on a set $X$ is a function $G\times X\to X$, denoted $(g,x)\mapsto g\cdot x$, such that $e\cdot x=x$ and $(gh)\cdot x=g\cdot(h\cdot x)$ for all $g,h\in G$ and $x\in X$. Equivalently, it is a homomorphism $G\to\operatorname{Sym}(X)$, where $\operatorname{Sym}(X)$ is the permutation group of $X$.
