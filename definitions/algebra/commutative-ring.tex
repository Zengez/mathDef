A \emph{commutative ring} is a ring $R$ in which $ab=ba$ for every $a,b\in R$. As elsewhere in this glossary, a ring is assumed to have a multiplicative identity $1_R$, including the possible zero ring unless a nonzero condition is stated. Commutativity concerns multiplication; ring addition is already commutative.
