A \emph{two-sided ideal} $I$ of a ring $R$ is an additive subgroup of $(R,+)$ such that $r a\in I$ and $a r\in I$ for every $r\in R$ and $a\in I$. For a commutative ring the two conditions agree; in a noncommutative ring one-sided ideals are weaker notions.
