For a commutative unital ring $R$, the \emph{polynomial ring} $R[x]$ consists of formal finite sums $\sum_{k=0}^n a_kx^k$ with coefficients $a_k\in R$. Addition is coefficientwise and multiplication is given by convolution of coefficients; the symbol $x$ is an indeterminate, not a specified element of $R$.
