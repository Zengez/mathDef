In this glossary, a \emph{ring} $R$ is a set whose addition makes $(R,+)$ an abelian group and whose multiplication is associative, distributes over addition on both sides, and has a two-sided identity $1_R$. Multiplication need not be commutative, and the zero ring is allowed unless a nonzero ring is explicitly required.
