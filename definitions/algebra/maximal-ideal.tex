% term: Maximal ideal
% related: algebra.ideal, algebra.prime-ideal

% status: draft

An ideal $M$ of a ring $R$ is \emph{maximal} among proper two-sided ideals if $M\ne R$ and every two-sided ideal $J$ satisfying $M\subseteq J\subseteq R$ is either $M$ or $R$. In a commutative unital ring, this is equivalent to $R/M$ being a field. In noncommutative rings the corresponding quotient-field characterization does not hold.
