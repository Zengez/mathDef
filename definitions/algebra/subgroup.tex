A \emph{subgroup} $H$ of a group $G$ is a subset that forms a group under the operation inherited from $G$. Equivalently, $H\ne\varnothing$ and $ab^{-1}\in H$ whenever $a,b\in H$. In particular, $H$ contains the identity and is closed under multiplication and inverses.
