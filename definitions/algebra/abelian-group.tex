A group $(G,\cdot)$ is \emph{abelian} if its operation is commutative: $ab=ba$ for every $a,b\in G$. Additive notation $a+b$ is common for abelian groups, but the definition is independent of the notation. Every cyclic group is abelian; the converse need not hold.
