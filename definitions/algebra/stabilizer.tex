For an action of a group $G$ on a set $X$ and $x\in X$, the \emph{stabilizer subgroup} is $G_x=\{g\in G:g\cdot x=x\}$. It is a subgroup of $G$; the orbit of $x$ is naturally in bijection with the left coset space $G/G_x$.
