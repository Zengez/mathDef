An \emph{integral domain} is a nonzero commutative ring $R$ with multiplicative identity $1_R\ne0_R$ such that $ab=0_R$ implies $a=0_R$ or $b=0_R$. Equivalently, the product of two nonzero elements is nonzero. This uses the glossary's unital-ring convention.
