A \emph{field extension} $L/K$ is an inclusion of fields $K\subseteq L$ in which the arithmetic operations and multiplicative identity on $K$ agree with those inherited from $L$. More abstractly, an injective unital field homomorphism $K\hookrightarrow L$ identifies $K$ with a subfield of $L$. The extension degree is the dimension $[L:K]$ of $L$ as a vector space over $K$.
