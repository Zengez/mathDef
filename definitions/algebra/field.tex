A \emph{field} is a commutative ring $K$ with multiplicative identity $1_K\ne0_K$ in which every nonzero element has a multiplicative inverse. Thus $(K\setminus\{0\},\cdot)$ is an abelian group. The condition $1_K\ne0_K$ excludes the zero ring.
