For a topological space $X$, its \emph{Borel $\sigma$-algebra} $\mathcal B(X)$ is the smallest $\sigma$-algebra containing the open sets of $X$, equivalently the $\sigma$-algebra generated by all closed sets. Its members are called Borel sets. The construction depends on the topology and does not automatically include every subset of a Borel null set.
