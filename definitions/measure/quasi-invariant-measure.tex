% id: measure.quasi-invariant-measure
% term: Quasi-invariant measure under a group action
% related: measure.absolute-continuity-of-measures, stochastic.invariant-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Quasi-invariant_measure
% status: draft

Let a group $G$ act on a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ through bimeasurable bijections, and let $\mu$ be a positive measure. It is \emph{quasi-invariant} if for every $g\in G$ the pushforward measure $g_*\mu$ and $\mu$ are mutually absolutely continuous: they have exactly the same measurable null sets. Quasi-invariance is weaker than invariance $g_*\mu=\mu$.
