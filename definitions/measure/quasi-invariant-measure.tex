% term: Quasi-invariant measure under a group action

% status: draft

Suppose a group $G$ acts by measurable bijections with measurable inverses on $(X,\Sigma)$. A positive measure $\mu$ is \emph{quasi-invariant} if the pushforward $g_*\mu$ is mutually absolutely continuous with $\mu$ for every $g\in G$.
