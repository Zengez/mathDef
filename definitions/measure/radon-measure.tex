On a Hausdorff topological space $X$, a \emph{Radon measure} in the compact-finiteness convention is a positive measure $\mu$ on the Borel $\sigma$-algebra that is finite on compact sets, inner regular on open sets, and outer regular on Borel sets. This particular choice of axioms is important: other references require local finiteness or inner regularity on all Borel sets, and these distinctions can matter outside familiar locally compact or metrizable settings.
