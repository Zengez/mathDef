A \emph{probability measure} on a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ is a countably additive measure $P:\Sigma\to[0,1]$ with $P(X)=1$. In particular, $P(\varnothing)=0$. The triple $(X,\Sigma,P)$ is called a probability space; probability measures are finite measures of total mass one.
