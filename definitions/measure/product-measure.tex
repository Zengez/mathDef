For $\sigma$-finite measure spaces $(X_i,\Sigma_i,\mu_i)$, $i=1,2$, the \emph{product measure} $\mu_1\otimes\mu_2$ is the unique measure on the product $\sigma$-algebra for which $(\mu_1\otimes\mu_2)(A_1\times A_2)=\mu_1(A_1)\mu_2(A_2)$ for measurable rectangles, with $0\cdot\infty=0$.
