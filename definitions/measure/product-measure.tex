For $\sigma$-finite measure spaces $(X_i,\Sigma_i,\mu_i)$, $i=1,2$, the \emph{product measure} $\mu_1\otimes\mu_2$ is the unique measure on $\Sigma_1\otimes\Sigma_2$ satisfying
\[
(\mu_1\otimes\mu_2)(A_1\times A_2)=\mu_1(A_1)\mu_2(A_2)
\]
for measurable rectangles $A_i\in\Sigma_i$, with the standard convention $0\cdot\infty=0$. The product $\sigma$-algebra is generated by measurable rectangles; completion is a separate operation.
