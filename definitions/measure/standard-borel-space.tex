A measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ is a \emph{standard Borel space} if one can equip $X$ with a Polish topology (separable and completely metrizable) whose Borel $\sigma$-algebra is exactly $\Sigma$. This is a property of the measurable structure, not of a fixed topology. Every Borel subset of a Polish space, with its induced Borel $\sigma$-algebra, is standard Borel.
