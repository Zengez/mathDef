A positive measure $\mu$ on a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ is \emph{$\sigma$-finite} if there are measurable sets $E_1,E_2,\ldots\in\Sigma$ with $X=\bigcup_{n=1}^{\infty}E_n$ and $\mu(E_n)<\infty$ for each $n$. Every finite measure is $\sigma$-finite. The covering sets need not initially be disjoint, though one can replace them with a measurable disjoint cover.
