Let $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ be a measure space and $B$ a Banach space. A strongly measurable function $f:X\to B$ is \emph{Bochner integrable} if $\int_X\|f(x)\|_B\,d\mu(x)<\infty$. Equivalently, there are integrable $B$-valued simple functions converging to $f$ in $L^1(\mu;B)$; the Bochner integral $\int f\,d\mu$ is the norm limit of their simple-function integrals.
