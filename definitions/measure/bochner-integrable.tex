For a measure space $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ and a Banach space $B$, a strongly measurable $f:X\to B$ is \emph{Bochner integrable} if $\int_X\|f\|_B\,d\mu<\infty$. Its integral is the norm limit of integrals of $B$-valued integrable simple functions approximating $f$ in $L^1$.
