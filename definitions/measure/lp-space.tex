% id: measure.lp-space
% term: Lebesgue $L^p$ space
% related: measure.integrable-function, measure.almost-everywhere
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lp_space
% status: draft

For a measure space $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ and $1\le p<\infty$, the \emph{Lebesgue space} $L^p(X,\mu)$ consists of almost-everywhere equivalence classes of measurable scalar-valued functions $f$ with
\[
\|f\|_p=\left(\int_X|f|^p\,d\mu\right)^{1/p}<\infty.
\]
The space $L^\infty(X,\mu)$ instead consists of equivalence classes of essentially bounded measurable functions with norm $\|f\|_\infty=\inf\{M\ge0:|f|\le M\text{ almost everywhere}\}$. Both are Banach spaces.
