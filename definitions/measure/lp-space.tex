% term: Lebesgue $L^p$ space

% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_integration_and_measure_theory_topics
% status: draft

For $1\le p<\infty$, the space $L^p(X,\mu)$ comprises almost-everywhere equivalence classes of measurable $f$ satisfying $\int_X|f|^p\,d\mu<\infty$.
