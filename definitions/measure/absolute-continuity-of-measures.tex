For two positive measures $\nu,\mu$ on $(X,\Sigma)$, one writes $\nu\ll\mu$ if $\mu(E)=0$ implies $\nu(E)=0$ for every $E\in\Sigma$. This is \emph{absolute continuity of $\nu$ with respect to $\mu$}.
