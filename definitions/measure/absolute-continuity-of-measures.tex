For positive measures $\nu,\mu$ defined on the same measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$, $\nu$ is \emph{absolutely continuous with respect to $\mu$}, denoted $\nu\ll\mu$, if $\mu(E)=0$ implies $\nu(E)=0$ for every $E\in\Sigma$. This is a relation between null sets, not a claim that the two measures have densities; a Radon--Nikodym density additionally requires suitable finiteness conditions.
