% status: draft

For a positive Borel measure $\mu$ on a topological space $X$, its \emph{topological support} is
\[
\operatorname{supp}\mu=\{x\in X:\mu(U)>0\text{ for every open neighborhood }U\ni x\}.
\]
Its complement is the union of all open $\mu$-null subsets and is therefore open, so the support is closed. Additional countability or regularity hypotheses may be needed to identify its complement with a single null set.
