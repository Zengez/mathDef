% term: Support of a positive Borel measure
% related: measure.borel-sigma-algebra, measure.radon-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Support_%28measure_theory%29
% status: draft

For a positive Borel measure $\mu$ on $X$, $\operatorname{supp}\mu=\{x\in X:\mu(U)>0\text{ for every open neighborhood }U\ni x\}$. This \emph{support} is closed.
