A \emph{finite signed measure} on $(X,\Sigma)$ is a function $\nu:\Sigma\to\mathbb R$ satisfying $\nu(\varnothing)=0$ and countable additivity on disjoint measurable families. Its values are finite, although they may be positive or negative. The Jordan decomposition expresses it as the difference of two finite mutually singular positive measures; broader conventions also allow one, but not both, of the infinite values $\pm\infty$.
