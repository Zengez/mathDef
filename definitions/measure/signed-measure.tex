A \emph{finite signed measure} on $(X,\Sigma)$ is a countably additive $\nu:\Sigma\to\mathbb R$ with $\nu(\varnothing)=0$: for pairwise disjoint measurable $E_n$, $\nu(\bigcup_nE_n)=\sum_n\nu(E_n)$.
