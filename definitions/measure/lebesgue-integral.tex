For a nonnegative measurable function $f:X\to[0,\infty]$ on $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$, the \emph{Lebesgue integral} is
\[
\int_X f\,d\mu
=\sup\left\{\int_Xs\,d\mu:s\text{ is a nonnegative measurable simple function},\ 0\le s\le f\right\}.
\]
It may be infinite. Integrals of signed or complex-valued functions are defined from positive/negative or real/imaginary parts when the relevant expressions are well-defined.
