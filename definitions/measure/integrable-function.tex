For a measure space $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$, a measurable real- or complex-valued function $f$ is \emph{Lebesgue integrable} if $\int_X|f|\,d\mu<\infty$. Its integral is then finite. For real $f$, write $f=f^+-f^-$, with both $f^+$ and $f^-$ having finite integrals; for complex $f$, integrate its real and imaginary parts separately. This is the $L^1(\mu)$ condition.
