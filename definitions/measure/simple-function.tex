A \emph{simple measurable function} $s:X\to\mathbb R$ is a measurable function with finite image. Equivalently, it has a representation
\[
s=\sum_{j=1}^{m}a_j\mathbf1_{A_j}
\]
for real coefficients $a_j$ and measurable sets $A_j$, which may be chosen pairwise disjoint. For nonnegative $s$, its Lebesgue integral is $\sum_j a_j\mu(A_j)$ in the nonnegative representation, using $0\cdot\infty=0$.
