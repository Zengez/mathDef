% term: Completion of a measure space
% related: measure.measurable-space, measure.null-set
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complete_measure
% status: draft

The \emph{completion} of $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ extends $\Sigma$ to $\overline\Sigma=\{A\cup N:A\in\Sigma,\ N\subseteq Z\in\Sigma,\ \mu(Z)=0\}$ with $\overline\mu(A\cup N)=\mu(A)$. Thus all subsets of null measurable sets become measurable and null.
