% status: draft

Measurable real-valued functions $f_n$ \emph{converge globally in measure} to $f$ on $(X,\Sigma,\mu)$ if $\mu(\{|f_n-f|>\varepsilon\})\to0$ for each $\varepsilon>0$. On infinite-measure spaces, local convergence in measure is generally a weaker notion.
