% term: Total variation measure of a signed measure
% related: measure.jordan-decomposition, measure.signed-measure

% status: draft

For a finite signed measure $\nu$, its \emph{total variation measure} is $|\nu|=\nu^++\nu^-$, using its Jordan components. Equivalently, $|\nu|(E)=\sup\sum_{j=1}^m|\nu(E_j)|$ over finite measurable partitions $E=\bigsqcup_{j=1}^mE_j$.
