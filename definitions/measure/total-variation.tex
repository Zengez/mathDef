% id: measure.total-variation
% term: Total variation measure of a signed measure
% related: measure.jordan-decomposition, measure.signed-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Total_variation
% status: draft

For a finite signed measure $\nu$ with Jordan decomposition $\nu=\nu^+-\nu^-$, its \emph{total variation measure} is $|\nu|=\nu^++\nu^-$. Equivalently, for measurable $E$,
\[
|\nu|(E)=\sup_{\pi}\sum_{A\in\pi}|\nu(A)|,
\]
where $\pi$ ranges over all finite measurable partitions of $E$. The \emph{total variation norm} of $\nu$ is the number $\|\nu\|_{\mathrm{TV}}=|\nu|(X)$, distinguished from the measure $|\nu|$ itself.
