For measurable spaces $(X,\Sigma)$ and $(Y,\mathcal T)$, a function $f:X\to Y$ is \emph{measurable} if $f^{-1}(B)\in\Sigma$ for every $B\in\mathcal T$. This means that $f$ respects the prescribed $\sigma$-algebras; it does not require continuity, and measurability depends on the choice of measurable structure on both spaces.
