The \emph{$n$-dimensional Lebesgue measure} $\lambda_n$ on $\mathbb R^n$ is the completion of the Borel measure assigning an axis-parallel half-open rectangle $\prod_{j=1}^{n}[a_j,b_j)$ the volume $\prod_{j=1}^n(b_j-a_j)$. It is translation invariant, and $\lambda_n(\{x\})=0$ for every point when $n\ge1$. The completion includes subsets of Borel null sets that need not be Borel.
