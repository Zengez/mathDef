% term: Inner regular Borel measure
% related: measure.borel-sigma-algebra, measure.radon-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_measure
% status: draft

A Borel measure $\mu$ on $X$ is \emph{inner regular on a class of measurable sets} if $\mu(A)=\sup\{\mu(K):K\subseteq A,\ K\text{ compact}\}$ for every $A$ in that class. Inner regularity only on open sets is weaker in general than on all Borel sets.
