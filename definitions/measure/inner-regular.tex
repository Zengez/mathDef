% term: Inner regular Borel measure
% related: measure.borel-sigma-algebra, measure.radon-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Radon_measure
% status: draft

A Borel measure $\mu$ on a topological space $X$ is \emph{inner regular on a specified class of Borel sets} if for every set $A$ in that class,
\[
\mu(A)=\sup\{\mu(K):K\subseteq A,\ K\text{ compact}\}.
\]
Inner regularity on all Borel sets is stronger, in general, than inner regularity only on open sets. Definitions of Radon measure combine regularity with additional topological and finiteness hypotheses.
