% id: measure.sigma-ideal
% term: Sigma-ideal of measurable sets
% related: measure.null-set, measure.measurable-space
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sigma-ideal
% status: draft

For a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$, a \emph{$\sigma$-ideal} $\mathcal N\subseteq\Sigma$ contains $\varnothing$, contains every measurable subset of any of its members, and is closed under countable unions. The measurable null sets of a measure constitute an example.
