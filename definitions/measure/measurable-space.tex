A \emph{measurable space} is a pair $(X,\Sigma)$ where $X$ is a set and $\Sigma\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ is a $\sigma$-algebra: it contains $X$, is closed under complementation relative to $X$, and is closed under countable unions. Its elements are the measurable subsets of $X$. A measure is additional structure, not part of a measurable space itself.
