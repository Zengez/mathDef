Two positive measures $\mu,\nu$ on the same measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ are \emph{mutually singular}, written $\mu\perp\nu$, if there exists $A\in\Sigma$ such that $\mu(X\setminus A)=0$ and $\nu(A)=0$. Equivalently, the two measures are concentrated on disjoint measurable sets. Neither measure must have total mass one, and the condition is symmetric in $\mu$ and $\nu$.
