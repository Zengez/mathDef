Positive measures $\mu,\nu$ on $(X,\Sigma)$ are \emph{mutually singular}, written $\mu\perp\nu$, if some measurable $A$ satisfies $\mu(X\setminus A)=0$ and $\nu(A)=0$. They are thereby concentrated on disjoint measurable sets.
