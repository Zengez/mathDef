A (nonnegative) \emph{measure} on a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ is a function $\mu:\Sigma\to[0,\infty]$ with $\mu(\varnothing)=0$ and \emph{countable additivity}: for every sequence $(E_j)$ of pairwise disjoint measurable sets,
\[
\mu\left(\bigcup_{j=1}^\infty E_j\right)=\sum_{j=1}^\infty\mu(E_j).
\]
It need not be finite, $\sigma$-finite, or complete unless further conditions are imposed.
