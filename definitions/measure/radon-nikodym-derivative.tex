Let $\mu,\nu$ be $\sigma$-finite positive measures on $(X,\Sigma)$ with $\nu\ll\mu$. The \emph{Radon--Nikodym derivative} $d\nu/d\mu$ is the $\mu$-almost-everywhere unique nonnegative measurable $f$ for which $\nu(A)=\int_A f\,d\mu$ for all $A\in\Sigma$.
