% id: measure.jordan-decomposition
% term: Jordan decomposition of a signed measure
% related: measure.hahn-decomposition, measure.signed-measure
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hahn_decomposition_theorem#Jordan_decomposition
% status: draft

For a finite signed measure $\nu$, its \emph{Jordan decomposition} is the unique representation $\nu=\nu^+-\nu^-$ as the difference of mutually singular finite positive measures. If $X=P\sqcup N$ is a Hahn decomposition, then
\[
\nu^+(E)=\nu(E\cap P),\qquad \nu^-(E)=-\nu(E\cap N)
\]
for all measurable $E$. The positive measures $\nu^\pm$ do not depend on the chosen Hahn decomposition.
