The \emph{Jordan decomposition} of a finite signed measure $\nu$ is the unique representation $\nu=\nu^+-\nu^-$ by mutually singular finite positive measures. For a Hahn partition $(P,N)$, $\nu^+(E)=\nu(E\cap P)$ and $\nu^-(E)=-\nu(E\cap N)$.
