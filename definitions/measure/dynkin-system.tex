A \emph{Dynkin system} or \emph{$\lambda$-system} on a set $X$ is a family $\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ satisfying $X\in\mathcal D$, $A\in\mathcal D\Rightarrow X\setminus A\in\mathcal D$, and closure under countable unions of pairwise disjoint sets in $\mathcal D$. Equivalently it contains $X$, is closed under proper differences $B\setminus A$ for $A\subseteq B$ in $\mathcal D$, and under increasing countable unions.
