A nonnegative measure $\mu$ on a measurable space $(X,\Sigma)$ is a \emph{finite measure} if $\mu(X)<\infty$. Every measurable subset then has finite measure. Finite measures are $\sigma$-finite, but the converse need not hold; probability measures are finite measures with total mass exactly $1$.
