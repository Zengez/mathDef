% id: measure.strong-measurability
% term: Strong measurability of a Banach-valued function
% related: measure.measurable-function, measure.bochner-integrable
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bochner_integral
% status: draft

A function $f:X\to B$ from a measure space into a Banach space is \emph{strongly measurable} if it is the almost-everywhere pointwise limit of a sequence of measurable $B$-valued simple functions.
