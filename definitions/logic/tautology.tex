A propositional formula $\varphi$ is a \emph{tautology} if it evaluates to true for every assignment of truth values to its propositional variables. Thus $P\lor\neg P$ is a tautology in classical propositional logic. A tautology is true because of its logical form, not because of a particular nonlogical interpretation.
