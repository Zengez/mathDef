Relative to a formal deductive system and a set of assumptions $T$, a \emph{formal proof} of a formula $\varphi$ is a finite sequence of well-formed formulas ending in $\varphi$, each line being an axiom, an allowed assumption from $T$, or the consequence of earlier lines by a specified inference rule. The notation $T\vdash\varphi$ expresses that such a proof exists.
