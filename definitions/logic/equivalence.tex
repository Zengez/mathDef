Formulas $\varphi$ and $\psi$ are \emph{logically equivalent} if they have the same truth value under every interpretation and assignment of their free variables. Equivalently, the biconditional $\varphi\leftrightarrow\psi$ is logically valid. For a fixed theory $T$, equivalence modulo $T$ is the weaker requirement $T\models\varphi\leftrightarrow\psi$.
