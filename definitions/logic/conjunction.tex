For propositions $P$ and $Q$, their \emph{conjunction} $P\land Q$ is true exactly when both $P$ and $Q$ are true. In classical propositional logic it is false in each of the other three truth assignments to $(P,Q)$. It is the Boolean \emph{and} connective.
