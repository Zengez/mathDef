Let $T$ be a set of sentences in a first-order language $L$. A \emph{model of $T$} is an $L$-structure $\mathcal M$ (with a nonempty domain and interpretations of the language's nonlogical symbols) such that $\mathcal M\models\varphi$ for every sentence $\varphi\in T$. Existence of a model is a semantic notion, distinct from the construction of formal proofs.
