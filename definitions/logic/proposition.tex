In classical mathematical reasoning, a \emph{proposition} is a declarative statement that is truth-evaluable under a fixed interpretation, rather than a question or command. In formal first-order logic, a \emph{sentence} is a well-formed formula with no free variables; its truth value is determined by a structure. The terms proposition, statement and sentence have different technical usages across logic and philosophy.
