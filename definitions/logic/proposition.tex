In elementary classical mathematics, a \emph{proposition} is commonly a truth-evaluable claim expressed by a declarative sentence. In philosophical logic, a proposition may instead be understood as the \emph{content or meaning} expressed by such a sentence, not the sentence itself. In formal first-order logic, a \emph{sentence} is a well-formed formula with no free variables, whose truth in a structure is defined by satisfaction. The distinction between propositions, statements and sentences depends on the context.
