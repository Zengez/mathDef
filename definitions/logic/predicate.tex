A \emph{predicate} $P(x_1,\ldots,x_n)$ is a logical expression whose truth may depend on assignments to its free variables. When a structure and variable assignment are fixed, the expression is either satisfied or not satisfied in classical logic. Quantifying a variable binds that variable and changes the expression's free-variable set.
