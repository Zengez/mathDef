For $n\ge1$, the \emph{$n$th homotopy group} $\pi_n(X,x_0)$ comprises based homotopy classes of continuous maps $(S^n,*)\to(X,x_0)$, with group operation induced by concatenation in a chosen coordinate of the $n$-cube. For $n\ge2$, this group is abelian.
