For $n\ge0$, the \emph{$n$th integral singular homology group} is $H_n(X;\mathbb Z)=\ker(\partial_n:C_n(X;\mathbb Z)\to C_{n-1}(X;\mathbb Z))/\operatorname{im}(\partial_{n+1})$, with $C_{-1}=0$ and $\partial_0=0$.
