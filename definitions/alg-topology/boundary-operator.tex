% term: Singular boundary operator

% status: draft

For each $n\ge1$ and singular $n$-simplex $\sigma:\Delta^n\to X$, the \emph{singular boundary operator} is $\partial_n\sigma=\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\sigma\circ\delta_i$, where $\delta_i:\Delta^{n-1}\to\Delta^n$ is the inclusion of the $i$th face. Extend $\mathbb Z$-linearly to singular chains, and set $\partial_0=0$. These maps satisfy $\partial_{n-1}\circ\partial_n=0$.
