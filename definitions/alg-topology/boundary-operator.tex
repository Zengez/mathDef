% status: draft

For a singular $n$-simplex $\sigma:\Delta^n\to X$, its boundary is $\partial_n\sigma=\sum_{i=0}^n(-1)^i\sigma\circ\delta_i$, where $\delta_i$ includes the $i$th face. Extend $\mathbb Z$-linearly.
