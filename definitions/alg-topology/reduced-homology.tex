For a nonempty topological space $X$, \emph{reduced singular homology} $\widetilde H_n(X;\mathbb Z)$ is the homology of the augmented singular chain complex $\cdots\to C_1(X)\to C_0(X)\xrightarrow{\epsilon}\mathbb Z\to0$, where the augmentation $\epsilon$ sends each singular $0$-simplex to $1$. It agrees with ordinary homology for $n>0$ and removes the canonical $\mathbb Z$ summand in degree zero.
