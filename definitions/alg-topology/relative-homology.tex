For a pair of spaces $A\subseteq X$, the \emph{relative singular homology} $H_n(X,A;\mathbb Z)$ is the degree-$n$ homology of $C_*(X;\mathbb Z)/C_*(A;\mathbb Z)$ with the induced boundary operator.
