For a topological space $X$ and an abelian coefficient group $G$, its \emph{singular cohomology} $H^n(X;G)$ is the degree-$n$ cohomology of $C^n(X;G)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb Z}(C_n(X;\mathbb Z),G)$ with coboundary $\delta\varphi=\varphi\circ\partial_{n+1}$.
