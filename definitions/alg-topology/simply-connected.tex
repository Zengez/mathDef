A topological space $X$ is \emph{simply connected} if it is path-connected and every loop based at some (equivalently any) point $x_0\in X$ is homotopic relative endpoints to the constant loop. Equivalently, it is path-connected with $\pi_1(X,x_0)$ trivial.
