For a topological space $X$ and coefficient abelian group $G$, a \emph{singular $n$-cycle} is a singular $n$-chain $c\in C_n(X;G)$ satisfying
\[
\partial_n c=0.
\]
Thus the group of singular $n$-cycles is $Z_n(X;G)=\ker(\partial_n)$; every singular $n$-boundary is a cycle because $\partial_n\partial_{n+1}=0$.
