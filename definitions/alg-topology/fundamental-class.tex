For a connected closed oriented topological $n$-manifold $M$, the \emph{fundamental class} $[M]\in H_n(M;\mathbb Z)\cong\mathbb Z$ is the generator whose image in each local homology group $H_n(M,M\setminus\{x\};\mathbb Z)$ agrees with the chosen local orientation at $x\in M$.
