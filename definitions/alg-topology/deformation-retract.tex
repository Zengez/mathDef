% term: Deformation retract
% related: alg-topology.retraction, alg-topology.homotopy
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/singular%2Bhomology
% status: draft

A subspace $A\subseteq X$ is a \emph{deformation retract} if a retraction $r:X\to A$ exists with a homotopy $H$ from $\operatorname{id}_X$ to the inclusion composed with $r$, fixing every point of $A$ throughout.
