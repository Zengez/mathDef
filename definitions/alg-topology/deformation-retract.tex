% status: draft

A subspace $A\subseteq X$ is a \emph{strong deformation retract} if there is a continuous $H:X\times[0,1]\to X$ with $H(x,0)=x$, $H(x,1)\in A$ for all $x\in X$, and $H(a,t)=a$ for every $a\in A$ and $t\in[0,1]$. Some authors use ``deformation retract'' for the weaker condition that the homotopy need not fix $A$ at intermediate times.
