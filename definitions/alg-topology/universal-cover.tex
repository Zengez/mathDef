A \emph{universal covering space} of a connected space $B$ is a covering $p:\widetilde B\to B$ with $\widetilde B$ path-connected and simply connected. Existence is not automatic: sufficiently well-behaved spaces (for example, connected, locally path-connected and semilocally simply connected spaces) admit such a covering.
