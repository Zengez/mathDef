For a topological space $X$ and a commutative coefficient ring $R$, the \emph{cup product} is the bilinear graded pairing $H^p(X;R)\times H^q(X;R)\to H^{p+q}(X;R)$ induced on singular cochains by the Alexander--Whitney construction. It makes $H^*(X;R)$ a graded ring.
