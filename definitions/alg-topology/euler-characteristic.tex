% term: Euler characteristic of a finite complex
% related: alg-topology.cell-complex, alg-topology.singular-homology
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/singular%2Bhomology
% status: draft

For a finite CW complex $X$ with $c_n$ cells of dimension $n$, its \emph{Euler characteristic} is $\chi(X)=\sum_n(-1)^nc_n$.
