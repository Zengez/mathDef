A \emph{singular $n$-simplex} in a topological space $X$ is a continuous map
\[
\sigma:\Delta^n\to X
\]
from the standard topological $n$-simplex to $X$. The map need not be injective; its image $|\sigma|=\sigma(\Delta^n)$ is called the support of the singular simplex.
