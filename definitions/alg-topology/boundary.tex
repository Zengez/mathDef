For a topological space $X$ and coefficient abelian group $G$, a \emph{singular $n$-boundary} is a singular $n$-chain lying in the image of
\[
\partial_{n+1}:C_{n+1}(X;G)\to C_n(X;G).
\]
Thus the group of singular $n$-boundaries is $B_n(X;G)=\operatorname{im}(\partial_{n+1})\subseteq Z_n(X;G)$.
