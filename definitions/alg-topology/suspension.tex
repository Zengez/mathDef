For a pointed space $(X,x_0)$, the \emph{reduced suspension} $\Sigma X$ is the quotient of $X\times[0,1]$ collapsing the subspace $(X\times\{0,1\})\cup(\{x_0\}\times[0,1])$ to a single basepoint. It differs from the unreduced suspension, which collapses the two ends separately.
