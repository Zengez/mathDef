In the nonempty-face convention, an \emph{abstract simplicial complex} $K$ on a vertex set $V$ is a collection of finite nonempty subsets of $V$ such that every nonempty subset of a face $\sigma\in K$ also lies in $K$. Other conventions also require or allow the empty face.
