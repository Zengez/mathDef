For a proper, lower semicontinuous, convex function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ and a parameter $\lambda>0$, its \emph{proximal operator} is
\[
\operatorname{prox}_{\lambda f}(x)=\underset{y\in\mathbb R^n}{\operatorname{argmin}}\left\{f(y)+\frac{1}{2\lambda}\|y-x\|^2\right\}.
\]
In finite-dimensional Euclidean space the minimizer exists and is unique. The related optimality condition is $(x-\operatorname{prox}_{\lambda f}(x))/\lambda\in\partial f(\operatorname{prox}_{\lambda f}(x))$.
