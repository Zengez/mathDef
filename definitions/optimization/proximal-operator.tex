% id: optimization.proximal-operator
% term: Proximal operator
% related: optimization.subdifferential, optimization.proper-convex-function
% source: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
% status: draft

For a proper lower semicontinuous convex function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,\infty]$ and $\lambda>0$, $\operatorname{prox}_{\lambda f}(x)$ is the unique minimizer of $f(y)+\|y-x\|^2/(2\lambda)$.
