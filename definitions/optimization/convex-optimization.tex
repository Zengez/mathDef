A \emph{convex optimization problem} is a problem of minimizing a convex extended-real-valued objective $f_0$ over a convex feasible set $C$ in a real vector space. A common finite-dimensional form is to minimize convex $f_0(x)$ subject to inequalities $f_i(x)\le0$ with convex $f_i$ and affine equalities $Ax=b$. Convexity refers to the objective and feasible set, not to a particular optimization algorithm.
