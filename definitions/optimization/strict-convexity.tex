A real-valued function $f:C\to\mathbb R$ on a convex subset $C$ of a real vector space is \emph{strictly convex} if, whenever $x,y\in C$ are distinct and $0<t<1$,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)<(1-t)f(x)+tf(y).
\]
Strict convexity implies at most one minimizer over a convex set, but does not guarantee existence of one.
