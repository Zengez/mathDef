For an extended-real-valued function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$, its \emph{epigraph} is
\[
\operatorname{epi}f=\{(x,t)\in\mathbb R^n\times\mathbb R:f(x)\le t\}.
\]
It is convex precisely when $f$ is convex, and closed precisely when $f$ is lower semicontinuous (in the Euclidean setting).
