% id: optimization.convex-cone
% term: Convex cone
% related: real.convex-set, optimization.dual-cone
% source: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
% status: draft

A subset $K$ of a real vector space is a \emph{convex cone} if $\alpha x+\beta y\in K$ whenever $x,y\in K$ and $\alpha,\beta\ge0$.
