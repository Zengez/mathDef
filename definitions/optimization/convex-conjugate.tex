For a function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$, its \emph{Fenchel (convex) conjugate} is the extended-real-valued function $f^*:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ defined by
\[
f^*(y)=\sup_{x\in\mathbb R^n}\bigl(\langle y,x\rangle-f(x)\bigr).
\]
The definition does not require $f$ itself to be convex; $f^*$ is always convex and lower semicontinuous in the usual finite-dimensional topology.
