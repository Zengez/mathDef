For an extended-real-valued function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$, its \emph{Fenchel (convex) conjugate} is
\[
f^*(y)=\sup_{x\in\mathbb R^n}\bigl(\langle y,x\rangle-f(x)\bigr).
\]
It is convex and lower semicontinuous even if $f$ is not convex. If $f$ is proper (finite somewhere), then $f^*$ takes values in $(-\infty,+\infty]$; if $f\equiv+\infty$, it is identically $-\infty$ under the extended-real convention.
