For a differentiable objective $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$ and positive step sizes $\alpha_k$, \emph{gradient descent} generates iterates by $x_{k+1}=x_k-\alpha_k\nabla f(x_k)$. The negative gradient is the steepest-descent direction in the Euclidean metric. Convergence to a minimizer requires additional assumptions and an appropriate step-size rule.
