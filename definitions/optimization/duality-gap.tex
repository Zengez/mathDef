% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Duality_gap
% status: draft

For a minimization problem and its Lagrange dual with finite optimal values $p^*=\inf_{x\text{ feasible}}f_0(x)$ and $d^*=\sup_{\lambda\ge0,\nu}g(\lambda,\nu)$, the \emph{duality gap} is $p^*-d^*\ge0$ by weak duality. A zero gap expresses equality of optimal values, whether or not either optimum is attained.
