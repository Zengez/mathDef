A \emph{quadratic program} minimizes or maximizes a quadratic objective subject to affine equality and inequality constraints. A \emph{convex quadratic program} in the minimization convention has objective $\frac12x^{\mathsf T}Px+q^{\mathsf T}x+r$, where $P$ is symmetric positive semidefinite, and affine constraints such as $Ax\le b$ and $Ex=h$. No positive-definiteness assumption is required for convexity.
