A real symmetric $n\times n$ matrix $A$ is \emph{positive semidefinite} if $x^{\mathsf T}Ax\ge0$ for all $x\in\mathbb R^n$, equivalently every eigenvalue of $A$ is nonnegative. For complex matrices the corresponding definition uses a Hermitian matrix and the form $z^*Az\ge0$. Zero eigenvalues are permitted.
