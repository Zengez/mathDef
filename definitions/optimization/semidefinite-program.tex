A \emph{semidefinite program} (SDP) is a convex optimization problem with a linear objective and a positive-semidefinite matrix constraint depending affinely on the decision variables. One standard form minimizes $c^{\mathsf T}x$ over $x\in\mathbb R^n$ subject to
\[
F_0+\sum_{i=1}^n x_iF_i\succeq0,
\]
where the $F_i$ are prescribed real symmetric matrices. Additional affine constraints may be included.
