% term: Convex subdifferential

For a proper convex function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ and a point $x$ in its effective domain, the \emph{convex subdifferential} is
\[
\partial f(x)=\{g\in\mathbb R^n:f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle g,y-x\rangle\text{ for every }y\in\mathbb R^n\}.
\]
Outside $\operatorname{dom}f$ it is defined to be empty. The set can be empty even at some points of the effective domain, especially at a boundary.
