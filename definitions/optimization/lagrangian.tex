For a problem minimizing $f_0(x)$ subject to $f_i(x)\le0$ and $h_j(x)=0$, its \emph{Lagrangian} is $L(x,\lambda,\nu)=f_0(x)+\sum_i\lambda_i f_i(x)+\sum_j\nu_j h_j(x)$ with $\lambda_i\ge0$.
