% status: draft

For a problem minimizing $f_0(x)$ over a domain $D\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ subject to $f_i(x)\le0$ ($i=1,\ldots,m$) and $h_j(x)=0$ ($j=1,\ldots,p$), its \emph{Lagrangian} is
\[
L(x,\lambda,\nu)=f_0(x)+\sum_{i=1}^m\lambda_i f_i(x)+\sum_{j=1}^p\nu_j h_j(x).
\]
The formula is defined for real multipliers; the Lagrange dual problem restricts $\lambda_i\ge0$, while $\nu_j$ are unrestricted.
