A real symmetric $n\times n$ matrix $A$ is \emph{positive definite} if $x^{\mathsf T}Ax>0$ for every nonzero $x\in\mathbb R^n$. Equivalently, all its eigenvalues are positive. For a complex Hermitian matrix the analogous condition is $z^*Az>0$ for all nonzero complex vectors $z$. Symmetry or Hermitian structure is part of the convention used here.
