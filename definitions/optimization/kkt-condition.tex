For a differentiable problem minimizing $f_0(x)$ subject to $f_i(x)\le0$ and $h_j(x)=0$, the \emph{Karush--Kuhn--Tucker (KKT) conditions} for a point $x$ and multipliers $(\lambda,\nu)$ are: primal feasibility $f_i(x)\le0$, $h_j(x)=0$; dual feasibility $\lambda_i\ge0$; complementary slackness $\lambda_i f_i(x)=0$; and stationarity
\[
\nabla f_0(x)+\sum_i\lambda_i\nabla f_i(x)+\sum_j\nu_j\nabla h_j(x)=0.
\]
Under suitable constraint qualifications these are necessary at local optima; in convex problems with affine equalities they are sufficient for global optimality.
