For a minimization problem with decision domain $D$ and Lagrangian $L(x,\lambda,\nu)$, the \emph{Lagrange dual function} is
\[
g(\lambda,\nu)=\inf_{x\in D}L(x,\lambda,\nu).
\]
For a nonempty domain and finite-valued objective/constraints, $g$ is finite or $-\infty$. It is concave in $(\lambda,\nu)$ regardless of convexity of the primal problem; nonnegative inequality multipliers ensure the resulting lower bound for feasible points.
