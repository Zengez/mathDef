% id: optimization.strong-convexity
% term: Strong convexity
% related: real.convex-function, optimization.strict-convexity

% status: draft

For $m>0$, a real-valued function $f:C\to\mathbb R$ on a convex subset of Euclidean space is \emph{$m$-strongly convex} if $x\mapsto f(x)-\frac m2\|x\|^2$ is convex on $C$. Equivalently,
\[
f((1-t)x+ty)\le(1-t)f(x)+tf(y)-\frac m2t(1-t)\|x-y\|^2
\]
for all $x,y\in C$ and $t\in[0,1]$. The constant depends on the chosen norm/inner product.
