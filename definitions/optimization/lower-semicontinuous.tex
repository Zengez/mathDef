A function $f:X\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ on a topological space $X$ is \emph{lower semicontinuous} if $\{x\in X:f(x)>a\}$ is open for every real $a$. On a metric space, this is equivalent to $f(x)\le\liminf_{n\to\infty}f(x_n)$ for every sequence $x_n\to x$. For a Euclidean domain, it is equivalent to the epigraph of $f$ being closed.
