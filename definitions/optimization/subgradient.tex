% term: Subgradient

For a proper convex function $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,+\infty]$ and $x\in\operatorname{dom}f$, a vector $g\in\mathbb R^n$ is a \emph{subgradient} of $f$ at $x$ if
\[
f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle g,y-x\rangle\quad\text{for all }y\in\mathbb R^n.
\]
Thus $g$ defines a global affine minorant touching $f$ at $x$. At a differentiable interior point, $\partial f(x)=\{\nabla f(x)\}$.
