% id: optimization.subgradient
% term: Subgradient
% related: real.convex-function, optimization.subdifferential
% source: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
% status: draft

For convex $f:\mathbb R^n\to(-\infty,\infty]$ and $x$ with $f(x)<\infty$, a vector $g$ is a \emph{subgradient} at $x$ if $f(y)\ge f(x)+\langle g,y-x\rangle$ for every $y$.
