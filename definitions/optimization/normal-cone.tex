For a convex set $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $x\in C$, its \emph{convex-analytic normal cone} is
\[
N_C(x)=\{v\in\mathbb R^n:\langle v,y-x\rangle\le0\ \text{for all }y\in C\}.
\]
By convention $N_C(x)=\varnothing$ for $x\notin C$. This is the subdifferential of the indicator function of $C$ and uses the outward-normal sign convention.
