% id: optimization.normal-cone
% term: Normal cone of a convex set
% related: real.convex-set, optimization.subgradient
% source: https://web.stanford.edu/~boyd/cvxbook/
% status: draft

For convex $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ and $x\in C$, the \emph{normal cone} is $N_C(x)=\{v:\langle v,y-x\rangle\le0\text{ for all }y\in C\}$.
