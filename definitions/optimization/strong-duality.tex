A minimization problem and its Lagrange dual satisfy \emph{strong duality} if the primal infimum $p^*$ equals the dual supremum $d^*$ (usually with a finite common value). Unlike \emph{weak duality}, which gives $d^*\le p^*$ under dual feasibility, strong duality need not hold for an arbitrary nonconvex or constrained problem. Equality of values alone does not imply attainment by either problem.
