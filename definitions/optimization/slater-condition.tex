% status: draft

Consider a convex program minimizing a convex objective subject to convex inequalities $f_i(x)\le0$, affine equalities $Ax=b$, and a convex domain $D$. A standard \emph{Slater constraint qualification} requires a point $x\in\operatorname{relint}D$ with $Ax=b$ and $f_i(x)<0$ for every nonlinear inequality. Affine inequalities may instead be satisfied nonstrictly in the refined Slater condition. Under standard finite-dimensional regularity hypotheses, Slater's condition implies strong Lagrange duality; the precise relative-interior convention matters.
