If $f:U\to\mathbb R$ is twice continuously differentiable on an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, its \emph{Hessian matrix} at $x\in U$ is $\nabla^2f(x)=(\partial^2 f/\partial x_i\partial x_j(x))_{i,j=1}^n$. By equality of mixed partial derivatives it is symmetric. It represents the second differential in Euclidean coordinates.
