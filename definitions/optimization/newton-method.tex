For twice differentiable $f:\mathbb R^n\to\mathbb R$, the \emph{Newton direction} at $x_k$ is the solution $d_k$ of $\nabla^2f(x_k)d_k=-\nabla f(x_k)$ when the Hessian is invertible. The full Newton update is $x_{k+1}=x_k+d_k$, and a damped update is $x_{k+1}=x_k+\alpha_k d_k$. The direction is descending when the Hessian is positive definite and $\nabla f(x_k)\ne0$; invertibility alone does not guarantee descent.
