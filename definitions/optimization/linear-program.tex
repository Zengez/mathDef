A \emph{linear program} in finitely many real variables minimizes or maximizes an affine objective $c^{\mathsf T}x+d$ subject to finitely many affine equality and inequality constraints, for example $Ax\le b$ and $Ex=r$. Its feasible region is a polyhedron; it may be empty, unbounded, or have no attained finite optimum.
