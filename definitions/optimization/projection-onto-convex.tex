% id: optimization.projection-onto-convex
% term: Euclidean projection onto a convex set
% related: real.convex-set, optimization.proximal-operator
% source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/s13660-015-0665-9
% status: draft

For a nonempty closed convex set $C\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, the \emph{Euclidean projection} $P_C(x)$ of $x\in\mathbb R^n$ is the unique point $y\in C$ minimizing $\|x-y\|$. It is characterized by
\[
\langle x-P_C(x),z-P_C(x)\rangle\le0\qquad(z\in C).
\]
Nonemptiness, closedness and convexity ensure existence and uniqueness in finite-dimensional Euclidean space.
