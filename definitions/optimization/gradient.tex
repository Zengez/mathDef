For a differentiable real-valued function $f$ on an open subset of Euclidean space, the \emph{gradient} $\nabla f(x)\in\mathbb R^n$ is the vector representing the differential under the Euclidean inner product:
\[
Df(x)[v]=\langle\nabla f(x),v\rangle\quad\text{for all }v\in\mathbb R^n.
\]
In Cartesian coordinates it has entries $\partial f/\partial x_i$.
