For a cone $K\subseteq\mathbb R^n$, its \emph{dual cone} (positive dual) is $K^*=\{y\in\mathbb R^n:\langle y,x\rangle\ge0\text{ for every }x\in K\}$. It is always a closed convex cone even if $K$ is not closed or convex. Some sources instead use the nonpositive polar cone, changing the inequality sign.
