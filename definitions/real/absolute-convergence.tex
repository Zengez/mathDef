A series of real or complex numbers $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n$ is \emph{absolutely convergent} if the nonnegative real series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty |a_n|$ converges to a finite value. For series in a normed space, replace absolute value by the norm.
