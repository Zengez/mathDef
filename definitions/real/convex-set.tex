% status: draft

A subset $C$ of a real vector space is \emph{convex} if the line segment between any two points of $C$ lies in $C$: for every $x,y\in C$ and $t\in[0,1]$, $(1-t)x+ty\in C$. This is an algebraic condition; no norm or topology is needed.
