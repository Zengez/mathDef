% status: draft

For a nonempty set $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ bounded above, the \emph{supremum} $\sup A$ is its least upper bound: $a\le\sup A$ for all $a\in A$, and $\sup A\le b$ for any other upper bound $b$. Under the extended-real convention one may also write $\sup\varnothing=-\infty$.
