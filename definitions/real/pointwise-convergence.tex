% id: real.pointwise-convergence
% term: Pointwise convergence
% related: real.uniform-convergence, analysis.convergence
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pointwise_convergence
% status: draft

Functions $f_n:E\to\mathbb R$ \emph{converge pointwise} to $f:E\to\mathbb R$ if, for every $x\in E$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $N=N(x,\varepsilon)$ such that $n\ge N$ implies $|f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon$. The index $N$ may depend on $x$; this distinguishes pointwise from uniform convergence.
