Let $f:I\to\mathbb R$ be defined on an open interval $I\subseteq\mathbb R$ and $a\in I$. Its \emph{derivative} at $a$ is the finite limit
\[
f'(a)=\lim_{h\to0}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h},
\]
if this limit exists. Here $h$ ranges over nonzero real numbers for which $a+h\in I$.
