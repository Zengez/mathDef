For $f:D\to\mathbb R$ and an accumulation point $a$ of $D$, $\lim_{x\to a}f(x)=L$ means that for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that $x\in D$ and $0<|x-a|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-L|<\varepsilon$.
