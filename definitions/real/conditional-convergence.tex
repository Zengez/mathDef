% status: draft

A series $\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n$ of real or complex numbers is \emph{conditionally convergent} if its sequence of partial sums converges but $\sum_{n=0}^\infty |a_n|$ diverges. Thus conditional convergence is convergence without absolute convergence.
