For a partition $a=x_0<\cdots<x_n=b$ and tags $\xi_i\in[x_{i-1},x_i]$, a \emph{Riemann sum} of $f$ is $\sum_{i=1}^n f(\xi_i)(x_i-x_{i-1})$.
