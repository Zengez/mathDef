Functions $f_n:E\to\mathbb R$ \emph{converge uniformly} to $f:E\to\mathbb R$ if
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\
\forall n\ge N\ \forall x\in E:\ |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.
\]
Unlike pointwise convergence, $N$ is chosen independently of $x$. Equivalently $\sup_{x\in E}|f_n(x)-f(x)|\to0$ when $E$ is nonempty and the suprema are finite eventually.
