Functions $f_n:E\to\mathbb R$ \emph{converge uniformly} to $f$ if
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\ 
\forall n\ge N\ \forall x\in E:\ |f_n(x)-f(x)|<\varepsilon.
\]
