A map $f:(X,d_X)\to(Y,d_Y)$ between metric spaces is \emph{Hölder continuous} with exponent $\alpha>0$ if there exists a constant $C\ge0$ such that $d_Y(f(x),f(y))\le C\,d_X(x,y)^\alpha$ for all $x,y\in X$. The common Hölder-class convention takes $0<\alpha\le1$; the displayed inequality also makes sense for larger exponents.
