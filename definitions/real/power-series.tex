A \emph{power series} in a real or complex variable $z$, centered at $a$, is an expression $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}c_n(z-a)^n$ with fixed scalar coefficients $c_n$. It is a formal series until evaluated where it converges. There is a radius $R\in[0,\infty]$ of absolute convergence for $|z-a|<R$ and divergence for $|z-a|>R$; behavior on $|z-a|=R$ varies.
