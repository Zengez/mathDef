For a nonempty set $A\subseteq\mathbb R$ bounded below, its \emph{infimum} $\inf A$ is the greatest lower bound: $\inf A\le a$ for all $a\in A$, and every lower bound $b$ of $A$ satisfies $b\le\inf A$. In the extended real numbers, one may also set $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$.
