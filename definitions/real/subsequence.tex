A \emph{subsequence} of a sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ is a sequence $(a_{n_k})_{k\ge1}$ obtained using strictly increasing indices $n_1<n_2<\cdots$. Necessarily $n_k\to\infty$, and the relative order of the selected terms is preserved.
