Given a sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge1}$ of real or complex numbers, the \emph{infinite series} $\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}a_n$ is defined to converge to $S$ when its partial sums $s_N=\sum_{n=1}^Na_n$ tend to $S$ as $N\to\infty$. Otherwise the series diverges; the symbolic sum alone does not imply existence of a finite value.
