% id: real.continuity-at-point
% term: Continuity at a point
% related: real.limit-of-function, real.derivative
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_real_analysis_topics
% status: draft

A function $f:D\to\mathbb R$ is \emph{continuous at} $a\in D$ if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $\delta>0$ such that $x\in D$ and $|x-a|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon$.
