% id: real.continuity-at-point
% term: Continuity at a point
% related: real.limit-of-function, real.derivative
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_function
% status: draft

A function $f:D\to\mathbb R$, with $D\subseteq\mathbb R$, is \emph{continuous at} $a\in D$ if, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $\delta>0$ such that $x\in D$ and $|x-a|<\delta$ imply $|f(x)-f(a)|<\varepsilon$. The quantifier over $x$ is relative to $D$, so continuity at an isolated point of $D$ is automatic.
