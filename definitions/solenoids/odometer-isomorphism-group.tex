For an odometer action $\Phi:\Gamma\curvearrowright X$, its \emph{isomorphism group} consists of homeomorphisms $h:X\to X$ for which some $\Theta\in\operatorname{Aut}(\Gamma)$ makes $h\circ\Phi(g)=\Phi(\Theta(g))\circ h$ for every $g\in\Gamma$. The subgroup with $\Theta=\operatorname{id}_{\Gamma}$ is the action's automorphism group.
