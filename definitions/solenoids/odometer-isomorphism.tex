% source_title: Mapping class groups of solenoidal manifolds
% status: draft

Odometer actions $\Phi_i:\Gamma_i\times X_i\to X_i$ are \emph{isomorphic} if there exist a homeomorphism $h:X_1\to X_2$ and a group isomorphism $\Theta:\Gamma_1\to\Gamma_2$ such that $h(\Phi_1(g,x))=\Phi_2(\Theta(g),h(x))$ for every $g\in\Gamma_1$ and $x\in X_1$.
