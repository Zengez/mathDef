For a group $\Gamma$, the \emph{abstract commensurator} $\operatorname{Comm}(\Gamma)$ is the group of equivalence classes of isomorphisms $\phi:H\to K$ between finite-index subgroups of $\Gamma$. Two such isomorphisms are equivalent when their restrictions agree on a finite-index subgroup of the intersection of their domains. Multiplication is induced by composition wherever representatives are jointly defined.
