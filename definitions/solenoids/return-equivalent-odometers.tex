Odometer actions $\Gamma\curvearrowright X$ and $\Gamma'\curvearrowright X'$ are \emph{return equivalent} if there are nonempty adapted clopen subsets $U\subseteq X$ and $U'\subseteq X'$ and a homeomorphism $h:U\to U'$ conjugating their restricted holonomy groups. Here $U$ is adapted when $\gamma U\cap U\ne\varnothing$ implies $\gamma U=U$.
