In the terminology of Hurder and Lukina, an \emph{odometer} is an action $\Phi:\Gamma\times X\to X$ of a finitely generated group on a Cantor space $X$ that is both minimal (every orbit is dense in $X$) and equicontinuous (the family of maps $\{\Phi(g):g\in\Gamma\}$ is equicontinuous).
