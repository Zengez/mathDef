Let $\Gamma\curvearrowright X$ be an odometer and fix $x\in X$. A \emph{dynamical commensurator at $x$} is a homeomorphism $h:U\to V$ between adapted clopen neighborhoods of $x$ that fixes $x$ and conjugates the holonomy groups of the restricted actions. Two such maps represent the same \emph{germ} if they coincide on some smaller adapted neighborhood of $x$. Their germs form the dynamical commensurator group.
