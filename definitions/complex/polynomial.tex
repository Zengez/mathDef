A \emph{complex polynomial} is a finite sum $p(z)=\sum_{k=0}^{n}a_kz^k$ with coefficients $a_k\in\mathbb C$ and $n\ge0$. If $p$ is nonzero and $a_n\ne0$, its degree is $n$; the zero polynomial has no finite degree under this convention. Every complex polynomial defines an entire function.
