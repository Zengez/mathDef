A function $f:U\to\mathbb C$ on an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb C$ is \emph{holomorphic} if its complex derivative
\[
f'(a)=\lim_{z\to a}\frac{f(z)-f(a)}{z-a}
\]
exists at every $a\in U$. In one complex variable this is equivalent to local representation by a convergent complex power series, and implies derivatives of all orders.
