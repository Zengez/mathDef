A real-valued function $u\in C^2(U)$ on an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is \emph{harmonic} if its Laplacian vanishes at every point:
\[
\Delta u=\sum_{j=1}^n\frac{\partial^2u}{\partial x_j^2}=0.
\]
For the classical definition $C^2$ regularity is assumed. Distributional or weak harmonicity can instead be defined without initially assuming classical second derivatives, and then implies smoothness under standard hypotheses.
