If $f$ is holomorphic on $0<|z-a|<r$, then $a$ is a \emph{pole of order $m\ge1$} if $(z-a)^m f(z)$ extends holomorphically to $a$ with a nonzero value there. Equivalently, $f$ has a Laurent expansion whose most negative nonzero term is $c_{-m}(z-a)^{-m}$. In that case $|f(z)|\to\infty$ as $z\to a$.
