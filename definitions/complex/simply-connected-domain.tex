A \emph{simply connected plane domain} is a nonempty connected open set $U\subseteq\mathbb C$ such that every continuous closed loop $\gamma:S^1\to U$ can be contracted within $U$ to a constant loop. Equivalently, its fundamental group is trivial; for plane domains, this is also equivalent to connectedness of the complement $\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus U$ in the Riemann sphere.
