For $z=x+iy\in\mathbb C$, its \emph{modulus} is the nonnegative real number
\[
|z|=\sqrt{x^2+y^2}=\sqrt{z\overline z}.
\]
It is the Euclidean distance from $z$ to $0$ in the complex plane and satisfies $|zw|=|z||w|$ and the triangle inequality $|z+w|\le|z|+|w|$.
