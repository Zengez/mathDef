The \emph{Cayley transform} $C(z)=\dfrac{z-i}{z+i}$ is a Möbius transformation mapping the upper half-plane $\mathbb H=\{z\in\mathbb C:\operatorname{Im}z>0\}$ biholomorphically onto the open unit disk $\mathbb D$. Its inverse on $\mathbb D$ is $C^{-1}(w)=i(1+w)/(1-w)$. Other conventions call its inverse or a related disk-to-half-plane map the Cayley transform.
