A \emph{Laurent series} with center $a\in\mathbb C$ is a series $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_n(z-a)^n$, interpreted as the sum of its nonnegative and negative-power parts where both converge. Its domain of convergence is an annulus $r<|z-a|<R$, possibly with $r=0$ or $R=\infty$, and the convergence is uniform on compact subannuli. Every holomorphic function on an annulus has a unique such expansion there.
