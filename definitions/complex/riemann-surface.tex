A \emph{Riemann surface} is a connected, Hausdorff, second-countable complex manifold of complex dimension one. Thus it admits an atlas of homeomorphisms onto open subsets of $\mathbb C$ whose transition maps are biholomorphic wherever defined. The charts specify a complex structure and need not identify the surface globally with a plane domain.
