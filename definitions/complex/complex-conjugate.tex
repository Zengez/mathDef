For $z=x+iy\in\mathbb C$ with real $x,y$, its \emph{complex conjugate} is $\overline z=x-iy$. Conjugation satisfies $\overline{z+w}=\overline z+\overline w$, $\overline{zw}=\overline z\,\overline w$, and $z\overline z=|z|^2$. It reverses the sign of the imaginary part.
