% status: draft

For $f=u+iv$ with real differentiable components on a plane domain, the \emph{Cauchy–Riemann equations} are $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$, equivalently $\partial f/\partial\bar z=0$. For $C^1$ functions these equations characterize holomorphy.
