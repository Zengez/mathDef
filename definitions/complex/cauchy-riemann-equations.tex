Writing $f=u+iv$ for real-valued functions $u,v$ on an open subset of $\mathbb C$, the \emph{Cauchy--Riemann equations} are $u_x=v_y$ and $u_y=-v_x$, equivalently $\partial f/\partial\overline z=0$ wherever the partial derivatives exist. At a point where $u$ and $v$ are real (Fréchet) differentiable, these equations are equivalent to complex differentiability at that point. If $u,v$ are $C^1$ on the open set, they characterize holomorphy throughout it.
