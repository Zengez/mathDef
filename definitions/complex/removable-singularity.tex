If $f$ is holomorphic on $0<|z-a|<r$, the isolated singularity at $a$ is \emph{removable} if there is a holomorphic function $F$ on $|z-a|<r$ such that $F(z)=f(z)$ for $z\ne a$. Equivalently, the Laurent expansion at $a$ has no negative powers; by Riemann's removable singularity theorem, local boundedness of $f$ near $a$ is sufficient and necessary.
