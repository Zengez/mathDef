% term: Complex differentiability
% related: complex.holomorphic-function, complex.complex-number
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Complex_differentiable_function
% status: draft

For a function $f:U\to\mathbb C$ on an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb C$ and a point $a\in U$, \emph{complex differentiability at $a$} means that the finite limit
\[
f'(a)=\lim_{\substack{h\to0\\h\ne0}}\frac{f(a+h)-f(a)}{h}
\]
exists in $\mathbb C$. The limit must be the same along every complex direction, unlike real one-dimensional differentiability. Holomorphy requires complex differentiability at every point of an open neighborhood.
