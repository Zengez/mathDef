For a differentiable function $f(x+iy)$ of two real variables, the \emph{Wirtinger differential operators} are
\[
\frac{\partial}{\partial z}=\frac12(\partial_x-i\partial_y),\qquad
\frac{\partial}{\partial\bar z}=\frac12(\partial_x+i\partial_y).
\]
