For a continuously real-differentiable function $f:U\subseteq\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$, write $z=x+iy$ and define the \emph{Wirtinger derivatives} by
\[
f_z=\frac12(f_x-if_y),\qquad f_{\overline z}=\frac12(f_x+if_y).
\]
These are the coefficients of the complex-linear and conjugate-linear parts of the real differential $df=f_z\,dz+f_{\overline z}\,d\overline z$. Such $f$ is holomorphic exactly when $f_{\overline z}=0$ throughout $U$.
