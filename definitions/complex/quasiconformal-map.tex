An orientation-preserving homeomorphism $f:U\to V$ between plane domains is \emph{$K$-quasiconformal} if $f$ belongs locally to $W^{1,2}$ and satisfies $|f_{\bar z}|\le k|f_z|$ almost everywhere, where $k=(K-1)/(K+1)<1$ and $K\ge1$.
