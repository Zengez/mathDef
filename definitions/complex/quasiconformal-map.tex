Let $U,V\subseteq\mathbb C$ be domains and $K\ge1$. An orientation-preserving homeomorphism $f:U\to V$ is \emph{$K$-quasiconformal} if $f\in W^{1,2}_{\mathrm{loc}}(U)$ and, almost everywhere,
\[
|f_{\overline z}|\le k|f_z|,\qquad k=\frac{K-1}{K+1}<1.
\]
Here $f_z,f_{\overline z}$ denote weak Wirtinger derivatives. The inequality bounds infinitesimal distortion; for $K=1$, the map is conformal.
