For a meromorphic function $f$ on a plane domain that is not identically zero, its \emph{logarithmic derivative} is the meromorphic function $f'/f$. Away from zeros and poles it equals the derivative of any local holomorphic branch of $\log f$. At a zero of order $m$ it has a simple pole of residue $m$; at a pole of order $m$ it has a simple pole of residue $-m$.
