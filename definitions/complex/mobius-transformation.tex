A \emph{Möbius transformation} is an automorphism of the Riemann sphere $\widehat{\mathbb C}$ represented in affine coordinates by
\[
M(z)=\frac{az+b}{cz+d},\qquad a,b,c,d\in\mathbb C,\quad ad-bc\ne0.
\]
The expression extends naturally across its pole and $z=\infty$; multiplying all four coefficients by the same nonzero scalar leaves $M$ unchanged. These maps form the group $\operatorname{PGL}_2(\mathbb C)$.
