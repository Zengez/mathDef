% term: Winding number of a plane loop
% related: complex.holomorphic-function, complex.residue
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Winding_number
% status: draft

For a piecewise $C^1$ closed curve $\gamma:[0,1]\to\mathbb C\setminus\{a\}$ with $\gamma(0)=\gamma(1)$, the \emph{winding number} about $a$ is the integer
\[
\operatorname{Ind}(\gamma,a)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{dz}{z-a}.
\]
It counts the total signed change of argument of $\gamma(t)-a$ divided by $2\pi$, and is invariant under homotopies of the loop that avoid $a$.
