For a piecewise $C^1$ closed curve $\gamma$ in $\mathbb C\setminus\{a\}$, its \emph{winding number} about $a$ is
\[
\operatorname{Ind}(\gamma,a)=\frac{1}{2\pi i}\int_\gamma\frac{dz}{z-a},
\]
an integer measuring signed revolutions around $a$.
