% term: Conformal radius
% related: complex.simply-connected-domain, scv.biholomorphic-map
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conformal_radius
% status: draft

For a simply connected proper domain $U\subsetneq\mathbb C$ and $a\in U$, choose the unique biholomorphism $\phi:U\to\mathbb D$ with $\phi(a)=0$ and $\phi'(a)>0$. The \emph{conformal radius} of $U$ at $a$ is $\operatorname{rad}(U,a)=1/\phi'(a)$.
