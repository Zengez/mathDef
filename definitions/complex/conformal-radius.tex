For a proper simply connected domain $U\subsetneq\mathbb C$ and $a\in U$, the Riemann mapping theorem gives a unique biholomorphism $\phi:U\to\mathbb D$ with $\phi(a)=0$ and $\phi'(a)>0$. The \emph{conformal radius} at $a$ is $\operatorname{rad}(U,a)=1/\phi'(a)>0$. More generally, it transforms under a conformal equivalence $g:U\to V$ by $\operatorname{rad}(V,g(a))=|g'(a)|\operatorname{rad}(U,a)$.
