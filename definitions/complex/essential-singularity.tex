Let $f$ be holomorphic on a punctured disk $0<|z-a|<r$. The point $a$ is an \emph{essential singularity} of $f$ if the Laurent expansion about $a$ has infinitely many nonzero terms with negative exponent. Equivalently, $a$ is an isolated singularity that is neither removable nor a pole. Essentiality refers to the punctured-neighborhood germ, not to a value artificially assigned at $a$.
