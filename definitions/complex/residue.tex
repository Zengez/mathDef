If $f$ is holomorphic on a punctured neighborhood of $a$ and has Laurent expansion $f(z)=\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_n(z-a)^n$, its \emph{residue at $a$} is $\operatorname{Res}_{z=a}f=c_{-1}$. For any sufficiently small positively oriented circle $\gamma$ about $a$ in this punctured neighborhood,
\[
\operatorname{Res}_{z=a}f=\frac1{2\pi i}\int_\gamma f(z)\,dz.
\]
The residue is defined even at a removable or essential isolated singularity.
