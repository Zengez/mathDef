An \emph{entire function} is a function $f:\mathbb C\to\mathbb C$ that is holomorphic at every complex number. Equivalently, its Taylor series about any point has infinite radius of convergence. Every complex polynomial defines an entire function, but an entire function need not be a polynomial.
