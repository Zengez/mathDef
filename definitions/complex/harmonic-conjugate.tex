For a real harmonic function $u$ on a plane domain $U$, a \emph{harmonic conjugate} is a real function $v$ on $U$ for which $u+iv$ is holomorphic. Such $v$ need not exist globally on a multiply connected domain, and is unique up to an additive constant when it exists on connected $U$.
