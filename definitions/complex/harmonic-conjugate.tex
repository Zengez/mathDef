For a real harmonic function $u$ on a plane domain $U$, a \emph{harmonic conjugate} is a real-valued function $v:U\to\mathbb R$ such that $u+iv$ is holomorphic. Locally such $v$ always exists. A global harmonic conjugate exists precisely when the closed one-form $-u_y\,dx+u_x\,dy$ has zero integral over every closed curve in $U$; in particular, existence is guaranteed when $U$ is simply connected. On connected $U$, two conjugates differ by a real constant.
