For a sequence $(a_j)\subset\mathbb D$ satisfying the Blaschke condition, define $b_a(z)=\frac{|a|}{a}\frac{a-z}{1-\bar a z}$ when $a\ne0$ and $b_0(z)=z$. A \emph{Blaschke product} is $B(z)=\eta\prod_j b_{a_j}(z)$ for $|\eta|=1$, with the empty product allowed.
