Let $(a_j)\subset\mathbb D$ satisfy the Blaschke condition. For $a\ne0$ define $b_a(z)=\dfrac{|a|}{a}\dfrac{a-z}{1-\overline a z}$, and put $b_0(z)=z$. A \emph{Blaschke product} is
\[
B(z)=\eta\prod_j b_{a_j}(z),\qquad |\eta|=1,
\]
with the empty product allowed. The product converges uniformly on compact subsets of $\mathbb D$, is holomorphic, and has precisely the prescribed zeros with their multiplicities.
