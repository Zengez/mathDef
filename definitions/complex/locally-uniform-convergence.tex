% id: complex.locally-uniform-convergence
% term: Locally uniform convergence of functions
% related: real.uniform-convergence, topology.compactness
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_convergence
% status: draft

A sequence of functions $f_n:U\to\mathbb C$ \emph{converges locally uniformly} to $f$ if for every compact $K\subset U$ and every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually $\sup_{z\in K}|f_n(z)-f(z)|<\varepsilon$.
