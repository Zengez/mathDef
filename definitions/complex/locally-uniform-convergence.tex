% term: Locally uniform convergence of functions
% related: real.uniform-convergence, topology.compactness

% status: draft

Let $f_n,f:U\to\mathbb C$, where $U$ is an open subset of $\mathbb C$. We say that $f_n$ \emph{converges locally uniformly} to $f$ if for every compact set $K\subset U$ and every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $N$ such that
\[
\sup_{z\in K}|f_n(z)-f(z)|<\varepsilon\qquad(n\ge N).
\]
Equivalently, the convergence is uniform on a neighborhood of every point of $U$; this is also called uniform convergence on compact subsets.
