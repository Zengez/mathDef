% status: draft

Let $a\in\mathbb C$ and let $f$ be holomorphic on some punctured disk $0<|z-a|<r$. The puncture $a$ is an \emph{isolated singularity} of this holomorphic function element, classified by the Laurent series of $f$ at $a$ as removable, a pole, or essential. A removable singularity may admit a holomorphic extension across $a$; being \emph{isolated} concerns the absence of other singular points sufficiently near $a$, not the impossibility of extension.
