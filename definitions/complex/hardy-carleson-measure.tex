A positive Borel measure $\mu$ on the unit disk $\mathbb D$ is an \emph{$H^2$-Carleson measure} if there is $C<\infty$ such that
\[
\int_{\mathbb D}|f(z)|^2\,d\mu(z)\le C\|f\|_{H^2(\mathbb D)}^2
\]
for every $f\in H^2(\mathbb D)$. Equivalently, the restriction of Hardy functions induces a bounded embedding $H^2(\mathbb D)\hookrightarrow L^2(\mu)$. For positive disk measures, this is characterized by a uniform Carleson-box bound on the measure.
