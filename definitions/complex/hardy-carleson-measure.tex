A positive Borel measure $\mu$ on $\mathbb D$ is an \emph{$H^2$-Carleson measure} if some $C<\infty$ satisfies
\[
\int_{\mathbb D}|f(z)|^2\,d\mu(z)\le C\|f\|_{H^2(\mathbb D)}^2
\]
for every $f\in H^2(\mathbb D)$.
