A finite or countably infinite sequence $(a_j)$ of points in the open unit disk $\mathbb D$, with zeros repeated according to multiplicity, satisfies the \emph{Blaschke condition} if
\[
\sum_j(1-|a_j|)<\infty.
\]
For an infinite sequence this forces its points eventually toward the unit circle and guarantees compact-local convergence of the associated normalized Blaschke product.
