% term: Principal part of a Laurent expansion
% related: complex.laurent-series, complex.pole
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_part
% status: draft

If $f$ is holomorphic on an annulus $r<|z-a|<R$ with Laurent expansion $\sum_{n=-\infty}^{\infty}c_n(z-a)^n$, its \emph{principal part} about $a$ on that annulus is
\[
\sum_{n=-\infty}^{-1}c_n(z-a)^n.
\]
When $r=0$, a zero principal part corresponds to a removable singularity, a finite nonzero principal part to a pole, and infinitely many nonzero negative-power coefficients to an essential singularity. This classification need not hold when $r>0$.
