% id: complex.principal-part
% term: Principal part of a Laurent expansion
% related: complex.laurent-series, complex.pole
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Principal_part
% status: draft

For a function with Laurent expansion $f(z)=\sum_{k=-\infty}^{\infty}a_k(z-z_0)^k$ on a punctured annulus about $z_0$, its \emph{principal part at $z_0$} is $\sum_{k<0}a_k(z-z_0)^k$.
