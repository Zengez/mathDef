For a locally univalent holomorphic function $f$ on a plane domain (so $f'\ne0$), its \emph{Schwarzian derivative} is
\[
S(f)=\frac{f'''}{f'}-\frac32\left(\frac{f''}{f'}\right)^2.
\]
It satisfies $S(M\circ f)=S(f)$ for every Möbius transformation $M$ defined on the relevant image, and the Schwarzian vanishes identically on a connected domain exactly when $f$ is the restriction of a Möbius transformation.
