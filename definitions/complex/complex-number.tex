A \emph{complex number} is an element of the field $\mathbb C=\{x+iy:x,y\in\mathbb R\}$, where $i^2=-1$. It can be identified with the real ordered pair $(x,y)$, with addition componentwise and multiplication $(x,y)(u,v)=(xu-yv,xv+yu)$. The real and imaginary parts are $x$ and $y$, respectively.
