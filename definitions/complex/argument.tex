For a nonzero complex number $z$, an \emph{argument} of $z$ is any real $\theta$ satisfying $z=|z|e^{i\theta}$. All arguments differ by integer multiples of $2\pi$. The \emph{principal argument} is a single-valued choice in a specified interval of length $2\pi$, often $(-\pi,\pi]$. No argument is defined for $z=0$.
