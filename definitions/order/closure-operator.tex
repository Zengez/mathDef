On a partially ordered set $(P,\leq)$, a \emph{closure operator} is a map $c:P\to P$ that is extensive ($x\leq c(x)$), monotone ($x\leq y\implies c(x)\leq c(y)$), and idempotent ($c(c(x))=c(x)$). Its fixed points are called closed elements. Unlike topological Kuratowski closure, a general closure operator on $\mathcal P(X)$ need not preserve finite unions or send $\varnothing$ to $\varnothing$.
