For a field $F$, an \emph{algebraic closure} of $F$ is an extension field $\overline F/F$ such that every element of $\overline F$ is algebraic over $F$ and $\overline F$ is algebraically closed (every nonconstant polynomial in $\overline F[t]$ has a root in $\overline F$). Algebraic closures are unique up to $F$-isomorphism, not canonically unique.
