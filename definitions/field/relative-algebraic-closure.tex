Given an extension of fields $K/k$, the \emph{relative algebraic closure of $k$ in $K$} is the subfield $\{x\in K:x\text{ is algebraic over }k\}$. It need not be an algebraically closed field; in particular this differs from an algebraic closure of $k$.
