% term: Stationary subset of a cardinal

For an uncountable regular cardinal $\kappa$, a subset $S\subseteq\kappa$ is \emph{stationary} if $S\cap C\ne\varnothing$ for every closed unbounded subset $C\subseteq\kappa$. In particular, every stationary subset is unbounded. This order-theoretic sense of stationary should not be confused with stationary stochastic processes.
