An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{strongly inaccessible} if it is regular and a strong limit cardinal: $2^\lambda<\kappa$ for every cardinal $\lambda<\kappa$. This is stronger than weak inaccessibility (uncountable regular limit cardinal). ZFC does not prove that a strongly inaccessible cardinal exists, assuming ZFC is consistent.
