An infinite cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{singular} if $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)<\kappa$. Equivalently, $\kappa$ is the supremum of an increasing sequence of ordinals indexed by an ordinal smaller than $\kappa$. In ZFC, $\aleph_\omega$ is a singular cardinal with cofinality $\aleph_0$.
