An infinite cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{singular} if $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)<\kappa$, where cofinality is computed for $\kappa$ as its initial ordinal. Equivalently, $\kappa$ is the supremum of a cofinal family of fewer than $\kappa$ ordinals below it. For example, in ZFC $\aleph_\omega$ has cofinality $\aleph_0$, so is singular.
