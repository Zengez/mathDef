% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Disjoint_sets
% status: draft

An indexed family $(A_i)_{i\in I}$ is \emph{pairwise disjoint} if $A_i\cap A_j=\varnothing$ for every two distinct indices $i,j\in I$. This is stronger than requiring the intersection of all members to be empty.
