For an indexed family $(A_i)_{i\in I}$, its \emph{disjoint union} is
\[
\bigsqcup_{i\in I}A_i=\{(i,a):i\in I,\ a\in A_i\}.
\]
Tagging each element by its index keeps distinct summands disjoint even when the original sets overlap. For $I=\varnothing$ the disjoint union is empty.
