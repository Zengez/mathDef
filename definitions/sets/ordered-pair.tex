In the \emph{Kuratowski construction}, the ordered pair of sets $a,b$ is $(a,b)=\{\{a\},\{a,b\}\}$. It satisfies $(a,b)=(c,d)$ if and only if $a=c$ and $b=d$. Other set-theoretic encodings of ordered pairs are possible and yield the same defining equality property.
