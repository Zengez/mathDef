% id: sets.intersection
% term: Intersection of a family of sets
% related: sets.union, sets.set
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Ordinal_number
% status: draft

For a nonempty index set $I$ and family $(A_i)_{i\in I}$, the \emph{intersection} is $\bigcap_{i\in I}A_i=\{x:\forall i\in I,\ x\in A_i\}$.
