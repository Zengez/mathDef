For sets $A,B$, the relation $A\subseteq B$ means that every element of $A$ is an element of $B$:
\[
A\subseteq B\quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad\forall x\,(x\in A\Rightarrow x\in B).
\]
It includes the case $A=B$; a \emph{proper subset} additionally requires $A\ne B$.
