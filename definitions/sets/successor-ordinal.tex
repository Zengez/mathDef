For an ordinal $\alpha$, its \emph{successor ordinal} is $\alpha+1=\alpha\cup\{\alpha\}$, the least ordinal strictly greater than $\alpha$. An ordinal is a successor ordinal if it has this form; $0$ and nonzero limit ordinals are not successors.
