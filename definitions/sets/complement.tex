Given a set $X$ and a subset $A\subseteq X$, the \emph{relative complement} of $A$ in $X$ is $X\setminus A=\{x\in X:x\notin A\}$. Without a specified ambient universe, the notation $A^c$ is ambiguous. Relative complementation depends on $X$.
