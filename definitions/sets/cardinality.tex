Two sets $A,B$ have the same \emph{cardinality} if a bijection $A\to B$ exists. In ZFC, the \emph{cardinality} $|A|$ may be represented by the least ordinal equinumerous with $A$ (its initial ordinal). Thus $|A|=|B|$ exactly when $A$ and $B$ are equinumerous; this ordinal representation uses the well-ordering theorem.
