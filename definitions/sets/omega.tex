The \emph{first infinite ordinal} $\omega$ is the set of all finite von Neumann ordinals, ordered by membership. It is the least infinite ordinal and has order type $0<1<2<\cdots$. Its cardinality is $\aleph_0$; as ordinals, $\omega$ and $\omega+1$ are different although their cardinalities coincide.
