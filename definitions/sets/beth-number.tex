The \emph{beth numbers} form a sequence of cardinals indexed by ordinals, defined by
\[
\beth_0=\aleph_0,\qquad \beth_{\alpha+1}=2^{\beth_\alpha},\qquad \beth_\lambda=\sup_{\alpha<\lambda}\beth_\alpha
\]
for each nonzero limit ordinal $\lambda$. In particular $\beth_1=|\mathbb R|$. The generalized continuum hypothesis is equivalent in ZFC to $\beth_\alpha=\aleph_\alpha$ for all ordinals $\alpha$.
