The \emph{beth numbers} $(\beth_\alpha)$ are cardinals defined by transfinite recursion: $\beth_0=\aleph_0$, $\beth_{\alpha+1}=2^{\beth_\alpha}$, and for every nonzero limit ordinal $\lambda$,
\[
\beth_\lambda=\sup_{\alpha<\lambda}\beth_\alpha.
\]
Thus $\beth_1=|\mathbb R|$. The generalized continuum hypothesis is equivalent in ZFC to $\beth_\alpha=\aleph_\alpha$ for every ordinal $\alpha$.
