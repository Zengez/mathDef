An \emph{ultrafilter on a set $X$} is a maximal proper filter $\mathcal U\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$. Equivalently, for every $A\subseteq X$ exactly one of $A$ and $X\setminus A$ lies in $\mathcal U$. It is \emph{principal} at $x\in X$ when $\mathcal U=\{A\subseteq X:x\in A\}$, and \emph{nonprincipal} otherwise. On an infinite set, the existence of nonprincipal ultrafilters generally uses a choice principle.
