For sets $A$ and $B$, their \emph{Cartesian product} is $A\times B=\{(a,b):a\in A,\ b\in B\}$, where $(a,b)$ is an ordered pair. The product is empty when either factor is empty. An indexed product consists of functions selecting one element from each factor.
