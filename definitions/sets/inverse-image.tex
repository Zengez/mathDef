% id: sets.inverse-image
% term: Inverse image of a subset
% related: sets.relation-image, functions.function
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Ordinal_number
% status: draft

For $f:X\to Y$ and $B\subseteq Y$, the \emph{inverse image} is $f^{-1}[B]=\{x\in X:f(x)\in B\}$, defined whether or not $f$ is invertible.
