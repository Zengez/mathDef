% term: Cofinality of an ordinal
% related: sets.limit-ordinal, sets.ordinal

% status: draft

The \emph{cofinality} $\operatorname{cf}(\alpha)$ of a limit ordinal $\alpha$ is the least order type of a strictly increasing unbounded subset of $\alpha$.
