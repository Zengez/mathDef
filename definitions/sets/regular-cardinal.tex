In the standard ZFC setting, an infinite cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{regular} if its cofinality as an initial ordinal is $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\kappa$. Thus no subset of $\kappa$ of cardinality less than $\kappa$ is unbounded in $\kappa$. The cardinal $\aleph_0$ and every infinite successor cardinal are regular in ZFC.
