An infinite cardinal $\kappa$, identified with its initial ordinal, is \emph{regular} if $\operatorname{cf}(\kappa)=\kappa$. Equivalently, $\kappa$ cannot be expressed as the supremum of fewer than $\kappa$ smaller ordinals. In ZFC, $\aleph_0$ and each infinite successor cardinal are regular; a singular cardinal has cofinality strictly below itself.
