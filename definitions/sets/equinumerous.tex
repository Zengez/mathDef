% term: Equinumerous sets
% related: functions.bijection, sets.cardinality
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equinumerosity
% status: draft

Sets $A$ and $B$ are \emph{equinumerous} if there exists a bijection $f:A\to B$. This defines an equivalence relation on sets and is the basis for comparing their cardinalities; it does not require the sets to be ordered.
