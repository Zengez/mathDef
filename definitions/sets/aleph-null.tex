The \emph{aleph-null cardinal} $\aleph_0$ is the cardinality of the set of natural numbers, or equivalently of the first infinite ordinal $\omega$. A set has cardinality $\aleph_0$ precisely when it admits a bijection with $\mathbb N$. It is the least infinite cardinal.
