For an indexed family of sets $(A_i)_{i\in I}$, its \emph{union} is
\[
\bigcup_{i\in I}A_i=\{x:\exists i\in I,\ x\in A_i\}.
\]
It contains exactly the elements occurring in at least one member of the family. By convention the union of an empty family is $\varnothing$.
