% term: Generic filter for forcing
% related: sets.forcing-poset, sets.filter-on-set
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generic_filter
% status: draft

Let $M$ be a transitive model of a suitable fragment of set theory and let $(\mathbb P,\le)\in M$ be a forcing poset with smaller conditions stronger. A subset $G\subseteq\mathbb P$ is an \emph{$M$-generic filter} if it is nonempty, upward closed toward weaker conditions, directed toward common stronger conditions, and meets every dense subset $D\subseteq\mathbb P$ that belongs to $M$. Here dense means every condition has a stronger condition in $D$.
