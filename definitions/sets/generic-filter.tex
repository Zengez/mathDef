% term: Generic filter for forcing
% related: sets.forcing-poset, sets.filter-on-set
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Generic_filter
% status: draft

Let $M$ be a transitive model of a suitable fragment of set theory and let the forcing poset $(\mathbb P,\le)$ belong to $M$, with smaller conditions stronger. An \emph{$M$-generic filter} is a nonempty $G\subseteq\mathbb P$ that is upward closed toward weaker conditions, is downward directed toward common stronger conditions, and meets every dense subset $D\subseteq\mathbb P$ with $D\in M$. Here $D$ is dense if every condition has a stronger extension belonging to $D$.
