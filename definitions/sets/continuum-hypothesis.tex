The \emph{continuum hypothesis} (CH) asserts that there is no cardinality strictly between that of $\mathbb N$ and that of $\mathbb R$. In ZFC it is equivalently the equation $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1$. Gödel and Cohen established that, assuming ZFC is consistent, neither CH nor its negation is provable from ZFC.
