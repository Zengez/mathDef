The \emph{continuum hypothesis} (CH) asserts that no set has cardinality strictly between that of $\mathbb N$ and that of $\mathbb R$. In ZFC it is equivalently the equality $2^{\aleph_0}=\aleph_1$. Gödel and Cohen showed the relative independence of CH from ZFC: assuming ZFC is consistent, neither CH nor its negation follows from ZFC.
