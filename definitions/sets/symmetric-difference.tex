For sets $A,B$, their \emph{symmetric difference} is
\[
A\triangle B=(A\setminus B)\cup(B\setminus A).
\]
It consists of elements belonging to exactly one of $A$ and $B$. Symmetric difference is commutative and associative, with identity $\varnothing$.
