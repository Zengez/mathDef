A \emph{proper filter} on a nonempty set $X$ is a nonempty family $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ satisfying $\varnothing\notin\mathcal F$, closure under finite intersections, and upward closure: $A\in\mathcal F$ and $A\subseteq B\subseteq X$ imply $B\in\mathcal F$. Necessarily $X\in\mathcal F$. For forcing posets, a related order-theoretic filter is required to be directed toward stronger conditions; the two uses are compatible but have different underlying orders.
