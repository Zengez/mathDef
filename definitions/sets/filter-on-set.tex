Let $X$ be a nonempty set. A \emph{proper filter on $X$} is a nonempty family $\mathcal F\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ such that $\varnothing\notin\mathcal F$, $A,B\in\mathcal F$ implies $A\cap B\in\mathcal F$, and $A\in\mathcal F$, $A\subseteq B\subseteq X$ implies $B\in\mathcal F$. Consequently $X\in\mathcal F$. Filters on arbitrary partially ordered sets have a related order-theoretic definition.
