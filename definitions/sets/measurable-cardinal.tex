An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{measurable} if there exists a nonprincipal $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter on $\kappa$. Here $\kappa$-completeness means that the intersection of every collection of fewer than $\kappa$ members of the ultrafilter is again a member. The ultrafilter can equivalently be viewed as a nontrivial $\{0,1\}$-valued countably additive measure with stronger completeness. In ZFC every measurable cardinal is strongly inaccessible.
