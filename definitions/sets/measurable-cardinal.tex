An uncountable cardinal $\kappa$ is \emph{measurable} if there exists a nonprincipal $\kappa$-complete ultrafilter on the set $\kappa$. Here $\kappa$-complete means that the intersection of any family of fewer than $\kappa$ members of the ultrafilter is also a member. In ZFC, every measurable cardinal is strongly inaccessible.
