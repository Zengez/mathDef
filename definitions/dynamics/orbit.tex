% status: draft

For a self-map $f:X\to X$ and $x\in X$, the \emph{forward orbit} is the set $\mathcal O^+(x)=\{f^n(x):n\in\mathbb N_0\}$, where $f^0(x)=x$. The ordered sequence $(f^n(x))_{n\ge0}$ is the corresponding forward trajectory. For a noninvertible map, a backward orbit need not exist or be unique.
