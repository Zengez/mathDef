% term: Recurrent point
% related: dynamics.orbit, topology.neighborhood
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Recurrent_point
% status: draft

Let $f:X\to X$ be a continuous self-map of a topological space. A point $x\in X$ is \emph{positively recurrent} if every neighborhood $U$ of $x$ contains $f^n(x)$ for arbitrarily large integers $n>0$, equivalently $x\in\omega(x)$ with $\omega(x)=\bigcap_{N\ge0}\overline{\{f^n(x):n\ge N\}}$. Here \emph{recurrent} means positive (Poisson) recurrence; the stronger Birkhoff/uniform-recurrence convention is distinguished in some references.
