Let $f:X\to X$ be a continuous map of a metric space $(X,d)$. An \emph{$\varepsilon$-chain} from $x$ to $y$ is a finite sequence $x=x_0,x_1,\ldots,x_n=y$ with $n\ge1$ and $d(f(x_i),x_{i+1})<\varepsilon$ for $0\le i<n$. A point $x$ is \emph{chain recurrent} if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is such an $\varepsilon$-chain from $x$ back to itself. For flows, a related definition also specifies a minimum duration between jumps.
