A \emph{discrete-time dynamical system} consists of a state space $X$ and a self-map $f:X\to X$, with evolution $f^0=\operatorname{id}_X$ and $f^{n+1}=f\circ f^n$ for $n\ge0$. In topological dynamics $X$ is a topological space and $f$ is usually assumed continuous; invertibility is not required for forward-time dynamics.
