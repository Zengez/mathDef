The \emph{phase space} (or state space) of a dynamical system is the space $X$ of its possible states, on which the evolution maps act. In smooth dynamical systems it is often a manifold; in general it may instead be a metric, topological, measurable, or other structured space. A point of $X$ specifies a state, not necessarily a physical position.
