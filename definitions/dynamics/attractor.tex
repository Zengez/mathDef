Let $f:X\to X$ be continuous on a metric space $(X,d)$. Under one common convention, an \emph{attractor} is a nonempty compact invariant set $A$ ($f(A)=A$) with a neighborhood $U$ such that $\operatorname{dist}(f^n(x),A)\to0$ for every $x\in U$. Other conventions additionally require Lyapunov stability, uniform attraction, or a trapping neighborhood; these notions should not be conflated.
