Let $f:X\to X$ be a continuous self-map of a compact metric space and let $x\in X$. The \emph{$\omega$-limit set} of $x$ is
\[
\omega(x)=\bigcap_{N\ge0}\overline{\{f^n(x):n\ge N\}}.
\]
Equivalently, it consists of all limits of subsequences $f^{n_k}(x)$ with $n_k\to\infty$. Compactness makes this set nonempty; for a general noncompact space it can be empty.
