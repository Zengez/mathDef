% term: Unstable manifold of a hyperbolic fixed point

For a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $f:M\to M$ and a hyperbolic fixed point $p$, the \emph{global unstable set} is $W^u(p)=\{x\in M:d(f^{-n}(x),p)\to0\text{ as }n\to\infty\}$. It is the stable set for $f^{-1}$ and has an immersed-manifold structure with $T_pW^u(p)$ equal to the sum of generalized eigenspaces of $D f_p$ with eigenvalue modulus greater than $1$. Invertibility is needed for this backward-orbit formulation.
