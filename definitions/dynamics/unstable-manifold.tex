% term: Unstable manifold of a hyperbolic fixed point
% related: dynamics.hyperbolic-fixed-point, dynamics.stable-manifold
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_manifold
% status: draft

For a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $f:M\to M$ and a hyperbolic fixed point $p$, the \emph{global unstable set} is
\[
W^u(p)=\{x\in M:d(f^{-n}(x),p)\to0\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.
\]
It is the stable set of the inverse diffeomorphism $f^{-1}$. The stable-manifold theorem makes it an injectively immersed submanifold whose tangent space at $p$ is the generalized eigenspace sum for eigenvalues of $Df_p$ of modulus greater than $1$.
