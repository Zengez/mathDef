A continuous map $f:X\to X$ is \emph{topologically transitive} in the positive-time open-set convention if for any two nonempty open sets $U,V\subseteq X$ there is an $n\ge1$ such that $f^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$. This does not generally imply the existence of a dense forward orbit without additional topological assumptions. It is weaker than topological mixing.
