A continuous self-map $f:X\to X$ of a topological space is \emph{topologically transitive} if, for every pair of nonempty open sets $U,V\subseteq X$, some integer $n\ge1$ satisfies $f^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$. Under additional hypotheses on $X$, this is equivalent to the existence of a dense forward orbit; the equivalence is not part of the general definition.
