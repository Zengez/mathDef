Let $f:M\to M$ be a $C^1$ diffeomorphism of a Riemannian manifold, $x\in M$, and $0\ne v\in T_xM$. If it exists, the \emph{Lyapunov exponent} in direction $v$ is
\[
\lambda(x,v)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log\frac{\|D(f^n)_x v\|}{\|v\|}.
\]
This is the asymptotic exponential rate of change of the tangent vector; existence of the limit is an additional condition, not automatic for arbitrary orbits.
