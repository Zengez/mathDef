Let $f:M\to M$ be a $C^1$ diffeomorphism of a Riemannian manifold and $0\ne v\in T_xM$. When it exists, the \emph{Lyapunov exponent in direction $v$} is
\[
\lambda(x,v)=\lim_{n\to\infty}\frac1n\log\frac{\|D(f^n)_xv\|}{\|v\|}.
\]
The limit need not exist for an arbitrary orbit; one can instead use upper and lower exponents defined with $\limsup$ and $\liminf$. Measurable-cocycle results give existence under further integrability and invariant-measure assumptions.
