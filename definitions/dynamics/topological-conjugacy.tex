% status: draft

Continuous self-maps $f:X\to X$ and $g:Y\to Y$ of topological spaces are \emph{topologically conjugate} if there exists a homeomorphism $h:X\to Y$ with $h\circ f=g\circ h$. Thus $h(f^n(x))=g^n(h(x))$ for every $n\ge0$, so conjugacy identifies their forward dynamics without requiring the maps themselves to be invertible.
