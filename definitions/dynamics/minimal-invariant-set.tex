% status: draft

Let $f:X\to X$ be continuous on a compact metric space. A \emph{minimal invariant set} is a nonempty closed set $M\subseteq X$ with $f(M)=M$ such that no proper nonempty closed subset $A\subsetneq M$ satisfies $f(A)\subseteq A$. Equivalently, the forward orbit of every $x\in M$ is dense in $M$. This notion should not be confused with a minimal element of a partially ordered set.
