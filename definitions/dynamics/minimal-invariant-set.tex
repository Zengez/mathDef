% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Minimal_set
% status: draft

For a continuous self-map $f:X\to X$ of a compact metric space, a \emph{minimal invariant set} is a nonempty closed subset $M\subseteq X$ with $f(M)=M$ such that no proper nonempty closed $A\subsetneq M$ satisfies $f(A)\subseteq A$. Equivalently, every forward orbit in $M$ is dense in $M$. The minimality concerns closed forward-invariant subsets, not points in an arbitrary order.
