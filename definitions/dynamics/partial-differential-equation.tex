A \emph{partial differential equation} (PDE) is a relation involving an unknown function $u$ of several independent variables and some of its partial derivatives. For example, the Laplace equation $\sum_{j=1}^{n}\partial_{x_j}^2u=0$ on a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb R^n$ is a second-order PDE. The function space and interpretation of derivatives must be specified when weak solutions are allowed.
