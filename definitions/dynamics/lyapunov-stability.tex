% status: draft

Let $f:X\to X$ be a map of a metric space $(X,d)$, and let $p$ be a fixed point. The point $p$ is \emph{Lyapunov stable} (for forward iterates) if, for every $\varepsilon>0$, there exists $\delta>0$ such that $d(x,p)<\delta$ implies $d(f^n(x),p)<\varepsilon$ for every integer $n\ge0$. Stability alone does not require $f^n(x)\to p$.
