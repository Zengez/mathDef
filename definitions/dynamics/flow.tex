A \emph{flow} on $X$ is a family $\varphi_t:X\to X$, for $t\in\mathbb R$, satisfying $\varphi_0=\operatorname{id}$ and $\varphi_{s+t}=\varphi_s\circ\varphi_t$, with appropriate continuity or smoothness assumptions.
