Let $f:X\to X$ be a homeomorphism of a compact metric space and $x\in X$. Its \emph{$\alpha$-limit set} is
\[
\alpha(x)=\bigcap_{N\ge0}\overline{\{f^{-n}(x):n\ge N\}},
\]
the set of subsequential limits of its backward orbit as $n\to\infty$. For a noninvertible self-map, backward trajectories must be specified and need not be unique.
