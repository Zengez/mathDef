For a homeomorphism $f:X\to X$ of a compact metric space, the \emph{$\alpha$-limit set} of $x\in X$ is
\[
\alpha_f(x)=\bigcap_{N\ge0}\overline{\{f^{-n}(x):n\ge N\}},
\]
the set of subsequential limits of its backward orbit. Compactness makes it nonempty. For a noninvertible map, backward-orbit choices must be specified, and the resulting limit sets need not be unique.
