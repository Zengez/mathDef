% id: dynamics.equilibrium
% term: Equilibrium of an ODE
% related: dynamics.autonomous-ode, dynamics.flow
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Equilibrium_point
% status: draft

For an autonomous ODE $\dot x=F(x)$ with $F:U\to\mathbb R^m$, an \emph{equilibrium} is a point $p\in U$ satisfying $F(p)=0$. The constant curve $x(t)=p$ is then a solution; when uniqueness holds, it is the trajectory through $p$.
