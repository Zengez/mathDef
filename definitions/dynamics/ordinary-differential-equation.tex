An \emph{ordinary differential equation} (ODE) is a relation involving an unknown function $x(t)$ of one independent variable and its ordinary derivatives, often written $F(t,x(t),x'(t),\ldots,x^{(k)}(t))=0$. A solution has enough differentiability to satisfy the relation throughout a specified interval.
