Let $f:M\to M$ be a $C^1$ self-map of a finite-dimensional smooth manifold and let $p$ be a fixed point. The point $p$ is \emph{hyperbolic} for the discrete-time map if the derivative $D f_p:T_pM\to T_pM$ has no complex eigenvalue of modulus $1$. For equilibria of autonomous flows, the corresponding condition excludes eigenvalues with zero real part for the linearized vector field.
