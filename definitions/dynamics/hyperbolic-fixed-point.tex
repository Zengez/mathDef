% status: draft

Let $f:M\to M$ be a $C^1$ self-map of a finite-dimensional smooth manifold and $p$ a fixed point. It is \emph{hyperbolic} for the discrete-time map if $Df_p$ has no complex eigenvalue on the unit circle. For an equilibrium $p$ of a $C^1$ autonomous vector field, the corresponding flow condition is that the linearized vector field have no eigenvalues with zero real part. Invertibility of $Df_p$ is not required for the map definition unless $f$ is assumed a diffeomorphism.
