% term: Nonwandering point
% related: dynamics.recurrent-point, dynamics.orbit
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Wandering_point
% status: draft

For a continuous map $f:X\to X$ of a topological space, a point $x\in X$ is \emph{nonwandering} if for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$ and every integer $N\ge1$, there exists $n\ge N$ such that $f^n(U)\cap U\ne\varnothing$. The return point can depend on $n$ and need not be $x$ itself; nonwandering is generally weaker than positive recurrence.
