% id: dynamics.nonwandering-point
% term: Nonwandering point
% related: dynamics.recurrent-point, dynamics.orbit
% source: https://planetmath.org/nonwanderingset
% status: draft

For a continuous self-map $f:X\to X$ of a topological space, a point $x\in X$ is \emph{nonwandering} if for every neighborhood $U$ of $x$ and every integer $N\ge1$ there exists $n\ge N$ with $f^n(U)\cap U\ne\varnothing$. The returning point may vary with $n$ and need not equal $x$ itself. Recurrence of the point implies nonwandering behavior, but the converse can fail.
