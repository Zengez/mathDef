An \emph{autonomous ordinary differential equation} on an open set $U\subseteq\mathbb R^m$ has the form $\dot x(t)=F(x(t))$, where $F:U\to\mathbb R^m$ has no explicit dependence on the independent variable $t$. A solution is a differentiable curve in $U$ satisfying the equation on its time interval.
