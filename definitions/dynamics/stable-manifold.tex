% term: Stable manifold of a hyperbolic fixed point
% related: dynamics.hyperbolic-fixed-point, dynamics.unstable-manifold

% status: draft

For a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $f:M\to M$ of a finite-dimensional smooth manifold and a hyperbolic fixed point $p$, the \emph{global stable set} is
\[
W^s(p)=\{x\in M:d(f^n(x),p)\to0\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.
\]
The stable-manifold theorem gives it an injectively immersed submanifold structure, with tangent space at $p$ equal to the direct sum of generalized eigenspaces of $Df_p$ for eigenvalues of modulus less than $1$. The local stable manifold is embedded near $p$.
