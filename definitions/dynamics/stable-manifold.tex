% term: Stable manifold of a hyperbolic fixed point
% related: dynamics.hyperbolic-fixed-point, dynamics.unstable-manifold
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stable_manifold
% status: draft

For a $C^1$ diffeomorphism $f:M\to M$ of a finite-dimensional manifold and a hyperbolic fixed point $p$, the \emph{global stable set} is $W^s(p)=\{x\in M:d(f^n(x),p)\to0\text{ as }n\to\infty\}$, using any compatible Riemannian distance. The stable-manifold theorem endows it with an immersed-manifold structure, with $T_pW^s(p)$ the sum of generalized eigenspaces of $D f_p$ for eigenvalues of modulus less than $1$. Local stable manifolds are embedded near $p$.
