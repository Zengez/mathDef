A continuous map $f:X\to X$ is \emph{topologically mixing} if for each pair of nonempty open subsets $U,V\subseteq X$ there exists $N\ge1$ such that
\[
f^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing\qquad\text{for every }n\ge N.
\]
This is stronger than topological transitivity and requires no invariant measure. It is distinct from measure-theoretic strong mixing.
