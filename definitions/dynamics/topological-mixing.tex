A continuous self-map $f:X\to X$ is \emph{topologically mixing} if for every pair of nonempty open sets $U,V\subseteq X$ there exists $N\ge1$ such that $f^n(U)\cap V\ne\varnothing$ for every $n\ge N$. This implies topological transitivity and uses no invariant measure; measure-theoretic mixing is a separate notion.
