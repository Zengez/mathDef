A \emph{recurrence relation} is an equation relating terms of an indexed sequence, often by expressing $a_n$ in terms of earlier terms such as $a_n=F(n,a_{n-1},\ldots,a_{n-k})$. When the relation explicitly determines each next term and sufficient initial values are supplied, it specifies a unique sequence. More general implicit recurrences may have several solutions.
