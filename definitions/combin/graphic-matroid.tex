For a finite undirected graph $G=(V,E)$, its \emph{graphic matroid} (cycle matroid) has ground set $E$ and independent subsets $F\subseteq E$ precisely when $(V,F)$ contains no cycle, i.e. is a forest. Its circuits are the inclusion-minimal edge sets of cycles. This construction also extends to multigraphs when loops and parallel edges are handled as matroid circuits.
