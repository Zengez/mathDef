An \emph{integer partition} of $n\ge1$ is a finite multiset of positive integers whose sum is $n$, with the order of the summands disregarded. Equivalently, it is a weakly decreasing finite sequence of positive integers summing to $n$. The empty partition is conventionally the unique partition of $0$.
