For a finite matroid $M=(E,\mathcal I)$, its \emph{rank function} is
\[
r_M(A)=\max\{|I|:I\subseteq A,\ I\in\mathcal I\},\qquad A\subseteq E.
\]
In particular, $r_M(\varnothing)=0$, $0\le r_M(A)\le|A|$, and the rank function is monotone and submodular. Rank is defined on every subset of the ground set.
