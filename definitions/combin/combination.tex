A \emph{$k$-combination} of a finite set $S$ is a subset $A\subseteq S$ with $|A|=k$; order does not matter and elements are not repeated. When $|S|=n$, there are $\binom nk$ such subsets for $0\le k\le n$, including the empty combination when $k=0$.
