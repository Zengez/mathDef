For integers $n\ge0$ and $0\le k\le n$, the \emph{binomial coefficient} is
\[
\binom nk=\frac{n!}{k!(n-k)!}.
\]
It counts the $k$-element subsets of an $n$-element set. The common extension sets $\binom nk=0$ when $k<0$ or $k>n$.
