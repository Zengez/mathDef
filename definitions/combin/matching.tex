A \emph{matching} in an undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ is a subset $M\subseteq E$ such that no two distinct edges of $M$ have a common endpoint. A vertex is \emph{matched} if it lies on an edge of $M$. A \emph{maximal} matching cannot be enlarged by adding an edge, while a \emph{maximum} matching has greatest possible cardinality.
