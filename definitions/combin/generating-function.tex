For a sequence $(a_n)_{n\ge0}$ over a commutative ring $R$, its \emph{ordinary generating function} is the formal power series
\[
A(x)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nx^n\in R[[x]].
\]
No analytic convergence is needed when the series is treated formally. Exponential generating functions instead divide the coefficient of $x^n$ by $n!$ (over a suitable coefficient ring).
