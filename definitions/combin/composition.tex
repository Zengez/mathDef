An \emph{integer composition} of $n\ge1$ is an ordered tuple $(a_1,\ldots,a_k)$ of positive integers with $a_1+\cdots+a_k=n$. Different orders are different compositions, in contrast to integer partitions. The empty tuple is often admitted as the unique composition of $0$.
