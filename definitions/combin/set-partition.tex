A \emph{partition} of a set $S$ is a family $\mathcal P$ of nonempty subsets of $S$ that are pairwise disjoint and whose union is $S$. Its members are called \emph{blocks}. Under this convention, the empty family is the unique partition of the empty set; no ordering of blocks is included.
