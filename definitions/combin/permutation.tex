A \emph{permutation} of a set $S$ is a bijection $\sigma:S\to S$. Permutations form the symmetric group on $S$ under composition. If $S$ has $n\ge0$ elements, there are exactly $n!$ permutations; for $n=0$, the unique empty bijection is counted.
