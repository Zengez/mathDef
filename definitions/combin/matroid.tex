A \emph{finite matroid} is a pair $(E,\mathcal I)$, where $E$ is finite and $\mathcal I\subseteq\mathcal P(E)$, such that $\varnothing\in\mathcal I$; $I\in\mathcal I$ and $J\subseteq I$ imply $J\in\mathcal I$; and if $I,J\in\mathcal I$ with $|I|<|J|$, some $e\in J\setminus I$ has $I\cup\{e\}\in\mathcal I$.
