A \emph{perfect matching} in an undirected graph $G=(V,E)$ is a matching $M\subseteq E$ in which every vertex of $V$ is incident with exactly one edge of $M$. For finite graphs, this implies that $|V|$ is even and $|M|=|V|/2$; having an even number of vertices alone does not guarantee a perfect matching.
