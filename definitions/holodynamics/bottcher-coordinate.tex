Suppose a holomorphic map $f$ fixes $a$ and has local degree $d\ge2$ there, so $f(z)-a=c(z-a)^d+O((z-a)^{d+1})$ with $c\ne0$. A \emph{Böttcher coordinate} is a local biholomorphic coordinate $\phi$ near $a$, with $\phi(a)=0$, satisfying
\[
\phi(f(z))=(\phi(z))^d
\]
where defined. Its derivative at $a$ obeys $\phi'(a)^{d-1}=c$, and the choice of derivative fixes the normalization up to a suitable root of unity.
