A family $\mathcal F$ of meromorphic functions on a plane domain $U$ is \emph{normal} if each sequence in $\mathcal F$ has a subsequence converging uniformly on compact subsets of $U$ in the spherical metric to a meromorphic function on $U$ or the constant map $\infty$. The use of spherical rather than Euclidean distance accommodates poles.
