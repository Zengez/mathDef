% term: Normal family of meromorphic functions

% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Julia_set
% status: draft

A family $\mathcal F$ of meromorphic functions on a plane domain $U$ is \emph{normal} if each sequence in $\mathcal F$ has a subsequence converging locally uniformly in the spherical metric to a meromorphic function or the constant map $\infty$.
