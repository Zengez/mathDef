For a continuous map $f:X\to X$ on a metric space and an attracting fixed point $a$, its \emph{basin of attraction} is
\[
B(a)=\{x\in X:d(f^n(x),a)\to0\text{ as }n\to\infty\}.
\]
For an attracting periodic orbit $\mathcal O$ of period $p$, its basin consists of points whose forward orbits approach that cycle in the metric: $d(f^n(x),\mathcal O)\to0$. The immediate basin is the component of the basin containing the attracting point or cycle component.
