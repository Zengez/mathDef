For a rational map $R$ of degree at least two, a \emph{Siegel disk} is a periodic, simply connected Fatou component $U$ such that its first-return map $R^p|_U$ is conformally conjugate to an irrational rotation $w\mapsto e^{2\pi i\theta}w$ of the unit disk, with $\theta\notin\mathbb Q$. If the period $p$ exceeds one, $U$ need not itself be invariant under one application of $R$.
