A periodic point $a$ of least period $p$ for a rational map of degree at least two is \emph{parabolic} in the broad complex-dynamical convention when the multiplier $(f^p)'(a)$ is a root of unity. Some texts reserve the term for multiplier exactly $1$ and call the general case rationally indifferent. The return map is not locally the identity for a rational map of degree at least two.
