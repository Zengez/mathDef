% term: Filled Julia set of a polynomial
% related: holodynamics.julia-set, holodynamics.iterate
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Filled_Julia_set
% status: draft

For a complex polynomial $p$ of degree at least two, its \emph{filled Julia set} is
\[
K(p)=\{z\in\mathbb C:\sup_{n\ge0}|p^n(z)|<\infty\}.
\]
It is compact and is the complement in $\mathbb C$ of the escaping set. The ordinary Julia set of $p$ is the topological boundary $\partial K(p)$, not generally the whole of $K(p)$.
