A \emph{wandering Fatou component} is a component $U$ whose forward-image Fatou components $U_n$ are all distinct for different $n\ge0$. For a rational map $R$ one may take $U_n=R^n(U)$. Sullivan's no-wandering-domain theorem states that such components do not exist for rational maps of degree at least two, although they can occur for certain transcendental holomorphic or meromorphic maps.
