For a rational map $R$ of degree at least two, a \emph{Herman ring} is a periodic Fatou component $U$ conformally equivalent to an annulus $A=\{r<|w|<1\}$ with $0<r<1$, such that the first-return map $R^p|_U$ is analytically conjugate to an irrational rotation $w\mapsto e^{2\pi i\theta}w$ of $A$, where $\theta\notin\mathbb Q$.
