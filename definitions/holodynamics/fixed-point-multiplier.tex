% id: holodynamics.fixed-point-multiplier
% term: Multiplier of a periodic point
% related: dynamics.periodic-point, holodynamics.attracting-periodic-point
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Julia_set
% status: draft

For a holomorphic self-map $f$ of a Riemann surface and a point $a$ of least period $p$, its \emph{multiplier} is $\lambda=(f^p)'(a)$ in a local holomorphic coordinate. The value is invariant under changes of coordinate because $f^p(a)=a$, and is constant along the periodic cycle, including the case $\lambda=0$.
