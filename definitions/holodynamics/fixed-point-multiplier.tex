% id: holodynamics.fixed-point-multiplier
% term: Multiplier of a periodic point
% related: dynamics.periodic-point, holodynamics.attracting-periodic-point
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Julia_set
% status: draft

If $z$ has period $p$ under a holomorphic map $f$ and its orbit avoids poles in local charts, the \emph{multiplier} is $\lambda=(f^p)'(z)$, interpreted as the local derivative in a chart.
