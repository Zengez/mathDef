A point $a$ of least period $p$ under a holomorphic map $f$ is \emph{superattracting} if its multiplier $(f^p)'(a)=0$. Equivalently the first-return map has local degree at least two at $a$. For a periodic cycle this occurs if at least one point on the cycle is a critical point of $f$.
