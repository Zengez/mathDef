% id: holodynamics.critical-point
% term: Critical point of a holomorphic map
% related: complex.holomorphic-function, holodynamics.critical-value
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/Julia_set
% status: draft

For a nonconstant holomorphic map $f$ between Riemann surfaces, a point $z$ is \emph{critical} if its derivative vanishes in local holomorphic coordinates.
