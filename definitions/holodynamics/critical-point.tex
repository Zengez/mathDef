% id: holodynamics.critical-point
% term: Critical point of a holomorphic map
% related: complex.holomorphic-function, holodynamics.critical-value
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Critical_point_(mathematics)
% status: draft

For a nonconstant holomorphic map $f:X\to Y$ of Riemann surfaces, a point $c\in X$ is a \emph{critical point} if the derivative of its local coordinate representation vanishes. Equivalently, the local mapping degree at $c$ is at least two. The definition is invariant under holomorphic coordinate changes, and applies also at poles and at the point $\infty$ when maps are considered on the Riemann sphere.
