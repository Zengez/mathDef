% id: holodynamics.immediate-basin
% term: Immediate attracting basin
% related: holodynamics.basin-attraction, holodynamics.fatou-component
% source: https://link.springer.com/article/10.1365/s13291-025-00300-1
% status: draft

For an attracting fixed point $a$ of a holomorphic map $f$, the \emph{immediate basin of attraction} is the connected component containing $a$ of its basin $B(a)=\{z:f^n(z)\to a\}$. The full basin may have additional components. For an attracting periodic cycle the immediate basin components containing the cycle points are permuted by the map.
