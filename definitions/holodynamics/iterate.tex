% term: Iterate of a map
% related: dynamics.discrete-dynamical-system, dynamics.orbit
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Iterated_function
% status: draft

For a map $f:X\to X$, its \emph{$n$th iterate} for $n\in\mathbb N_0$ is recursively defined by $f^0=\operatorname{id}_X$ and $f^{n+1}=f\circ f^n$. It obeys $f^{m+n}=f^m\circ f^n$. Here $f^n$ denotes functional composition, not the pointwise $n$th power.
