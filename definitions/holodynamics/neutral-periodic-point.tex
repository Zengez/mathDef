A point $a$ of least period $p$ under a holomorphic map is \emph{neutral} or \emph{indifferent} if the multiplier $\lambda=(f^p)'(a)$ has modulus $|\lambda|=1$. It is rationally indifferent if $\lambda$ is a root of unity and irrationally indifferent otherwise. Neutrality alone does not guarantee local analytic linearizability.
