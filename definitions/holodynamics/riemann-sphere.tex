The \emph{Riemann sphere} $\widehat{\mathbb C}=\mathbb C\cup\{\infty\}$ is the one-point compactification of the complex plane equipped with the complex structure given by ordinary coordinate $z$ away from $\infty$ and by $w=1/z$ near $\infty$. It is biholomorphic to the complex projective line $\mathbb{CP}^1$.
