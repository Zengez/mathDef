% term: Periodic Fatou component

% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Classification_of_Fatou_components
% status: draft

A Fatou component $U$ of a rational map $R$ is \emph{periodic} if $R^p(U)=U$ for some $p\ge1$. The least such $p$ is its period, and $R^p|_U$ is the first-return map. Component periodicity does not assert that individual points of $U$ are periodic.
