% term: Degree of a rational map
% related: holodynamics.rational-map, complex.polynomial
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rational_function
% status: draft

For a nonconstant rational map $R:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$ represented by relatively prime polynomials $P,Q\in\mathbb C[z]$ with $Q\ne0$, its \emph{degree} is
\[
\deg R=\max\{\deg P,\deg Q\}.
\]
It equals the number of preimages of a generic point of the Riemann sphere, counted with local multiplicities. Canceling all common polynomial factors is necessary before applying this formula.
