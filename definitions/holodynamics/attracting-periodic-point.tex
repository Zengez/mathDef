% term: Attracting periodic point

Let $f$ be a holomorphic self-map of a Riemann surface, and let $a$ have least period $p\ge1$ under iteration. The point $a$ is \emph{attracting} if the multiplier $\lambda=(f^p)'(a)$ in a local coordinate satisfies $|\lambda|<1$. This criterion is independent of the coordinate and is shared by every point of the periodic cycle. The case $\lambda=0$ is called superattracting.
