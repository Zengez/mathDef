% id: holodynamics.repelling-periodic-point
% term: Repelling periodic point
% related: holodynamics.fixed-point-multiplier, holodynamics.attracting-periodic-point
% source: https://www.matem.unam.mx/~omar/no-wandering-domains.html
% status: draft

For a holomorphic self-map $f$ of a Riemann surface and a point $a$ with least period $p$, the point is \emph{repelling} if the multiplier of its first-return map has $|(f^p)'(a)|>1$. This is independent of holomorphic coordinates. For rational maps of degree at least two, every repelling periodic point belongs to the Julia set.
