A Fatou component $U$ of a rational map $R$ is \emph{preperiodic} if there exists $n\ge0$ such that $R^n(U)$ is a periodic Fatou component. A component that is preperiodic but not itself periodic is \emph{strictly preperiodic}. Sullivan's no-wandering-domain theorem implies that every Fatou component of a rational map of degree at least two is preperiodic.
