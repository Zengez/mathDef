A \emph{rational map on the Riemann sphere} is a nonconstant holomorphic map $R:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$. In an affine coordinate it can be written $R(z)=P(z)/Q(z)$ for relatively prime complex polynomials $P,Q$, with its value extended holomorphically across poles as a sphere-valued map and at $\infty$. Its degree is $\max\{\deg P,\deg Q\}$.
