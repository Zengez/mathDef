For the quadratic family $p_c(z)=z^2+c$, the \emph{Mandelbrot set} is
\[
M=\{c\in\mathbb C:\sup_{n\ge0}|p_c^n(0)|<\infty\}.
\]
The point $0$ is the unique finite critical point of $p_c$; equivalently $c\in M$ precisely when the filled Julia set $K(p_c)$ is connected.
