% id: holodynamics.julia-set
% term: Julia set of a rational map
% related: holodynamics.fatou-set, holodynamics.rational-map
% source: https://www.matem.unam.mx/~omar/no-wandering-domains.html
% status: draft

For a rational map $R:\widehat{\mathbb C}\to\widehat{\mathbb C}$ of degree at least two, its \emph{Julia set} is the complement $J(R)=\widehat{\mathbb C}\setminus F(R)$ of the locus of normality of its iterates. It is nonempty, closed, perfect and completely invariant, and equals the closure of the repelling periodic points.
