% term: Integral element over a ring
% related: commalg.noetherian-ring, algebra.algebraic-element

% status: draft

For an extension $A\subseteq B$ of commutative rings, $b\in B$ is \emph{integral over $A$} if it satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in $A$.
