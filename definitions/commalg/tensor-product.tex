For modules $M,N$ over a commutative ring $R$, the \emph{tensor product} $M\otimes_RN$ is an $R$-module with an $R$-bilinear map $\iota:M\times N\to M\otimes_RN$ such that every $R$-bilinear map $b:M\times N\to P$ factors uniquely as $b=\widetilde b\circ\iota$ for an $R$-linear map $\widetilde b:M\otimes_RN\to P$.
