% term: Localization of a ring
% related: algebra.commutative-ring, commalg.local-ring
% source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/00DY
% status: draft

For a commutative ring $R$ and multiplicatively closed $S\subseteq R$ with $1\in S$, the \emph{localization} $S^{-1}R$ consists of fractions $r/s$ modulo the relation $r/s=r'/s'$ if some $t\in S$ satisfies $t(rs'-r's)=0$.
