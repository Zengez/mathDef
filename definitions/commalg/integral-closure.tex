Let $A\subseteq B$ be commutative rings with identity. The \emph{integral closure of $A$ in $B$} is $\{b\in B:b^n+a_{n-1}b^{n-1}+\cdots+a_0=0\text{ for some }n\geq 1\text{ and }a_i\in A\}$. It is a subring of $B$. When $A$ is an integral domain, its integral closure without a specified ambient ring usually means its integral closure in its field of fractions.
