A sequence $M_1\xrightarrow{f_1}M_2\xrightarrow{f_2}M_3$ of module maps is \emph{exact at $M_2$} if $\operatorname{im}f_1=\ker f_2$. A longer sequence is exact when this holds at each interior term.
