Let $R$ be a commutative ring. An $R$-module $M$ is \emph{flat} if the tensor-product functor
\[
-\otimes_R M:\operatorname{Mod}_R\longrightarrow\operatorname{Mod}_R
\]
is exact: for every exact sequence of $R$-modules $N_1\to N_2\to N_3$, the induced sequence $N_1\otimes_R M\to N_2\otimes_R M\to N_3\otimes_R M$ is exact. Equivalently, tensoring with $M$ preserves injections (its right exactness holds for every module). For noncommutative rings, the corresponding definition distinguishes left and right module structures.
