For a finite field extension $L/K$ and $\alpha\in L$, the \emph{field trace} $\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\alpha)$ is the trace of the $K$-linear endomorphism $m_\alpha:L\to L$ given by $x\mapsto\alpha x$. The trace lies in $K$ and is $K$-linear in $\alpha$; finite separability is not needed for this definition.
