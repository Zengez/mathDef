For a finite field extension $L/K$ and $\alpha\in L$, the \emph{field trace} $\operatorname{Tr}_{L/K}(\alpha)$ is the trace of the $K$-linear map $m_\alpha:L\to L$, $x\mapsto\alpha x$.
