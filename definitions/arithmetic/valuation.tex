A \emph{normalized discrete valuation} on a field $K$ is a surjective map $v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z$ with $v(xy)=v(x)+v(y)$ and $v(x+y)\ge\min\{v(x),v(y)\}$ whenever $x+y\ne0$, extended by $v(0)=+\infty$. Its valuation ring is $\{0\}\cup\{x\in K^\times:v(x)\ge0\}$; surjectivity excludes the trivial valuation.
