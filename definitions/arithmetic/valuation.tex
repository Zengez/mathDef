A \emph{discrete valuation} on a field $K$ is a surjective map $v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z$ with $v(xy)=v(x)+v(y)$ and $v(x+y)\ge\min\{v(x),v(y)\}$ when $x+y\ne0$. Set $v(0)=\infty$.
