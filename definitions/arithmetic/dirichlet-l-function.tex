For a Dirichlet character $\chi$ modulo $q$, its \emph{Dirichlet $L$-function} is initially defined in the half-plane $\Re(s)>1$ by the absolutely convergent Dirichlet series
\[
L(s,\chi)=\sum_{n=1}^{\infty}\frac{\chi(n)}{n^s}.
\]
It has a meromorphic continuation to $\mathbb C$; the continuation is entire for nonprincipal characters, while a principal character gives a pole at $s=1$.
