For a discrete valuation ring $R$ with fraction field $K$ and normalized valuation $v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z$, a \emph{uniformizer} is an element $\pi\in R$ with $v(\pi)=1$. Equivalently, its unique maximal ideal satisfies $\mathfrak m=(\pi)$. Any two uniformizers differ by a unit of $R$.
