The \emph{ring of $p$-adic integers} is $\mathbb Z_p=\{x\in\mathbb Q_p:|x|_p\le1\}$. It is a complete discrete valuation ring with maximal ideal $p\mathbb Z_p$, residue field $\mathbb F_p$, and canonical inverse-limit description $\mathbb Z_p\cong\varprojlim_{n\ge1}\mathbb Z/p^n\mathbb Z$.
