For an integral domain $R$ with fraction field $K$, a \emph{fractional ideal} is a nonzero $R$-submodule $I\subseteq K$ for which there is some nonzero $d\in R$ such that $dI\subseteq R$. A \emph{principal fractional ideal} has the form $aR$ for $a\in K^\times$. Over a Dedekind domain, nonzero fractional ideals form a group under multiplication.
