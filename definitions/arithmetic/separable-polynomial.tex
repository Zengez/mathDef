A nonzero polynomial $f\in K[x]$ over a field $K$ is \emph{separable} if it has no repeated roots in an algebraic closure of $K$. Equivalently $\gcd(f,f')=1$ for a polynomial of positive degree. This need not hold for irreducible polynomials over imperfect fields of positive characteristic.
