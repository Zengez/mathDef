For arithmetic functions $f,g:\mathbb Z_{>0}\to\mathbb C$, their \emph{Dirichlet convolution} is the arithmetic function
\[
(f*g)(n)=\sum_{d\mid n}f(d)g(n/d),\qquad n\ge1,
\]
where the sum is over positive divisors. It is associative and commutative, with identity the function taking value $1$ at $1$ and $0$ elsewhere.
