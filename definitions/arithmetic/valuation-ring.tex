A subring $R$ of a field $K$ is a \emph{valuation ring of $K$} if for every $x\in K^\times$, at least one of $x$ or $x^{-1}$ belongs to $R$. Such a ring is local and integrally closed, and its ideals are linearly ordered by inclusion. Its value group need not equal $\mathbb Z$.
