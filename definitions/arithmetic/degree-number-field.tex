For a number field $K$ (a finite extension of $\mathbb Q$), its \emph{degree} is the positive integer $[K:\mathbb Q]=\dim_{\mathbb Q}K$. If $K=\mathbb Q(\alpha)$ for an algebraic number $\alpha$, this equals the degree of the minimal polynomial of $\alpha$ over $\mathbb Q$.
