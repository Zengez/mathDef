For a field extension $L/K$, its \emph{Galois group} $\operatorname{Gal}(L/K)$ is the group $\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)$ of field automorphisms $\sigma:L\to L$ satisfying $\sigma(a)=a$ for all $a\in K$. Composition is the group operation. The notation is used even when the extension itself is not Galois, although additional theorems require that hypothesis.
