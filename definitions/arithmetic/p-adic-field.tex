For a prime $p$, the \emph{field of $p$-adic numbers} $\mathbb Q_p$ is the completion of $\mathbb Q$ under the metric $d_p(x,y)=|x-y|_p$. The $p$-adic absolute value extends uniquely to this complete nonarchimedean local field.
