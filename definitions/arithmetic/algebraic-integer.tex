A complex number $\alpha$ is an \emph{algebraic integer} if it satisfies a monic polynomial with coefficients in $\mathbb Z$. Equivalently, its minimal polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ has coefficients in $\mathbb Z$. All algebraic integers are algebraic numbers, but not conversely.
