For a nonconstant polynomial $f\in K[x]$, a \emph{splitting field} is an extension $L/K$ in which $f$ factors into linear factors and that is generated over $K$ by its roots. It is unique up to $K$-isomorphism and is a finite normal extension; separability of $f$ is not required.
