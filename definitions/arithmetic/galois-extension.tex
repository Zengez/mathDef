An algebraic field extension $L/K$ is \emph{Galois} if it is both normal and separable. For a finite extension this is equivalent to $|\operatorname{Aut}_K(L)|=[L:K]$. Normality means every irreducible polynomial over $K$ with a root in $L$ splits over $L$; separability requires distinct roots of the minimal polynomials.
