A \emph{number field} is a field extension $K/\mathbb Q$ of finite degree $[K:\mathbb Q]$. Equivalently, $K$ is isomorphic to $\mathbb Q(\alpha)$ for some algebraic number $\alpha$ by the primitive element theorem. Its ring of integers consists of those elements integral over $\mathbb Z$.
