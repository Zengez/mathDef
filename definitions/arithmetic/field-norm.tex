For a finite field extension $L/K$ and $\alpha\in L$, the \emph{field norm} $N_{L/K}(\alpha)$ is the determinant over $K$ of the $K$-linear multiplication map $m_\alpha:L\to L$, $x\mapsto\alpha x$. It belongs to $K$, satisfies $N_{L/K}(\alpha\beta)=N_{L/K}(\alpha)N_{L/K}(\beta)$, and does not require $L/K$ to be Galois.
