A \emph{nonarchimedean local field} is a field $K$ complete with respect to a nontrivial discrete valuation and having a finite residue field. Equivalently, it is a nondiscrete locally compact topological field with a nonarchimedean absolute value. Examples include finite extensions of $\mathbb Q_p$ and Laurent series fields $\mathbb F_q((t))$.
