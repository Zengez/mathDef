For a commutative local ring $(R,\mathfrak m)$ with maximal ideal $\mathfrak m$, the \emph{residue field} is $R/\mathfrak m$. More generally, each prime ideal $\mathfrak p$ has a residue field $\kappa(\mathfrak p)=R_{\mathfrak p}/\mathfrak pR_{\mathfrak p}$.
