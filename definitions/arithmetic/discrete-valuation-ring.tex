A \emph{discrete valuation ring} is an integral domain $R$ with fraction field $K$ admitting a surjective valuation $v:K^\times\to\mathbb Z$ for which $R=\{0\}\cup\{x\in K^\times:v(x)\ge0\}$. Equivalently, it is a local principal ideal domain that is not a field. Its unique nonzero maximal ideal is generated by an element of valuation $1$, called a uniformizer.
