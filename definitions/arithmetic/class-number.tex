The \emph{class number} $h_K$ of a number field $K$ is the order of the ideal class group of its ring of integers $\mathcal O_K$: $h_K=|\operatorname{Cl}(\mathcal O_K)|$. This group classifies nonzero fractional ideals modulo principal fractional ideals and is finite for number fields.
