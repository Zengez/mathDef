For a Dedekind domain $R$ with fraction field $K$, its \emph{ideal class group} $\operatorname{Cl}(R)$ is the abelian group of nonzero fractional ideals of $R$ under multiplication, modulo the subgroup $\{aR:a\in K^\times\}$ of principal fractional ideals. Its identity is the class of $R$, and it is finite when $R$ is the ring of integers of a number field.
