A complex number $\alpha$ is \emph{transcendental over $\mathbb Q$} if no nonzero polynomial $f\in\mathbb Q[x]$ satisfies $f(\alpha)=0$. Equivalently, $\alpha$ is not algebraic over $\mathbb Q$.
