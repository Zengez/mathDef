For $\Re(s)>1$, the \emph{Riemann zeta function} is the absolutely convergent Dirichlet series $\zeta(s)=\sum_{n=1}^\infty n^{-s}$. It admits a unique meromorphic continuation to $\mathbb C$, holomorphic except for a simple pole at $s=1$ with residue $1$.
