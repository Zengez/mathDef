% id: arithmetic.p-adic-valuation
% term: p-adic valuation on rationals
% related: number.prime-number, arithmetic.valuation
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/wiki/P-adic_number
% status: draft

For prime $p$ and $x\in\mathbb Q^\times$, write $x=p^m a/b$ with $p\nmid ab$; the \emph{$p$-adic valuation} is $v_p(x)=m$.
