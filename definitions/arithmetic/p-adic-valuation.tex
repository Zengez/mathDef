% id: arithmetic.p-adic-valuation
% term: p-adic valuation on rationals
% related: number.prime-number, arithmetic.valuation
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_order
% status: draft

Let $p$ be prime. Every nonzero rational $x$ has a unique expression $x=p^m(a/b)$ with $m\in\mathbb Z$ and integers $a,b$ not divisible by $p$. The \emph{$p$-adic valuation} is $v_p(x)=m$, extended by $v_p(0)=+\infty$. It satisfies $v_p(xy)=v_p(x)+v_p(y)$.
