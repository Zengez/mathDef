% id: arithmetic.p-adic-valuation
% term: p-adic valuation on rationals
% related: number.prime-number, arithmetic.valuation
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/P-adic_order
% status: draft

Let $p$ be prime. For each nonzero rational number $x$, there is a \emph{unique integer} $m$ such that
\[
x=p^m\frac ab,
\]
where $a,b$ are integers not divisible by $p$ and $b\ne0$. The \emph{$p$-adic valuation} is $v_p(x)=m$, extended by $v_p(0)=+\infty$. The integer $m$ is unique, even though the particular numerator and denominator $a,b$ need not be. It satisfies $v_p(xy)=v_p(x)+v_p(y)$ for nonzero $x,y$.
