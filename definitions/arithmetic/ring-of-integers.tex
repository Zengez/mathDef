% status: draft

For a number field $K$, its \emph{ring of integers} $\mathcal O_K$ consists of the elements $\alpha\in K$ satisfying some monic polynomial in $\mathbb Z[x]$. It is the integral closure of $\mathbb Z$ in $K$ and is a free $\mathbb Z$-module of rank $[K:\mathbb Q]$.
