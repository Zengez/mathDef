A complex number $\alpha$ is an \emph{algebraic number} if it is a root of a nonzero polynomial in $\mathbb Q[x]$ (equivalently, in $\mathbb Z[x]$). Its unique monic irreducible polynomial over $\mathbb Q$ is its minimal polynomial.
