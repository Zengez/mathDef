If $\alpha$ is algebraic over a field $K$, its \emph{minimal polynomial over $K$} is the unique monic polynomial $m_{\alpha,K}\in K[x]$ of smallest positive degree satisfying $m_{\alpha,K}(\alpha)=0$. It is irreducible and divides every polynomial in $K[x]$ that vanishes at $\alpha$.
