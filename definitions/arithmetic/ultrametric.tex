An \emph{ultrametric} on a set $X$ is a metric $d:X\times X\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying the strong triangle inequality $d(x,z)\le\max\{d(x,y),d(y,z)\}$ for all $x,y,z\in X$. In an ultrametric space, every triangle has at least two sides of equal maximal length.
