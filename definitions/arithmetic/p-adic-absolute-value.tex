For a prime $p$, the \emph{$p$-adic absolute value} on $\mathbb Q$ is $|0|_p=0$ and $|x|_p=p^{-v_p(x)}$ for $x\ne0$. It is multiplicative and satisfies the strong triangle inequality $|x+y|_p\le\max\{|x|_p,|y|_p\}$.
