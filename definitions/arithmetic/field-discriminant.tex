For an integral basis $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ of $\mathcal O_K$, the \emph{field discriminant} is $\det(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha_i\alpha_j))_{i,j}$.
