Let $K/\mathbb Q$ be a number field of degree $n$ and let $\alpha_1,\ldots,\alpha_n$ be an integral basis of its ring of integers $\mathcal O_K$. Its \emph{field discriminant} is
\[
\Delta_K=\det\bigl(\operatorname{Tr}_{K/\mathbb Q}(\alpha_i\alpha_j)\bigr)_{1\le i,j\le n}.
\]
The value is an integer independent of the choice of integral basis, since changes of integral basis have determinant $\pm1$.
