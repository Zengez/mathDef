A positive integer $n$ is \emph{perfect} if the sum of its positive divisors strictly smaller than $n$ equals $n$. Equivalently, the sum-of-divisors function satisfies $\sigma(n)=2n$. The integer $1$ is not perfect, since it has no positive proper divisors.
