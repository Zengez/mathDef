For integers $a,b$, the relation $a\mid b$ (\emph{$a$ divides $b$}) means that there is $k\in\mathbb Z$ with $b=ak$. In particular every nonzero integer divides $0$; the literal relation also gives $0\mid0$, though some treatments restrict divisors to $a\ne0$. State that restriction when used.
