For integers $a,b$ not both zero, their \emph{greatest common divisor} $\gcd(a,b)$ is the unique positive integer $d$ dividing both $a$ and $b$ such that every common divisor of $a,b$ also divides $d$. Equivalently it is their greatest positive common divisor. The usual extension sets $\gcd(0,0)=0$.
