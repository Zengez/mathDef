The number-theoretic \emph{Möbius function} $\mu:\mathbb Z_{>0}\to\{-1,0,1\}$ is defined by $\mu(1)=1$; for $n>1$, $\mu(n)=0$ when some prime square divides $n$, and otherwise $\mu(n)=(-1)^r$, where $r$ is the number of distinct prime divisors of $n$. Thus $\mu(n)$ is multiplicative, and $\sum_{d\mid n}\mu(d)=0$ when $n>1$.
