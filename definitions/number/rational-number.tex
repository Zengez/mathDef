A \emph{rational number} is a real number expressible as $a/b$ with $a,b\in\mathbb Z$ and $b\ne0$. Two such fractions $a/b$ and $c/d$ represent the same rational number precisely when $ad=bc$. The rationals form the field $\mathbb Q$.
