Two integers $a,b$, not both zero, are \emph{coprime} (relatively prime) if $\gcd(a,b)=1$. Equivalently, there exist integers $u,v$ with $au+bv=1$ (Bézout's identity). In particular, $1$ is coprime to every integer, including $0$.
