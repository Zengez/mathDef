For nonzero integers $a,b$, their \emph{least common multiple} $\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)$ is the smallest positive integer divisible by both. It satisfies $\gcd(a,b)\operatorname{lcm}(a,b)=|ab|$. A common extension sets $\operatorname{lcm}(a,0)=0$ for all integers $a$.
