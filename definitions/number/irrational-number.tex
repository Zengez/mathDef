An \emph{irrational number} is a real number $x\in\mathbb R$ not expressible as $a/b$ with $a,b\in\mathbb Z$ and $b\ne0$. Equivalently, $x\in\mathbb R\setminus\mathbb Q$. The terminology applies to real numbers, not indiscriminately to arbitrary complex numbers.
