In the usual elementary number-theory convention, an \emph{arithmetic function} is a map $f:\mathbb Z_{>0}\to\mathbb C$. Some authors restrict its values to $\mathbb R$ or $\mathbb Z$, while broader conventions include functions on ideals or other arithmetic objects. Multiplicativity is an additional property, not part of the definition.
