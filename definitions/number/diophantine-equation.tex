A \emph{Diophantine equation} is a polynomial equation $P(x_1,\ldots,x_m)=0$ with integer coefficients for which solutions in integers are sought (sometimes with a stated restriction to positive integers or rational solutions). Requiring integer solutions distinguishes this problem from solving the same equation over the reals or complexes.
