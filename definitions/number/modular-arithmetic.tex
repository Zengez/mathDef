For an integer modulus $n\ge1$, \emph{modular arithmetic} is arithmetic in the quotient ring $\mathbb Z/n\mathbb Z$ of residue classes. Addition and multiplication are defined by $[a]_n+[b]_n=[a+b]_n$ and $[a]_n[b]_n=[ab]_n$. These operations are well-defined because congruence modulo $n$ is compatible with integer addition and multiplication.
