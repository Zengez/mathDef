On a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, a property holds \emph{$P$-almost surely} if it holds on some measurable event $A\in\mathcal F$ with $P(A)=1$. Equivalently, it may fail only on a subset of a measurable null set. Almost sure does not mean that there are no exceptional outcomes.
