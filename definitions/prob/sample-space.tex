The \emph{sample space} $\Omega$ of a probability model is the set of all possible elementary outcomes. In a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, only subsets belonging to the specified $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal F$ are events to which $P$ assigns probabilities. Elementary outcomes need not be equally likely, and individual singletons need not be measurable.
