For $n\ge1$ numerical observations $x_1,\ldots,x_n\in\mathbb R$, their \emph{sample mean} is $\overline{x}=\frac1n\sum_{i=1}^n x_i$. For random observations $X_1,\ldots,X_n$, the sample mean $\overline X$ is itself a random variable. Independence is not needed to define it.
