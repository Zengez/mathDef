% term: Expected value
% related: prob.random-variable, prob.variance
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Expected_value
% status: draft

For a real random variable $X$ with $E[|X|]<\infty$ on a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, its \emph{expected value} is $E[X]=\int_\Omega X\,dP$. For discrete $X$ with values $x$ of positive mass this becomes the absolutely convergent sum $E[X]=\sum_x xP(X=x)$. Extended expectations require a separate convention.
