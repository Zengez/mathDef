For events $A,B$ in a probability space with $P(B)>0$, the \emph{conditional probability} of $A$ given $B$ is $P(A\mid B)=P(A\cap B)/P(B)$. For fixed such $B$, the map $A\mapsto P(A\mid B)$ is a probability measure; the simple quotient does not define conditioning on a null event.
