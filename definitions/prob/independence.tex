Events $A,B$ in a probability space are \emph{independent} if $P(A\cap B)=P(A)P(B)$. For a family of events, \emph{mutual independence} means that for every finite subfamily $A_1,\ldots,A_m$, one has $P(\bigcap_{j=1}^m A_j)=\prod_{j=1}^mP(A_j)$. Pairwise independence is a weaker property.
