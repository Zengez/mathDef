For real square-integrable random variables $X,Y$ with strictly positive variances, the \emph{Pearson correlation coefficient} is
\[
\rho(X,Y)=\frac{\operatorname{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}\,\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(Y)}}.
\]
It lies in $[-1,1]$ and is undefined by this formula when either variance is zero.
