The \emph{cumulative distribution function} of a real-valued random variable $X$ is $F_X(t)=P(X\le t)$ for $t\in\mathbb R$. It is nondecreasing and right-continuous, with $\lim_{t\to-\infty}F_X(t)=0$ and $\lim_{t\to+\infty}F_X(t)=1$. These properties characterize real-valued probability distribution functions.
