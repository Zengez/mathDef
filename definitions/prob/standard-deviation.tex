For a real random variable $X$ with finite second moment, its \emph{standard deviation} is $\sigma_X=\sqrt{\operatorname{Var}(X)}\ge0$. It has the same physical units as $X$ and is zero exactly when $X$ is almost surely constant. The sample standard deviation uses the square root of a chosen sample-variance normalization.
