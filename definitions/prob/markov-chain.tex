A discrete-time stochastic process $(X_n)_{n\ge0}$ with a finite or countable state space $S$ is a \emph{Markov chain} if, whenever a history $X_0=i_0,\ldots,X_n=i_n$ has positive probability,
\[
P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_0=i_0,\ldots,X_n=i_n)=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i_n)
\]
for every $j\in S$. The transition probabilities may depend on $n$; a \emph{time-homogeneous} chain uses the same transition matrix at each step.
