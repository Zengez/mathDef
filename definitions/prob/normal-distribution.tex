The \emph{normal distribution} $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$, with $\sigma>0$, has density
\[
f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\exp\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right).
\]
