The (nondegenerate) \emph{normal distribution} $N(\mu,\sigma^2)$ on $\mathbb R$, where $\mu\in\mathbb R$ and $\sigma>0$, has density
\[
f(x)=\frac{1}{\sigma\sqrt{2\pi}}\exp\left(-\frac{(x-\mu)^2}{2\sigma^2}\right).
\]
Its mean is $\mu$ and variance is $\sigma^2$. The case $\sigma=0$ is sometimes admitted as a degenerate point-mass law but has no Lebesgue density.
