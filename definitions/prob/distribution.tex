If $X:(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)\to(S,\mathcal S)$ is a measurable random element, its \emph{probability distribution} (or law) is the pushforward probability measure $\mu_X$ on $(S,\mathcal S)$ defined by $\mu_X(B)=P(X^{-1}(B))=P(X\in B)$ for $B\in\mathcal S$. It is a measure, not necessarily a density function.
