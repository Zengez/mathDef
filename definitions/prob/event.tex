In a probability space $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, an \emph{event} is a measurable subset $A\in\mathcal F$ of the sample space $\Omega$. Its probability is $P(A)$. An arbitrary subset of $\Omega$ need not be an event if the chosen $\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal F$ does not contain it.
