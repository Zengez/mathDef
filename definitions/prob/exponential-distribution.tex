The \emph{exponential distribution} with rate $\lambda>0$ has Lebesgue density $f(x)=\lambda e^{-\lambda x}$ for $x\ge0$ and $f(x)=0$ for $x<0$. Its distribution function is $F(x)=1-e^{-\lambda x}$ for $x\ge0$ (and zero below), and its mean is $1/\lambda$. The rate and scale parametrizations are reciprocal.
