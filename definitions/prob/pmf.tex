% status: draft

For a discrete random variable $X$ with values in a finite or countable set $S$, its \emph{probability mass function} is $p_X(x)=P(X=x)$ for $x\in S$. It satisfies $p_X(x)\ge0$ and $\sum_{x\in S}p_X(x)=1$, and determines the distribution by $P(X\in A)=\sum_{x\in A}p_X(x)$ for $A\subseteq S$.
