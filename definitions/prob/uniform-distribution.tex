For real numbers $a<b$, the \emph{continuous uniform distribution} on $[a,b]$ has density $f(x)=1/(b-a)$ for $a\le x\le b$ and $f(x)=0$ otherwise. Values assigned to the density at the endpoints are immaterial to the probability law. Its probability on a Borel set $B$ equals the length of $B\cap[a,b]$ divided by $b-a$.
