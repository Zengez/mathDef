A \emph{$\sigma$-algebra} $\mathcal F$ on a set $\Omega$ is a family of subsets satisfying $\Omega\in\mathcal F$, $A\in\mathcal F\Rightarrow\Omega\setminus A\in\mathcal F$, and $\bigcup_{n\ge1}A_n\in\mathcal F$ whenever every $A_n\in\mathcal F$. Consequently, it contains $\varnothing$ and is closed under countable intersections.
