For a real random variable $X$ with $E[X^2]<\infty$, its \emph{variance} is $\operatorname{Var}(X)=E[(X-E[X])^2]=E[X^2]-(E[X])^2$. It is nonnegative and vanishes exactly when $X$ is almost surely constant. This is a population quantity, distinct from a particular sample-variance estimator.
