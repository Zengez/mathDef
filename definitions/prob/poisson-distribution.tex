A random variable $X$ has the \emph{Poisson distribution} with parameter $\lambda>0$ if $P(X=k)=e^{-\lambda}\lambda^k/k!$ for all integers $k\ge0$, and $P(X\in\mathbb N_0)=1$. Its mean and variance both equal $\lambda$. Some conventions also allow $\lambda=0$, giving a point mass at $0$.
