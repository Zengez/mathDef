A \emph{real-valued random variable} on $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ is a measurable map $X:(\Omega,\mathcal F)\to(\mathbb R,\mathcal B(\mathbb R))$: for every Borel set $B\subseteq\mathbb R$, the inverse image $X^{-1}(B)$ belongs to $\mathcal F$. The associated probability law is the pushforward measure $P\circ X^{-1}$.
