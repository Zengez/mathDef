For a time-homogeneous Markov chain $(X_n)$ on a finite or countable state space $S$, its \emph{transition matrix} $P=(p_{ij})_{i,j\in S}$ consists of probabilities $p_{ij}=P(X_{n+1}=j\mid X_n=i)$ whenever the conditioning event has positive probability (or from a specified transition kernel otherwise). It satisfies $p_{ij}\ge0$ and $\sum_{j\in S}p_{ij}=1$ for each $i$. In a time-inhomogeneous chain, matrices may depend on $n$.
