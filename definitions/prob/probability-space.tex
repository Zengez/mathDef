A \emph{probability space} is $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$, where $\mathcal F$ is a $\sigma$-algebra of subsets of $\Omega$ and $P:\mathcal F\to[0,1]$ is countably additive with $P(\Omega)=1$.
