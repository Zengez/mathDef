A \emph{probability space} is a triple $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ in which $\Omega$ is a sample space, $\mathcal F$ is a $\sigma$-algebra of subsets of $\Omega$, and $P:\mathcal F\to[0,1]$ is countably additive with $P(\Omega)=1$. In particular, $P(\varnothing)=0$, and the events are precisely the sets in $\mathcal F$.
