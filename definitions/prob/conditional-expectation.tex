% term: Conditional expectation
% related: prob.expectation, prob.sigma-algebra
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Conditional_expectation
% status: draft

On $(\Omega,\mathcal F,P)$ let $X\in L^1(P)$ and let $\mathcal G\subseteq\mathcal F$ be a sub-$\sigma$-algebra. A \emph{conditional expectation} $E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is a $\mathcal G$-measurable integrable random variable $Y$ such that $\int_GY\,dP=\int_GX\,dP$ for all $G\in\mathcal G$. It exists and is unique up to $P$-almost sure equality, not pointwise uniqueness.
