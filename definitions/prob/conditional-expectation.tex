For $X\in L^1$ and a sub-$\sigma$-algebra $\mathcal G$, the \emph{conditional expectation} $E[X\mid\mathcal G]$ is a $\mathcal G$-measurable integrable variable $Y$ with $\int_GY\,dP=\int_GX\,dP$ for all $G\in\mathcal G$.
