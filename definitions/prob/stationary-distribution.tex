% status: draft

For a time-homogeneous Markov chain on a finite or countable state space with row-stochastic transition matrix $P=(p_{ij})$, a \emph{stationary distribution} is a probability row vector $\pi=(\pi_i)$ such that $\pi_i\ge0$, $\sum_i\pi_i=1$, and $\pi P=\pi$. Thus starting with law $\pi$ leaves the marginal distribution unchanged after each step. Stationarity need not imply uniqueness.
