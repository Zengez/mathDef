An \emph{atlas} of dimension $n$ on a topological manifold $M$ is a collection of coordinate charts $(U_\alpha,\varphi_\alpha)$ such that the chart domains $U_\alpha$ cover $M$. An atlas need not have differentiable transition maps; the additional requirement of smooth overlap transitions defines a smooth atlas.
