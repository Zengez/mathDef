% term: Tangent bundle
% related: manifold.tangent-space, manifold.vector-bundle
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tangent_bundle
% status: draft

For a smooth manifold $M$, the \emph{tangent bundle} is the disjoint union $TM=\bigsqcup_{p\in M}T_pM$, equipped with projection $\pi:TM\to M$ sending $v\in T_pM$ to $p$. Local charts endow $TM$ with a natural smooth rank-$\dim M$ vector-bundle structure. Smooth sections of $TM$ are precisely smooth vector fields.
