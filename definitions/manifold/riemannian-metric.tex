A \emph{Riemannian metric} on a smooth manifold $M$ is a smooth section $g\in\Gamma(\operatorname{Sym}^2T^*M)$ such that the symmetric bilinear form $g_p:T_pM\times T_pM\to\mathbb R$ is positive definite for each $p$. In local coordinates $g=\sum_{i,j}g_{ij}\,dx^i\otimes dx^j$ with a smooth positive-definite matrix $(g_{ij})$.
