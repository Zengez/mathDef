A \emph{topological $n$-manifold} is a Hausdorff, second-countable topological space $M$ such that every point has an open neighborhood homeomorphic to an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$. In this convention manifolds have no boundary; manifolds with boundary have local charts modeled on the closed half-space of $\mathbb R^n$. A topological manifold need not carry any prescribed smooth structure.
