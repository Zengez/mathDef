For a smooth manifold $M$, its \emph{cotangent bundle} is the dual vector bundle $T^*M=\bigsqcup_{p\in M}T_p^*M$ with projection $(p,\xi)\mapsto p$. Each fiber consists of linear functionals on $T_pM$. Smooth sections of $T^*M$ are differential one-forms, and the bundle has a canonical smooth structure.
