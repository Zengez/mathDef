A \emph{symplectic form} on a smooth manifold $M$ is a smooth differential two-form $\omega\in\Omega^2(M)$ that is closed, $d\omega=0$, and pointwise nondegenerate: if $\omega_p(v,w)=0$ for every $w\in T_pM$, then $v=0$. Consequently, every tangent space has even dimension. The closedness requirement distinguishes symplectic forms from merely almost-symplectic forms.
