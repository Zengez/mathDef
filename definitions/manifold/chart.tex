A \emph{coordinate chart} $(U,\varphi)$ of dimension $n$ on a topological manifold $M$ consists of an open subset $U\subseteq M$ and a homeomorphism $\varphi:U\to\varphi(U)$ onto an open subset of $\mathbb R^n$. The points of $U$ are given coordinate tuples by $\varphi$, but a chart does not by itself supply a global coordinate system.
