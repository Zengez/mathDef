A \emph{smooth rank-$k$ vector bundle} over $M$ is a smooth surjection $\pi:E\to M$ whose fibers are $k$-dimensional vector spaces and which is locally smoothly isomorphic to $U\times\mathbb R^k$ by fiberwise linear maps.
