% status: draft

A smooth map $F:M\to N$ between smooth manifolds is an \emph{immersion} if its differential $dF_p:T_pM\to T_{F(p)}N$ is injective at every $p\in M$. An immersion is locally an embedding but need not be globally injective, and an injective immersion need not be a topological embedding.
