A \emph{Riemannian manifold} $(M,g)$ is a smooth real manifold $M$ equipped with a Riemannian metric $g$, meaning a smoothly varying positive-definite inner product on each tangent space. The metric determines lengths of curves, a path distance on each connected component, and a unique Levi--Civita connection.
