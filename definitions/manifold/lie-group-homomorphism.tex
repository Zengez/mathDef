A \emph{Lie group homomorphism} $\varphi:G\to H$ is a smooth map between Lie groups that preserves multiplication: $\varphi(gh)=\varphi(g)\varphi(h)$ for all $g,h\in G$. It then also preserves the identity and inverses, and its differential at the identity induces a Lie algebra homomorphism $d\varphi_e:\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak h$.
