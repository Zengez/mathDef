A subset $S$ of a smooth $n$-manifold $M$ is an \emph{embedded $k$-dimensional submanifold} if for every $p\in S$ there is a smooth chart $(U,\varphi)$ centered near $p$ with
\[
\varphi(U\cap S)=\varphi(U)\cap(\mathbb R^k\times\{0\}).
\]
It receives the subspace topology and the induced smooth structure. The notion differs from an immersed submanifold, whose topology need not be the subspace topology.
