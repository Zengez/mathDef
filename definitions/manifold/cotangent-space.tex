For a smooth manifold $M$ and $p\in M$, the \emph{cotangent space} $T_p^*M$ is the real linear dual $(T_pM)^*=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathbb R}(T_pM,\mathbb R)$. Its elements are covectors (linear functionals on tangent vectors). For a smooth function $f$, the differential $df_p$ belongs to $T_p^*M$.
