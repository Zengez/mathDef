A \emph{smooth $n$-manifold} is a Hausdorff second-countable space $M$ locally homeomorphic to $\mathbb R^n$, equipped with a smooth structure, equivalently a maximal atlas whose coordinate changes are smooth. Second countability and Hausdorffness are standard conventions; manifolds with boundary require charts modeled on a half-space instead.
