% term: Lie bracket of vector fields

% status: draft

For smooth vector fields $X,Y$ on a smooth manifold $M$, their \emph{Lie bracket} is the unique smooth vector field $[X,Y]$ whose action as a derivation on smooth functions is
\[
[X,Y](f)=X(Y(f))-Y(X(f)).
\]
It is bilinear and antisymmetric, obeys the Jacobi identity, and in general is not $C^\infty(M)$-bilinear.
