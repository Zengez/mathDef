For a Riemannian metric $g$ on a smooth manifold $M$, the \emph{Levi--Civita connection} is the unique affine connection $\nabla$ satisfying metric compatibility
\[
X(g(Y,Z))=g(\nabla_XY,Z)+g(Y,\nabla_XZ)
\]
and vanishing torsion $\nabla_XY-\nabla_YX=[X,Y]$. These two properties determine $\nabla$ through the Koszul formula.
