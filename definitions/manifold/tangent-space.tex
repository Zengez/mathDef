For $p$ on a smooth manifold $M$, its \emph{tangent space} $T_pM$ may be defined as the real vector space of derivations at $p$ on germs of smooth functions: an $\mathbb R$-linear functional $v$ with $v(fg)=f(p)v(g)+g(p)v(f)$. Equivalently, it consists of velocities of smooth curves through $p$, modulo equality of derivatives in local coordinates.
