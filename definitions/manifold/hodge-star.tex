On an oriented Riemannian manifold $(M^n,g)$ the \emph{Hodge star} is the vector-bundle isomorphism $*:\Omega^k(M)\to\Omega^{n-k}(M)$ uniquely specified by
\[
\alpha\wedge*\beta=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle_g\,\operatorname{vol}_g
\]
for all real $k$-forms $\alpha,\beta$. For this positive-definite metric the identity $**\alpha=(-1)^{k(n-k)}\alpha$ holds. Orientation is used to select the volume form.
