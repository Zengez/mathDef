On an oriented Riemannian $n$-manifold $(M,g)$, the \emph{Hodge star} is the linear map $*:\Omega^k(M)\to\Omega^{n-k}(M)$ on real forms uniquely determined by $\alpha\wedge*\beta=\langle\alpha,\beta\rangle_g\,\mathrm{vol}_g$.
