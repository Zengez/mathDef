An \emph{affine connection} on $TM$ is an $\mathbb R$-bilinear operator $\nabla_XY$ satisfying $\nabla_{fX}Y=f\nabla_XY$ and $\nabla_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_XY$.
