An \emph{affine connection} on the tangent bundle of a smooth manifold $M$ is an $\mathbb R$-bilinear map
\[
\nabla:\mathfrak X(M)\times\mathfrak X(M)\to\mathfrak X(M),\qquad(X,Y)\mapsto\nabla_XY,
\]
satisfying $\nabla_{fX}Y=f\nabla_XY$ and $\nabla_X(fY)=X(f)Y+f\nabla_XY$ for all $f\in C^\infty(M)$. It differentiates vector fields along vector fields, without requiring a metric or vanishing torsion.
