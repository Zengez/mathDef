A \emph{tangent vector} $v$ at $p\in M$ can be defined as an $\mathbb R$-linear derivation on germs of smooth real functions at $p$, satisfying the Leibniz identity
\[
v(fg)=f(p)v(g)+g(p)v(f).
\]
Under a coordinate chart $(x^1,\ldots,x^n)$ it is represented uniquely by $v=\sum_i v^i(\partial/\partial x^i)_p$.
