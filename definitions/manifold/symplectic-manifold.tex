A \emph{symplectic manifold} is a pair $(M,\omega)$ where $M$ is a smooth manifold and $\omega$ is a closed, nondegenerate differential two-form on $M$. The nondegeneracy of $\omega$ forces $\dim M$ to be even, and the top exterior power $\omega^n$ supplies a natural orientation when $\dim M=2n$.
