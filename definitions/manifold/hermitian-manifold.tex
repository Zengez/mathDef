A \emph{Hermitian manifold} is a complex manifold $M$ together with a smooth positive-definite Hermitian inner product $h_p$ on each holomorphic tangent space $T_p^{1,0}M$. Its real part yields a Riemannian metric $g$ compatible with the complex structure $J$, meaning $g(JX,JY)=g(X,Y)$. The associated fundamental two-form need not be closed; that additional condition gives a Kähler metric.
