% term: Section of a bundle
% related: manifold.vector-bundle, manifold.vector-field
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/smooth%2Bmanifold
% status: draft

A \emph{section} of $\pi:E\to M$ is a map $s:M\to E$ satisfying $\pi\circ s=\operatorname{id}_M$; a smooth section is additionally smooth.
