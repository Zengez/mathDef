% id: manifold.differential
% term: Differential of a smooth map
% related: manifold.tangent-space, manifold.smooth-map
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pushforward_(differential)
% status: draft

For a smooth map $F:M\to N$ and $p\in M$, its \emph{differential} (pushforward) $dF_p:T_pM\to T_{F(p)}N$ is the linear map characterized, for tangent vectors viewed as derivations, by
\[
(dF_pv)(g)=v(g\circ F)
\]
for smooth functions $g$ defined near $F(p)$. In local coordinates it is represented by the Jacobian matrix of $F$, and its compositions satisfy the chain rule.
