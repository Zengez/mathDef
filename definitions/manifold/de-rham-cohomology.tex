For a smooth manifold $M$, the \emph{$k$th de Rham cohomology} is
\[
H_{\mathrm{dR}}^k(M)=
\frac{\ker(d:\Omega^k(M)\to\Omega^{k+1}(M))}
{\operatorname{im}(d:\Omega^{k-1}(M)\to\Omega^k(M))},
\]
where $d$ is the exterior derivative and $d^2=0$. At $k=0$, the denominator is $\{0\}$, so $H^0_{\mathrm{dR}}(M)$ consists of locally constant smooth functions. Coefficients are real unless stated otherwise.
