For a smooth manifold $M$, \emph{$k$th de Rham cohomology} is $H_{\mathrm{dR}}^k(M)=\ker(d:\Omega^k(M)\to\Omega^{k+1}(M))/\operatorname{im}(d:\Omega^{k-1}(M)\to\Omega^k(M))$. For $k=0$, the denominator is $\{0\}$.
