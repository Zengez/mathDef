A \emph{Lie group} is a group $G$ equipped with a finite-dimensional smooth manifold structure such that multiplication $G\times G\to G$, $(g,h)\mapsto gh$, and inversion $G\to G$, $g\mapsto g^{-1}$, are smooth. A Lie group need not be connected or compact. Its tangent space at the identity carries a natural Lie algebra structure.
