A \emph{diffeomorphism} between smooth manifolds $M,N$ is a bijective smooth map $F:M\to N$ whose inverse $F^{-1}:N\to M$ is also smooth. It is an isomorphism in the category of smooth manifolds. A smooth bijection need not be a diffeomorphism if its inverse is not smooth.
