The \emph{exterior derivative} on a smooth manifold $M$ is the unique family of real-linear operators $d:\Omega^k(M)\to\Omega^{k+1}(M)$ extending the usual differential $df$ of functions and satisfying
\[
d(\alpha\wedge\beta)=d\alpha\wedge\beta+(-1)^k\alpha\wedge d\beta\quad(\alpha\in\Omega^k(M)),\qquad d^2=0.
\]
In local coordinates $d(\sum_I f_I\,dx^I)=\sum_I df_I\wedge dx^I$.
