A \emph{smooth vector field} on a smooth manifold $M$ is a smooth section $X:M\to TM$ of its tangent bundle, so $X(p)\in T_pM$ for every point $p$. Locally, in coordinates $(x^1,\ldots,x^n)$ it has the form $X=\sum_i X^i\,\partial/\partial x^i$ with smooth coefficient functions $X^i$.
