A \emph{smooth atlas} on a topological $n$-manifold $M$ is an atlas $(U_\alpha,\varphi_\alpha)$ for which all nonempty-overlap transition maps $\varphi_\beta\circ\varphi_\alpha^{-1}$ are $C^\infty$ diffeomorphisms between open subsets of $\mathbb R^n$. Two smooth atlases determine the same smooth structure exactly when their union is again a smooth atlas.
