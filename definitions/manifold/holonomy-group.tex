% id: manifold.holonomy-group
% term: Holonomy group of a connection
% related: manifold.vector-bundle, manifold.affine-connection
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holonomy
% status: draft

For a connection $\nabla$ on a smooth vector bundle $E\to M$ and a point $p\in M$, the \emph{holonomy group} at $p$ is the subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(E_p)$ consisting of parallel-transport maps around piecewise smooth loops based at $p$.
