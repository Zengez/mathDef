% id: manifold.holonomy-group
% term: Holonomy group of a connection
% related: manifold.vector-bundle, manifold.affine-connection
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Holonomy
% status: draft

For a connection $\nabla$ on a smooth finite-rank vector bundle $E\to M$ and a base point $p\in M$, its \emph{holonomy group} $\operatorname{Hol}_p(\nabla)$ is the subgroup of $\operatorname{GL}(E_p)$ formed by parallel-transport linear isomorphisms around piecewise smooth loops based at $p$. Along paths joining base points in the same component, parallel transport identifies their holonomy groups up to conjugation.
