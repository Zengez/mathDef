A \emph{differential $k$-form} on a smooth manifold $M$ is a smooth section $\omega\in\Gamma(\bigwedge^kT^*M)$. At each point $p$, its value $\omega_p$ is an alternating $k$-linear functional on $T_pM$. In degree zero, differential forms are precisely smooth scalar functions.
