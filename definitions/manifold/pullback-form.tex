For smooth $F:M\to N$ and $\omega\in\Omega^k(N)$, the \emph{pullback} $F^*\omega$ satisfies $(F^*\omega)_p(v_1,\ldots,v_k)=\omega_{F(p)}(dF_pv_1,\ldots,dF_pv_k)$.
