% term: Pullback of differential forms
% related: manifold.differential-form, manifold.differential

% status: draft

For a smooth map $F:M\to N$ and a differential $k$-form $\omega\in\Omega^k(N)$, its \emph{pullback} $F^*\omega\in\Omega^k(M)$ is defined by
\[
(F^*\omega)_p(v_1,\ldots,v_k)=\omega_{F(p)}(dF_pv_1,\ldots,dF_pv_k).
\]
It preserves wedge products and commutes with exterior differentiation: $F^*(d\omega)=d(F^*\omega)$. Pullback is contravariant in $F$.
