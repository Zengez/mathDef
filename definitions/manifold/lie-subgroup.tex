An \emph{immersed Lie subgroup} of a Lie group $G$ is a subgroup $H\le G$ with a Lie-group manifold structure for which the inclusion $H\hookrightarrow G$ is a smooth injective immersion and group homomorphism. The topology of $H$ need not equal its subspace topology in $G$. An \emph{embedded Lie subgroup} additionally has inclusion a smooth embedding; closed subgroups have a canonical embedded Lie subgroup structure.
