A \emph{Lie algebra} over a field $F$ is a vector space $\mathfrak g$ with a bilinear bracket $[x,y]$ satisfying $[x,x]=0$ and $[x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0$.
