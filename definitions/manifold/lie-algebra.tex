A \emph{Lie algebra} over a field $F$ is a vector space $\mathfrak g$ equipped with a bilinear operation $[\cdot,\cdot]:\mathfrak g\times\mathfrak g\to\mathfrak g$ that is alternating, $[x,x]=0$, and satisfies the Jacobi identity
\[
[x,[y,z]]+[y,[z,x]]+[z,[x,y]]=0.
\]
In characteristic different from $2$, alternation is equivalent to skew-symmetry; in characteristic $2$ it is safer to state alternation explicitly.
