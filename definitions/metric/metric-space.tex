A \emph{metric space} is a pair $(X,d)$, where $d:X\times X\to[0,\infty)$ is a function such that
\[
d(x,y)=0\iff x=y,\qquad d(x,y)=d(y,x),\qquad
d(x,z)\le d(x,y)+d(y,z).
\]
