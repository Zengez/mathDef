For $z\in\mathbb B_d\setminus\{0\}$, define $T_z=\{\zeta\in\mathbb B_d:|1-\langle\zeta,z/|z|\rangle|\le1-|z|\}$. For $z=0$ put $T_0=\mathbb B_d$.
