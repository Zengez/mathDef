For $1<p<\infty$ and positive weights $w,\sigma$ on $\mathbb B_d$, set
\[
[w,\sigma]_{\mathcal B_p}
=\sup_{z\in\mathbb B_d}
\langle w\rangle_{T_z}\langle\sigma\rangle_{T_z}^{p-1},
\]
using volume averages over the Carleson tents $T_z$. A weight $w$ belongs to $\mathcal B_p$ when $[w,w^{1-p'}]_{\mathcal B_p}<\infty$. This is the paper's classical Carleson-tent characteristic, distinct from its dyadic $B_p$ characteristic.
