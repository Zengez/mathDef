For a measurable $Q$ of positive finite measure and a Young function $\Phi$, the \emph{Orlicz average} of $f\ge0$ is $\langle f\rangle_{\Phi,Q}=\inf\{\lambda>0:|Q|^{-1}\int_Q\Phi(f/\lambda)\le1\}$.
