For metric spaces $(X,d_X)$ and $(Y,d_Y)$, an \emph{isometric embedding} is a map $f:X\to Y$ such that $d_Y(f(x),f(y))=d_X(x,y)$ for all $x,y\in X$. Such a map is automatically injective. Some authors reserve \emph{isometry} for surjective distance-preserving maps; under that convention $X$ and $Y$ are isometric exactly when such a surjection exists.
