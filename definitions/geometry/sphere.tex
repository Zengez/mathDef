For $n\ge1$, center $c\in\mathbb R^n$ and radius $r>0$, the Euclidean \emph{$(n-1)$-sphere} is
\[
S^{n-1}(c,r)=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:\|x-c\|=r\}.
\]
It is the boundary of the closed ball $\{x:\|x-c\|\le r\}$; the sphere is not the filled ball itself.
