A \emph{convex polytope} in $\mathbb R^n$ is the convex hull of a nonempty finite collection of points. Equivalently, it is a nonempty bounded convex polyhedron (an intersection of finitely many affine half-spaces). Polytopes need not have full dimension in the ambient space.
