For $c\in\mathbb R^2$ and $r\ge0$, the \emph{closed Euclidean disk} is $\overline B(c,r)=\{x\in\mathbb R^2:\|x-c\|\le r\}$. Its boundary is the circle of radius $r$ when $r>0$. The \emph{open disk} uses the strict inequality $\|x-c\|<r$.
