Given $k\ge1$ points $p_1,\ldots,p_k$ in a real affine space, an \emph{affine combination} is an expression $\sum_{i=1}^{k}a_ip_i$ with real coefficients satisfying $\sum_{i=1}^k a_i=1$. The expression is independent of the origin chosen to identify the affine space with a vector space. If also $a_i\ge0$ for every $i$, it is a convex combination.
