A \emph{simple polygonal boundary} in the Euclidean plane is a closed chain of at least three line segments meeting consecutively at endpoints, with no other intersections and with distinct consecutive vertices. Under the filled-polygon convention, the \emph{simple polygon} is the union of this boundary and its bounded interior. The word \emph{polygon} is also used for the boundary alone, so the intended convention must be stated.
