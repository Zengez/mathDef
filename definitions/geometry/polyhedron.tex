A \emph{convex polyhedron} in $\mathbb R^n$ is an intersection of finitely many closed affine half-spaces,
\[
P=\{x\in\mathbb R^n:Ax\le b\},
\]
for a real matrix $A$ and vector $b$. The intersection need not be bounded; a bounded nonempty polyhedron is a convex polytope. Some conventions include the empty set.
