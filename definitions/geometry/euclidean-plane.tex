A \emph{Euclidean plane} is a two-dimensional real affine space whose translation vector space has a positive-definite inner product. The induced distance between points is $d(p,q)=\|q-p\|$, independent of the choice of origin. Choosing orthonormal coordinates identifies it isometrically with the standard $\mathbb R^2$.
