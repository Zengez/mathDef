Two intersecting lines in Euclidean space are \emph{perpendicular} if the inner product of their nonzero direction vectors is zero, equivalently their angle is $\pi/2$. In higher-dimensional spaces, two skew lines may have orthogonal direction vectors without intersecting; whether these are called perpendicular depends on convention.
