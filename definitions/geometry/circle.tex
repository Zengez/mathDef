A \emph{circle} of center $c\in\mathbb R^2$ and radius $r>0$ is the set $\{x\in\mathbb R^2:\|x-c\|=r\}$, using Euclidean distance. It is the boundary of the corresponding closed disk. Allowing $r=0$ produces a degenerate singleton, which is excluded here.
