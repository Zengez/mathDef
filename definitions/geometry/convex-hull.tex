For a subset $S$ of a real vector space, its \emph{convex hull} $\operatorname{conv}(S)$ is the intersection of all convex subsets containing $S$. Equivalently, it consists of all finite convex combinations $\sum_{i=1}^k a_ix_i$ with $x_i\in S$, $a_i\ge0$, and $\sum_i a_i=1$. Under the usual convention $\operatorname{conv}(\varnothing)=\varnothing$.
