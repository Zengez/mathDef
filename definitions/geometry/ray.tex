For a point $p$ in a real affine space and a nonzero direction vector $v$, the \emph{closed ray} with origin $p$ and direction $v$ is $\{p+tv:t\ge0\}$. The open ray omits the origin and uses $t>0$. Positive scalar multiples of $v$ determine the same ray.
