The \emph{radius} $r$ of a Euclidean circle or sphere centered at $c$ is the nonnegative distance from $c$ to each point on it, so the locus is $\{x:\|x-c\|=r\}$. For a nondegenerate circle or sphere $r>0$; a radius is also any segment joining its center to a point on the locus.
