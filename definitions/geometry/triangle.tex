A \emph{nondegenerate (filled) triangle} in a Euclidean plane is the convex hull of three noncollinear points $a,b,c$. Its boundary consists of the three line segments $[a,b]$, $[b,c]$ and $[c,a]$. Some usages mean only this boundary by \emph{triangle}; noncollinearity ensures positive area.
