For nonzero vectors $u,v$ in a real inner-product space, the \emph{unoriented angle} between them is the unique $\theta\in[0,\pi]$ satisfying
\[
\cos\theta=\frac{\langle u,v\rangle}{\|u\|\,\|v\|}.
\]
The quotient lies in $[-1,1]$ by Cauchy--Schwarz. Zero vectors have no angle under this definition.
