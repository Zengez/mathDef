Two affine lines in a real affine space are \emph{parallel in the direction-space sense} if their one-dimensional direction subspaces coincide. In the Euclidean plane this means that they either coincide or are disjoint. Some elementary geometry texts require distinct lines and therefore exclude coincident lines.
