% term: Closed convex hull
% related: geometry.convex-hull, topology.closure
% source: https://encyclopediaofmath.org/index.php?title=Convex_hull
% status: draft

For a subset $A$ of a topological vector space $V$, its \emph{closed convex hull} is the intersection of all closed convex subsets of $V$ containing $A$. Equivalently, it is the topological closure of the convex hull of $A$ in $V$.
