For a subset $S$ of a vector space $V$ over $F$, its \emph{linear span} is $\operatorname{span}_F(S)$, the set of all finite $F$-linear combinations of elements of $S$, equivalently the smallest linear subspace containing $S$. By convention, $\operatorname{span}(\varnothing)=\{0\}$.
