For a vector space $V$ over a field $F$, a \emph{Hamel basis} $B\subseteq V$ is a linearly independent subset whose linear span is $V$. Every vector has a unique expression as a \emph{finite} $F$-linear combination of elements of $B$. This differs from a Schauder basis, which permits convergent infinite series.
