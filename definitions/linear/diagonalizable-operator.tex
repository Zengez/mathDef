% status: draft

An endomorphism $T:V\to V$ of a finite-dimensional vector space over a field $F$ is \emph{diagonalizable over $F$} if there exists an $F$-basis consisting of eigenvectors of $T$. Diagonalizability depends on the field; extension of scalars may change the answer.
