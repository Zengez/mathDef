An \emph{inner product} on a real vector space is a symmetric bilinear map $\langle\cdot,\cdot\rangle$ with $\langle v,v\rangle>0$ for all $v\ne0$. On a complex vector space it is a positive-definite Hermitian sesquilinear map, with the convention of linearity in either the first or second argument explicitly specified when needed.
