For $T:V\to V$ linear over a field $F$ and an eigenvalue $\lambda\in F$, its \emph{eigenspace} is $E_\lambda(T)=\ker(T-\lambda I)$, a subspace that includes $0$ alongside all eigenvectors of eigenvalue $\lambda$.
