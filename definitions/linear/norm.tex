For a real or complex vector space $V$, a \emph{norm} is a function $\|\cdot\|:V\to[0,\infty)$ satisfying $\|v\|=0\iff v=0$, $\|\alpha v\|=|\alpha|\|v\|$, and $\|v+w\|\le\|v\|+\|w\|$.
