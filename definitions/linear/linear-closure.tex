For a subset $A$ of a topological vector space $V$, the \emph{linear closure} (or \emph{closed linear span}) of $A$ is the closure in $V$ of the vector subspace $\operatorname{span}(A)$ generated by $A$. Equivalently, it is the intersection of all closed vector subspaces of $V$ containing $A$.
