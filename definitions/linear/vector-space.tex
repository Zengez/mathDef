A \emph{vector space} over a field $F$ is an abelian group $(V,+)$ together with scalar multiplication $F\times V\to V$ satisfying $a(u+v)=au+av$, $(a+b)v=av+bv$, $(ab)v=a(bv)$, and $1_Fv=v$, for every $a,b\in F$ and $u,v\in V$.
