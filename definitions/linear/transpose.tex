% status: draft

For an $m\times n$ matrix $A$ over a ring $R$, its \emph{transpose} $A^{\mathsf T}$ is the $n\times m$ matrix $(A^{\mathsf T})_{ij}=a_{ji}$. Over $\mathbb C$, this operation does not conjugate entries, unlike the conjugate transpose.
