For a square matrix $A=(a_{ij})\in M_n(R)$ over a commutative ring $R$, its \emph{determinant} is
\[
\det A=\sum_{\sigma\in S_n}\operatorname{sgn}(\sigma)\prod_{i=1}^{n} a_{i,\sigma(i)}.
\]
