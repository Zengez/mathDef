For an $n\times n$ matrix $A$ over a commutative ring $R$, the \emph{characteristic polynomial} is the monic polynomial $p_A(t)=\det(tI_n-A)\in R[t]$. The alternative convention $\det(A-tI_n)$ differs by $(-1)^n$.
