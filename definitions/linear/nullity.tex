For a linear map $T:V\to W$, the \emph{nullity} of $T$ is
\[
\operatorname{nullity}(T)=\dim(\ker T).
\]
When $V$ is finite-dimensional, the rank--nullity theorem gives
\[
\operatorname{rank}(T)+\operatorname{nullity}(T)=\dim V.
\]
