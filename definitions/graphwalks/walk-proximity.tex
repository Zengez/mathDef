For a walk space $W(G)$, a sub-$\sigma$-algebra $\Sigma_0$ of index sets, and its metric $d_\tau$, a \emph{walk proximity} $P:W(G)^2\times\Sigma_0\to\mathbb R$ depends on restricted walks over each index set $A$ and tends to $0$ as the restricted-walk distance tends to $0$.
