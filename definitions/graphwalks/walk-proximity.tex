Let $\Sigma_0$ be a sub-$\sigma$-algebra of the index-set $\sigma$-algebra $\Sigma$. A \emph{proximity} on the walk space $W(G)$ is a map
\[
P:W(G)\times W(G)\times\Sigma_0\to\mathbb R
\]
such that, for each fixed $A\in\Sigma_0$, $P(w_1,w_2,A)$ depends only on the restricted walks $w_1(A)$ and $w_2(A)$, and is continuous with respect to the restricted-walk metric $d_\tau$. In particular, if $d_\tau(w_n(A),u(A))\to0$, then $P(w_n,u,A)\to0$.
