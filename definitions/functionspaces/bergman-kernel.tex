For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ with Lebesgue volume $dV$, let $A^2(\Omega)$ be the Hilbert space of square-integrable holomorphic functions. Its \emph{Bergman reproducing kernel} $K_\Omega(z,w)$ is holomorphic in $z$, antiholomorphic in $w$, and satisfies
\[
f(z)=\int_\Omega f(w)K_\Omega(z,w)\,dV(w),\qquad f\in A^2(\Omega).
\]
For an orthonormal basis $(e_j)$, $K_\Omega(z,w)=\sum_j e_j(z)\overline{e_j(w)}$, with compact-local convergence. It can vanish identically when $A^2(\Omega)=\{0\}$.
