For $0<p<\infty$, the \emph{analytic Hardy space} $H^p(\mathbb D)$ consists of holomorphic functions $f$ such that
\[
\|f\|_{H^p}=\sup_{0<r<1}\left(\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta\right)^{1/p}<\infty.
\]
For $p\ge1$ this is a norm and for $0<p<1$ it is a quasi-norm. The analogous space $H^\infty$ uses the supremum norm rather than this finite-$p$ expression.
