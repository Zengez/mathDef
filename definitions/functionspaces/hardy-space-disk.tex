For $0<p<\infty$, the \emph{Hardy space} $H^p(\mathbb D)$ comprises holomorphic functions $f$ for which
\[
\sup_{0<r<1}\left(\frac1{2\pi}\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^p\,d\theta\right)^{1/p}<\infty.
\]
For $p\ge1$ the expression is a norm; for $0<p<1$ it defines a quasi-norm.
