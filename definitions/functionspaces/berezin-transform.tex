Let $A^2(\Omega)$ be a nontrivial Bergman Hilbert space with reproducing kernel $K$, and assume $K(z,z)>0$. For a bounded measurable symbol $\varphi$ the \emph{Berezin transform} is
\[
\widetilde{\varphi}(z)=\int_\Omega\varphi(w)\frac{|K(w,z)|^2}{K(z,z)}\,dV(w).
\]
For the normalized kernel function $k_z(w)=K(w,z)/\sqrt{K(z,z)}$, this equals $\langle T_\varphi k_z,k_z\rangle$ when the Hilbert-space inner product is linear in its first argument and $T_\varphi=P(\varphi\cdot)$.
