Let $\mathcal H=A^2(\Omega)$ have Bergman kernel $K$ and $K(z,z)>0$. For a bounded measurable symbol $\varphi$, its \emph{Berezin transform} is
\[
\widetilde\varphi(z)=\frac{1}{K(z,z)}\int_\Omega\varphi(w)|K(w,z)|^2\,dV(w).
\]
Equivalently, $\widetilde\varphi(z)=\langle T_\varphi k_z,k_z\rangle$ for the normalized kernel $k_z$.
