Let $\mathcal H$ be a reproducing-kernel Hilbert space of functions on $X$, with kernel $K$. Its \emph{kernel section at $w$} is $K_w=K(\cdot,w)\in\mathcal H$, characterized by the evaluation identity
\[
f(w)=\langle f,K_w\rangle_{\mathcal H},\qquad f\in\mathcal H,
\]
using the convention that the inner product is linear in its first variable. One has $\|K_w\|_{\mathcal H}^2=K(w,w)$.
