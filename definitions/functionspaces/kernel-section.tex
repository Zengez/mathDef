For a reproducing kernel $K$ of a Hilbert function space on $X$, the \emph{kernel section} at $w\in X$ is $K_w=K(\cdot,w)\in\mathcal H$. It represents evaluation at $w$ by $f(w)=\langle f,K_w\rangle$.
