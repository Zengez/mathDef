For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ and $0<p<\infty$, the \emph{Bergman space} is
\[
A^p(\Omega)=\left\{f\in\mathcal O(\Omega):\int_\Omega|f(z)|^p\,dV(z)<\infty\right\}.
\]
Here $dV$ is the unweighted Euclidean volume measure. The functional $(\int|f|^p)^{1/p}$ is a norm for $p\ge1$, and a quasi-norm for $0<p<1$. In particular, $A^2(\Omega)$ is a reproducing-kernel Hilbert space.
