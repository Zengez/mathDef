For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ and $0<p<\infty$, its \emph{Bergman space} is
\[
A^p(\Omega)=\left\{f\in\mathcal O(\Omega):\int_\Omega |f(z)|^p\,dV(z)<\infty\right\}.
\]
Here $dV$ is Euclidean Lebesgue volume. The expression $(\int|f|^p)^{1/p}$ is a norm for $p\ge1$ and a quasi-norm for $0<p<1$.
