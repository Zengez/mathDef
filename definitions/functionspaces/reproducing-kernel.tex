For an RKHS $\mathcal H$ on $X$, the \emph{reproducing kernel} $K:X\times X\to\mathbb C$ is uniquely characterized by $K(\cdot,y)\in\mathcal H$ and
\[
f(y)=\langle f,K(\cdot,y)\rangle_{\mathcal H}\qquad(f\in\mathcal H).
\]
The inner product is taken linear in its first argument. Consequently $K(y,x)=\overline{K(x,y)}$, $K(x,x)\ge0$, and every finite matrix $(K(x_i,x_j))$ is positive semidefinite.
