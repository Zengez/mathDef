For a reproducing-kernel Hilbert space $\mathcal H$ on $X$, the \emph{reproducing kernel} is the function $K:X\times X\to\mathbb C$ such that $K(\cdot,y)\in\mathcal H$ and
\[
f(y)=\langle f,K(\cdot,y)\rangle_{\mathcal H},\qquad f\in\mathcal H,
\]
where the inner product is linear in its first argument.
