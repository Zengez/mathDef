For $n\ge1$, the \emph{Segal--Bargmann} or \emph{Bargmann--Fock space} $\mathcal F^2(\mathbb C^n)$ is the Hilbert space of entire functions $f$ satisfying
\[
\|f\|_{\mathcal F^2}^2=\frac1{\pi^n}\int_{\mathbb C^n}|f(z)|^2e^{-|z|^2}\,dV(z)<\infty.
\]
This specific Gaussian normalization gives reproducing kernel $K(z,w)=e^{\sum_jz_j\overline{w_j}}$.
