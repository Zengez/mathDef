For $n\ge1$, the \emph{Segal–Bargmann space} $\mathcal F^2(\mathbb C^n)$ is the Hilbert space of entire $f$ with
\[
\|f\|^2=\pi^{-n}\int_{\mathbb C^n}|f(z)|^2e^{-|z|^2}\,dV(z)<\infty.
\]
It is also called a holomorphic Bargmann–Fock space, with this Gaussian normalization.
