Let $K$ be the reproducing kernel of a Hilbert function space $\mathcal H$. At a point $w$ with $K(w,w)>0$, its \emph{normalized kernel section} is
\[
k_w=\frac{K(\cdot,w)}{\sqrt{K(w,w)}}\in\mathcal H.
\]
It has unit Hilbert norm, since $\|K(\cdot,w)\|_{\mathcal H}^2=K(w,w)$. At a point with $K(w,w)=0$, this normalization is not defined.
