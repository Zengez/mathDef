For a reproducing kernel $K$ with $K(w,w)>0$, the \emph{normalized kernel section} at $w$ is $k_w=K(\cdot,w)/\sqrt{K(w,w)}$. It satisfies $\|k_w\|_{\mathcal H}=1$.
