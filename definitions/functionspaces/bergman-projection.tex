For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{Bergman projection} $P_\Omega:L^2(\Omega,dV)\to A^2(\Omega)$ is the orthogonal projection onto the closed subspace of square-integrable holomorphic functions. It is represented pointwise by
\[
P_\Omega f(z)=\int_\Omega K_\Omega(z,w)f(w)\,dV(w),
\]
where the integral is understood as the $L^2$ inner product of $f$ with the appropriate kernel section. The projection is bounded on $L^2$ with norm at most $1$.
