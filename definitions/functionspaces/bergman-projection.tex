For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{Bergman projection} $P_\Omega:L^2(\Omega,dV)\to A^2(\Omega)$ is the orthogonal projection. In terms of the Bergman kernel,
\[
P_\Omega f(z)=\int_\Omega K_\Omega(z,w)f(w)\,dV(w).
\]
