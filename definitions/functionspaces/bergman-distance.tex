For a bounded connected domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ with Bergman metric $g$, the \emph{Bergman distance} between $p,q\in\Omega$ is
\[
d_B(p,q)=\inf_\gamma\int_0^1\sqrt{g_{\gamma(t)}(\dot\gamma(t),\dot\gamma(t))}\,dt,
\]
where the infimum ranges over piecewise $C^1$ curves $\gamma$ joining $p$ to $q$.
