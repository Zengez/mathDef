Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ be a bounded connected domain carrying its positive-definite Bergman metric $g$. The \emph{Bergman distance} is the intrinsic path distance
\[
d_B(p,q)=\inf_\gamma\int_0^1\sqrt{\sum_{j,k}g_{j\bar k}(\gamma(t))\dot\gamma_j(t)\overline{\dot\gamma_k(t)}}\,dt,
\]
where $\gamma$ ranges over piecewise $C^1$ paths in $\Omega$ joining $p$ to $q$. A convention multiplying the Bergman metric by a constant also rescales this distance.
