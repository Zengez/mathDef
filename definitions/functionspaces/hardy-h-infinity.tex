The space $H^\infty(\mathbb D)$ consists of all bounded holomorphic functions $f:\mathbb D\to\mathbb C$. With
\[
\|f\|_\infty=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}|f(z)|,
\]
it is a commutative Banach algebra under pointwise operations. Its elements need not extend continuously to the unit circle.
