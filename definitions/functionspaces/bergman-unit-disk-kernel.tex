For the disk $\mathbb D=\{z:|z|<1\}$ with ordinary Euclidean area measure $dA=dx\,dy$, the \emph{Bergman kernel} is
\[
K_{\mathbb D}(z,w)=\frac{1}{\pi(1-z\overline w)^2}.
\]
With normalized area $dA/\pi$ instead, its reproducing kernel is $(1-z\overline w)^{-2}$. These formulas differ because the reproducing kernel depends on the normalization of the underlying Hilbert-space measure.
