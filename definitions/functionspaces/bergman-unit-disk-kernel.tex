On the unit disk $\mathbb D$ with ordinary planar Lebesgue area $dA$, the Bergman kernel is
\[
K_{\mathbb D}(z,w)=\frac{1}{\pi(1-z\bar w)^2}.
\]
If instead the area measure is normalized to $dA/\pi$, the factor $1/\pi$ is omitted.
