For $H^2(\mathbb D)$ with normalized boundary measure $d\theta/(2\pi)$, the \emph{Szegő kernel} is
\[
S(z,w)=\frac{1}{1-z\bar w},\qquad z,w\in\mathbb D.
\]
It satisfies $f(w)=\langle f,S(\cdot,w)\rangle_{H^2}$.
