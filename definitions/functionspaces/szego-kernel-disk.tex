For $H^2(\mathbb D)$ with norm $\|f\|_{H^2}^2=\sum_{n\ge0}|a_n|^2$ when $f(z)=\sum a_nz^n$, equivalently with normalized circle measure $d\theta/(2\pi)$, the \emph{Szegő kernel} is
\[
S(z,w)=\frac1{1-z\overline w}.
\]
It reproduces evaluations: $f(w)=\langle f,S(\cdot,w)\rangle_{H^2}$. This is distinct from the Bergman kernel $(1-z\overline w)^{-2}$ for normalized planar area.
