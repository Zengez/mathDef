For $\alpha>-1$, let $dA_\alpha(z)=\frac{\alpha+1}{\pi}(1-|z|^2)^\alpha\,dA(z)$ on $\mathbb D$. The \emph{standard weighted Bergman space} $A_\alpha^2(\mathbb D)$ consists of holomorphic $f$ with $\int_{\mathbb D}|f|^2\,dA_\alpha<\infty$.
