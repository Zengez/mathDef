For a parameter $\alpha>-1$, define the normalized weighted area measure on $\mathbb D$ by
\[
dA_\alpha(z)=\frac{\alpha+1}{\pi}(1-|z|^2)^\alpha\,dA(z),
\]
where $dA=dx\,dy$ denotes ordinary area. The \emph{standard weighted Bergman space} $A_\alpha^2(\mathbb D)$ is the Hilbert space of holomorphic $f$ with $\int_{\mathbb D}|f|^2\,dA_\alpha<\infty$. The measure has total mass $1$ for $\alpha>-1$.
