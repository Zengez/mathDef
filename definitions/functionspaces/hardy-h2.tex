The \emph{Hardy Hilbert space} $H^2(\mathbb D)$ consists of holomorphic $f(z)=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_nz^n$ satisfying $\sum_n|a_n|^2<\infty$. For $g(z)=\sum_nb_nz^n$ its inner product, linear in the first argument, is
\[
\langle f,g\rangle_{H^2}=\sum_{n=0}^\infty a_n\overline{b_n}.
\]
Equivalently $\|f\|_{H^2}^2=\sup_{0<r<1}(2\pi)^{-1}\int_0^{2\pi}|f(re^{i\theta})|^2\,d\theta$.
