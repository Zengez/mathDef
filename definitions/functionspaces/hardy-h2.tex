The Hilbert Hardy space $H^2(\mathbb D)$ consists of holomorphic $f(z)=\sum_{n\ge0}a_nz^n$ satisfying $\sum_{n\ge0}|a_n|^2<\infty$, with inner product $\langle f,g\rangle=\sum_n a_n\overline{b_n}$ for $g(z)=\sum_n b_nz^n$.
