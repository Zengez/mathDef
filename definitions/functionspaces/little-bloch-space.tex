The \emph{little Bloch space} $\mathcal B_0(\mathbb D)$ is the subspace of Bloch functions $f$ satisfying
\[
\lim_{|z|\to1^-}(1-|z|^2)|f'(z)|=0.
\]
It is a closed subspace of the Bloch Banach space in the norm $|f(0)|+\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|f'(z)|$. The limit condition is stronger than merely having finite Bloch seminorm.
