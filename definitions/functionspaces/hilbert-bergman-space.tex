For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{Hilbert Bergman space} $A^2(\Omega)=\mathcal O(\Omega)\cap L^2(\Omega,dV)$ has inner product $\langle f,g\rangle=\int_\Omega f(z)\overline{g(z)}\,dV(z)$. It is a closed subspace of $L^2(\Omega)$.
