For a domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$, the \emph{Hilbert Bergman space}
\[
A^2(\Omega)=\{f\in\mathcal O(\Omega):\int_\Omega|f|^2\,dV<\infty\}
\]
has inner product $\langle f,g\rangle=\int_\Omega f\overline g\,dV$, linear in the first argument. It is a closed subspace of $L^2(\Omega,dV)$ because point evaluations of square-integrable holomorphic functions are locally bounded by their $L^2$ norms. It may equal $\{0\}$ on some unbounded domains.
