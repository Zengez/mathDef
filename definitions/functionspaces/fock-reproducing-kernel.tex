For the Segal--Bargmann or Bargmann--Fock Hilbert space of entire functions $f$ on $\mathbb C^n$ with inner product from $\pi^{-n}e^{-|z|^2}dV(z)$, the \emph{reproducing kernel} is
\[
K(z,w)=e^{z\cdot\overline w}=\exp\left(\sum_{j=1}^n z_j\overline{w_j}\right).
\]
This exact expression depends on the Gaussian normalization and changes with a different weight parameter.
