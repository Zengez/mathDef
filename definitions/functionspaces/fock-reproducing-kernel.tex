For the Segal–Bargmann Hilbert space with measure $\pi^{-n}e^{-|z|^2}dV(z)$, the \emph{reproducing kernel} is
\[
K(z,w)=\exp\left(\sum_{j=1}^n z_j\bar w_j\right).
\]
