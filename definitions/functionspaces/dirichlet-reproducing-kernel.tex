% term: Reproducing kernel of the Dirichlet space

In the analytic Dirichlet space $\mathcal D(\mathbb D)$ with inner product induced by $\|f\|_{\mathcal D}^2=|f(0)|^2+\pi^{-1}\int_{\mathbb D}|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z)$, the \emph{reproducing kernel} is
\[
K_{\mathcal D}(z,w)=1+\log\frac1{1-z\overline w}
=1+\sum_{n=1}^\infty\frac{(z\overline w)^n}{n}.
\]
The logarithm is specified by its convergent power series. Changing to a different equivalent Dirichlet-space norm changes the reproducing kernel; for instance, the norm with coefficient weights $n+1$ gives a different formula.
