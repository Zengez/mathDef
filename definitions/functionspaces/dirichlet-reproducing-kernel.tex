For $\mathcal D(\mathbb D)$ with norm $|f(0)|^2+\pi^{-1}\int_{\mathbb D}|f'|^2\,dA$, its reproducing kernel is
\[
K_{\mathcal D}(z,w)=1+\log\frac{1}{1-z\bar w},
\]
with the analytic logarithm given by its power series on the unit disk.
