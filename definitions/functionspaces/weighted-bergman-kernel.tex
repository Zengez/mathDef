Fix $\alpha>-1$ and equip $\mathbb D$ with normalized weighted area $dA_\alpha(z)=(\alpha+1)\pi^{-1}(1-|z|^2)^\alpha\,dA(z)$, where $dA=dx\,dy$. The \emph{reproducing kernel} of the standard weighted Bergman Hilbert space $A_\alpha^2(\mathbb D)$ is
\[
K_\alpha(z,w)=\frac1{(1-z\overline w)^{\alpha+2}}.
\]
Changing the normalization constant in $dA_\alpha$ changes the coefficient in front of this kernel. The power is defined by the analytic branch obtained from its series in $z\overline w$.
