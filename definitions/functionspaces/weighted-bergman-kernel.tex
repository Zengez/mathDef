For $\alpha>-1$, the reproducing kernel of $A_\alpha^2(\mathbb D)$ with normalized measure $dA_\alpha=\frac{\alpha+1}{\pi}(1-|z|^2)^\alpha dA$ is
\[
K_\alpha(z,w)=\frac{1}{(1-z\bar w)^{\alpha+2}}.
\]
