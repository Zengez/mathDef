% term: Hankel operator on a Bergman space

% status: draft

Let $P_\Omega:L^2(\Omega)\to A^2(\Omega)$ be the Bergman projection and let $\varphi\in L^\infty(\Omega)$. The \emph{big Hankel operator} with symbol $\varphi$ is the bounded linear map
\[
H_\varphi:A^2(\Omega)\to A^2(\Omega)^\perp,\qquad
H_\varphi f=(I-P_\Omega)(\varphi f).
\]
This Bergman-space construction should not be confused with the distinct Hankel operator on Hardy spaces or with the small Hankel operator.
