For bounded measurable $\varphi$ on $\Omega$, the (big) \emph{Bergman Hankel operator} is $H_\varphi:A^2(\Omega)\to L^2(\Omega)\ominus A^2(\Omega)$, $H_\varphi f=(I-P_\Omega)(\varphi f)$.
