For a bounded domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ with Bergman kernel $K_\Omega$, the \emph{Bergman metric} is the Hermitian metric with coefficient matrix
\[
g_{j\bar k}(z)=\frac{\partial^2}{\partial z_j\,\partial\bar z_k}\log K_\Omega(z,z).
\]
For tangent $\xi$, its squared norm is $\sum_{j,k}g_{j\bar k}(z)\xi_j\overline{\xi_k}$.
