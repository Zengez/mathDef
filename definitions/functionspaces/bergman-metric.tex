For a bounded domain $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ with Bergman kernel $K_\Omega$, the \emph{Bergman metric} is the Hermitian metric with coefficients
\[
g_{j\bar k}(z)=\frac{\partial^2\log K_\Omega(z,z)}{\partial z_j\,\partial\bar z_k}.
\]
It is positive definite on a bounded domain and is invariant under biholomorphic coordinate changes. The Hermitian squared length of a tangent vector $\xi$ is $\sum_{j,k}g_{j\bar k}\xi_j\overline{\xi_k}$. Some authors use a constant multiple of this metric.
