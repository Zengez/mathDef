For a domain $\Omega$ and $a\in\Omega$ with $K_\Omega(a,a)>0$, the \emph{Bergman extremal function} normalized to take the value $1$ at $a$ is
\[
e_a(z)=\frac{K_\Omega(z,a)}{K_\Omega(a,a)}.
\]
It is the unique $L^2$-norm minimizer among all $f\in A^2(\Omega)$ with $f(a)=1$, and $\|e_a\|_2=K_\Omega(a,a)^{-1/2}$.
