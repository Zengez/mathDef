For $\Omega$ with $K_\Omega(a,a)>0$, the \emph{Bergman extremal function} with value $1$ at $a$ is $e_a(z)=K_\Omega(z,a)/K_\Omega(a,a)$. Among $f\in A^2(\Omega)$ satisfying $f(a)=1$, it minimizes the $L^2$ norm, equal to $K_\Omega(a,a)^{-1/2}$.
