For a domain $\Omega$ and bounded measurable $\varphi:\Omega\to\mathbb C$, the \emph{Bergman Toeplitz operator} $T_\varphi:A^2(\Omega)\to A^2(\Omega)$ is $T_\varphi f=P_\Omega(\varphi f)$, where $P_\Omega$ is the Bergman projection.
