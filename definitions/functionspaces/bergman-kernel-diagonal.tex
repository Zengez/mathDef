For a domain $\Omega$ with Bergman reproducing kernel $K_\Omega$, the \emph{diagonal Bergman kernel} is
\[
B_\Omega(z)=K_\Omega(z,z)=\sup\bigl\{|f(z)|^2:f\in A^2(\Omega),\ \|f\|_2\le1\bigr\}.
\]
It is nonnegative. On domains with no nonzero square-integrable holomorphic functions, the Bergman kernel and its diagonal vanish identically; taking $\log B_\Omega$ there is not meaningful.
