For a domain $\Omega$ with Bergman kernel $K_\Omega$, its \emph{diagonal kernel} is $B_\Omega(z)=K_\Omega(z,z)=\sup\{|f(z)|^2:f\in A^2(\Omega),\,\|f\|_2\le1\}$. It is nonnegative and may vanish identically on domains with $A^2(\Omega)=\{0\}$.
