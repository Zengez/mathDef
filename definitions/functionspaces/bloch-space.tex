The \emph{Bloch space} $\mathcal B$ is the space of holomorphic functions $f$ on $\mathbb D$ for which
\[
\beta(f)=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|f'(z)|<\infty.
\]
It becomes a Banach space with norm $\|f\|_{\mathcal B}=|f(0)|+\beta(f)$.
