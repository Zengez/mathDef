The \emph{Bloch space} $\mathcal B(\mathbb D)$ comprises holomorphic functions $f$ on $\mathbb D$ for which
\[
\beta(f)=\sup_{z\in\mathbb D}(1-|z|^2)|f'(z)|<\infty.
\]
The expression $\beta$ is a seminorm, since constants have seminorm zero. The norm $\|f\|_{\mathcal B}=|f(0)|+\beta(f)$ makes $\mathcal B(\mathbb D)$ a Banach space.
