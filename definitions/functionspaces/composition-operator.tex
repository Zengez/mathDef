For a function space $\mathcal H$ on a set $\Omega$ and a map $\varphi:\Omega\to\Omega$, the \emph{composition operator} is
\[
C_\varphi f=f\circ\varphi
\]
on its natural domain $\{f\in\mathcal H:f\circ\varphi\in\mathcal H\}$. The operator is linear; it need not be bounded or everywhere defined on $\mathcal H$. For spaces of holomorphic functions, $\varphi$ is normally assumed holomorphic.
