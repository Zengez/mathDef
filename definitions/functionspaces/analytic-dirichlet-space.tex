The \emph{analytic Dirichlet space} $\mathcal D(\mathbb D)$ is the Hilbert space of holomorphic functions $f$ on the unit disk satisfying $\int_{\mathbb D}|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z)<\infty$. With unnormalized Euclidean area $dA$ one common norm is
\[
\|f\|_{\mathcal D}^2=|f(0)|^2+\frac1\pi\int_{\mathbb D}|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z).
\]
The derivative seminorm alone vanishes on constants, hence the value-at-zero term is needed for a norm.
