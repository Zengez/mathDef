The \emph{analytic Dirichlet space} $\mathcal D(\mathbb D)$ consists of holomorphic $f$ on $\mathbb D$ with $\int_{\mathbb D}|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z)<\infty$, equipped with
\[
\|f\|_{\mathcal D}^2=|f(0)|^2+\frac1\pi\int_{\mathbb D}|f'(z)|^2\,dA(z).
\]
