Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ and let $\omega:\Omega\to(0,\infty)$ be continuous. The \emph{weighted Bergman space} $A_\omega^p(\Omega)$ for $0<p<\infty$ consists of all holomorphic $f$ with $\int_\Omega |f(z)|^p\omega(z)\,dV(z)<\infty$. For $p=2$, it is a Hilbert space with weighted $L^2$ inner product.
