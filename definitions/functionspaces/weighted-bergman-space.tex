Let $\Omega\subseteq\mathbb C^n$ be a domain and $\omega:\Omega\to(0,\infty)$ a continuous weight. For $0<p<\infty$, the \emph{weighted Bergman space} is
\[
A_\omega^p(\Omega)=\left\{f\in\mathcal O(\Omega):\int_\Omega |f(z)|^p\omega(z)\,dV(z)<\infty\right\}.
\]
For $p\ge1$ the weighted $L^p$ expression yields a norm, while for $0<p<1$ it is a quasi-norm. When $p=2$, the weighted $L^2$ inner product makes $A_\omega^2$ a Hilbert space with bounded point evaluations, hence a reproducing kernel.
