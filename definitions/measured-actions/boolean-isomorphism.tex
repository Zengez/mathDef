A \emph{Boolean isomorphism} between two Boolean measure-preserving $G$-actions is a measure-preserving Borel injection $\Phi:X_0\to Y$ from a conull set, with $\Phi(\alpha_gx)=\beta_g\Phi(x)$ on a possibly $g$-dependent conull subset of $X_0$.
