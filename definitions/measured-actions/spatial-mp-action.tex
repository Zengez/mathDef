For a Polish group $G$ and standard Borel $\sigma$-finite measured space $(X,\lambda)$, a \emph{spatial measure-preserving action} is a Borel action $\alpha:G\times X\to X$ whose transformations $\alpha_g$ are measure-preserving Borel bijections.
