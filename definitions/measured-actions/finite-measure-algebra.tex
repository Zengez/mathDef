For $(X,\lambda)$, identify finite-measure Borel subsets whenever their symmetric difference is null. Their equivalence classes form $\operatorname{MAlg}_f(X,\lambda)$, equipped with $d_\lambda([A],[B])=\lambda(A\triangle B)$.
