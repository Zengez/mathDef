A \emph{spatial isomorphism} between measure-preserving $G$-actions is an equivariant measure-preserving Borel injection $\Phi:X_0\to Y$, with $X_0$ a $G$-invariant conull Borel set; equivariance holds for all $g$ and all $x\in X_0$.
