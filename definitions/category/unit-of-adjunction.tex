Given an adjunction $F:\mathcal C\rightleftarrows\mathcal D:G$ with $F\dashv G$, its \emph{unit} is the natural transformation $\eta:1_{\mathcal C}\Rightarrow G F$ whose component $\eta_X:X\to G F X$ corresponds to $1_{F X}$ under the natural bijection $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(F X,Y)\cong\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,GY)$ at $Y=F X$. With the counit $\varepsilon:FG\Rightarrow1_{\mathcal D}$ it obeys the triangle identities.
