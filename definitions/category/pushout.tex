Given a span $B\xleftarrow{f}A\xrightarrow{g}C$, a \emph{pushout} consists of an object $P$ and arrows $i_B:B\to P$, $i_C:C\to P$ with $i_B f=i_Cg$. For any arrows $b:B\to X$, $c:C\to X$ with $bf=cg$, there is a unique $u:P\to X$ with $u i_B=b$ and $u i_C=c$. This is the colimit of the span, dual to a pullback.
