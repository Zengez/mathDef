A category $\mathcal C$ is \emph{small} if both its collection of objects and its collection of all morphisms are sets, rather than proper classes. A category is \emph{locally small} if each individual hom-collection $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,Y)$ is a set. Thus every small category is locally small, but a locally small category may have a proper class of objects.
