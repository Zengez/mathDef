For $F,G:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$, a \emph{natural transformation} $\eta:F\Rightarrow G$ consists of $\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)$ such that $G(f)\eta_X=\eta_YF(f)$ for every $f:X\to Y$.
