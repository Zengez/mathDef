For covariant functors $F,G:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$, a \emph{natural transformation} $\eta:F\Rightarrow G$ assigns to every object $X$ a morphism $\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)$ such that for every arrow $f:X\to Y$ the naturality square commutes:
\[
G(f)\circ\eta_X=\eta_Y\circ F(f).
\]
Natural transformations are the morphisms in the functor category $[\mathcal C,\mathcal D]$.
