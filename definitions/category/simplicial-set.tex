A \emph{simplicial set} is a contravariant functor $X:\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$, where $\Delta$ is the category of nonempty finite ordinals and order-preserving maps. Equivalently, it is a family of sets with face and degeneracy maps satisfying the simplicial identities.
