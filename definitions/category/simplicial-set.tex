A \emph{simplicial set} is a covariant functor $X:\Delta^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$, equivalently a contravariant functor from the simplex category $\Delta$ of nonempty finite ordinals $[n]=\{0,\ldots,n\}$ and order-preserving maps. Concretely, it is a family of sets $X_n=X([n])$, equipped with face maps $d_i:X_n\to X_{n-1}$ and degeneracy maps $s_i:X_n\to X_{n+1}$ satisfying the simplicial identities.
