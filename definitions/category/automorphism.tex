For an object $X$ of a category $\mathcal C$, an \emph{automorphism} is an isomorphism $f:X\to X$, meaning an endomorphism admitting an inverse endomorphism $g:X\to X$ with $gf=fg=1_X$. The automorphisms of $X$ form a group under composition, denoted $\operatorname{Aut}_{\mathcal C}(X)$.
