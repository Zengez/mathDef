% id: category.limit
% term: Limit of a diagram
% related: category.functor, category.colimit, category.product
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/category%2Btheory
% status: draft

For a functor $F:J\to\mathcal C$, a \emph{limit} is a universal cone $\Delta L\Rightarrow F$: every cone $\Delta X\Rightarrow F$ factors uniquely through it.
