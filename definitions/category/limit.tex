For a diagram $F:J\to\mathcal C$ with small index category $J$, a \emph{limit} is an object $L$ with a cone $\lambda:\Delta L\Rightarrow F$ such that for every cone $\mu:\Delta X\Rightarrow F$ there exists a unique morphism $u:X\to L$ satisfying $\mu=\lambda\circ(\Delta u)$. Thus a limit, when it exists, is the terminal object of the category of cones over $F$.
