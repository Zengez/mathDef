A \emph{binary product} of $A$ and $B$ in a category is an object $P$ with projections $p_A:P\to A$ and $p_B:P\to B$ such that for every pair of arrows $f:X\to A$, $g:X\to B$, there is a unique arrow $u:X\to P$ with $p_A\circ u=f$ and $p_B\circ u=g$. When it exists, the product is unique up to a unique compatible isomorphism.
