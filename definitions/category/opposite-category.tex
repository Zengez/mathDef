For a category $\mathcal C$, its \emph{opposite category} $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}$ has the same objects and $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}}(X,Y)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(Y,X)$. If $f:X\to Y$ and $g:Y\to Z$ are arrows in $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}$, then their composite in $\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}$ corresponds to the reverse-order composite in $\mathcal C$. Taking opposites twice recovers $\mathcal C$.
