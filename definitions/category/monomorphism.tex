A morphism $f:A\to B$ is a \emph{monomorphism} if it is left-cancellable: for every object $X$ and all $g,h:X\to A$, the equality $f\circ g=f\circ h$ implies $g=h$. It is not necessarily an injective map of underlying sets unless the category and its concrete interpretation justify that identification.
