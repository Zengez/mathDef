A \emph{groupoid} is a category in which every morphism is invertible. Equivalently, for every arrow $f:X\to Y$ there is an arrow $g:Y\to X$ with $g f=1_X$ and $f g=1_Y$. A group is a groupoid with exactly one object; a groupoid may have many objects and no arrows between some pairs.
