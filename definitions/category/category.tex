A \emph{category} $\mathcal C$ has a collection of objects, collections $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,Y)$ of morphisms for each ordered pair of objects, associative composition maps $\operatorname{Hom}(Y,Z)\times\operatorname{Hom}(X,Y)\to\operatorname{Hom}(X,Z)$, and identity morphisms $1_X$ satisfying $1_Y\circ f=f=f\circ1_X$. In this glossary the hom-collections are assumed to be sets, so the categories under discussion are \emph{locally small}; the collection of objects need not be a set.
