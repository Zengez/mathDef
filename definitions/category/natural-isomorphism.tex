A natural transformation $\eta:F\Rightarrow G$ between functors $F,G:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is a \emph{natural isomorphism} if each component $\eta_X:F(X)\to G(X)$ is an isomorphism in $\mathcal D$. In this case the inverses $(\eta_X)^{-1}$ themselves form a natural transformation $G\Rightarrow F$, which is inverse to $\eta$ in the functor category.
