% id: category.colimit
% term: Colimit of a diagram
% related: category.limit, category.coproduct
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Limit_(category_theory)
% status: draft

For a diagram $F:J\to\mathcal C$ indexed by a small category $J$, a \emph{colimit} of $F$ is an object $L$ equipped with a cocone $\lambda:F\Rightarrow\Delta L$ such that for any other cocone $\mu:F\Rightarrow\Delta X$, there exists a unique morphism $u:L\to X$ with $\mu=(\Delta u)\circ\lambda$. Thus a colimit is an initial object of the category of cocones under $F$, when it exists.
