% id: category.colimit
% term: Colimit of a diagram
% related: category.limit, category.coproduct
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/category%2Btheory
% status: draft

For $F:J\to\mathcal C$, a \emph{colimit} is a universal cocone $F\Rightarrow\Delta L$: every cocone $F\Rightarrow\Delta X$ factors uniquely through it.
