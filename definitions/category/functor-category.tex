For a small category $\mathcal C$ and a locally small category $\mathcal D$, the \emph{functor category} $[\mathcal C,\mathcal D]$ has covariant functors $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ as objects and natural transformations $\alpha:F\Rightarrow G$ as morphisms. Composition is performed componentwise: $(\beta\circ\alpha)_X=\beta_X\circ\alpha_X$. The identity transformation has component $1_{F(X)}$ for each $X\in\mathcal C$.
