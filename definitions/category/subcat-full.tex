A subcategory $\mathcal D\subseteq\mathcal C$ is \emph{full} if, for every pair of its objects $X,Y$, it contains \emph{every} morphism $X\to Y$ of $\mathcal C$, so $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(X,Y)=\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,Y)$. Once an object collection is specified, it determines a unique full subcategory, with all arrows of $\mathcal C$ between those objects.
