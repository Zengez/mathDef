% term: Sieve on an object

% status: draft

A \emph{sieve} $S$ on an object $U$ of a locally small category $\mathcal C$ is a family of morphisms with codomain $U$ closed under precomposition: whenever $f:V\to U$ belongs to $S$ and $g:W\to V$ is any morphism, $f\circ g$ belongs to $S$. Equivalently, it is a subfunctor of the representable presheaf $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(-,U)$.
