An \emph{additive category} is a preadditive category (its morphism sets are abelian groups and composition is bilinear) having a zero object and every binary biproduct. A biproduct of $X,Y$ is simultaneously their product and coproduct, with canonical injections and projections satisfying the usual matrix identities. In particular, the category has finite products and coproducts.
