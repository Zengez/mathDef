For functors $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ and $G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$, an \emph{adjunction} $F\dashv G$ is a family of bijections
\[
\Phi_{X,Y}:\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal D}(F X,Y)\longrightarrow\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,G Y)
\]
natural in $X\in\mathcal C$ and $Y\in\mathcal D$. The functor $F$ is the left adjoint and $G$ the right adjoint. Equivalently, the adjunction consists of a unit and counit satisfying the triangle identities.
