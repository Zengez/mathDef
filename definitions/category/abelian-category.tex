An \emph{abelian category} is an additive category $\mathcal A$ in which every morphism has a kernel and a cokernel, every monomorphism is a kernel of some morphism, and every epimorphism is a cokernel of some morphism. Equivalently, every morphism factors as an epimorphism followed by a monomorphism that identifies its coimage with its image. This definition does not require arbitrary limits or colimits.
