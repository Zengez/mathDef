% term: Covariant functor
% related: category.category, category.natural-transformation
% source: https://ncatlab.org/nlab/show/category%2Btheory
% status: draft

A \emph{functor} $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ assigns objects and morphisms, preserving domains, codomains, identities $F(1_X)=1_{F(X)}$, and composition $F(gf)=F(g)F(f)$.
