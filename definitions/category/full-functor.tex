% term: Full functor
% related: category.functor, category.faithful-functor
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Full_and_faithful_functors
% status: draft

A functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is \emph{full} if, for each pair $X,Y\in\mathcal C$, every morphism $F X\to F Y$ in $\mathcal D$ is $F(f)$ for some morphism $f:X\to Y$. Thus every induced hom-set map is surjective. Fullness alone does not imply faithfulness or essential surjectivity on objects.
