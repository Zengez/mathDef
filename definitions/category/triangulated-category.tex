A \emph{triangulated category} is an additive category $\mathcal T$ equipped with an additive autoequivalence $\Sigma:\mathcal T\to\mathcal T$ (the translation or shift) and a specified class of \emph{distinguished triangles} $X\to Y\to Z\to\Sigma X$. They satisfy Verdier's axioms, including the existence of trivial triangles, completing arrows to triangles, stability under rotation and isomorphism, extension of morphisms to morphisms of triangles, and the octahedral axiom. The chosen triangulation is extra structure on $\mathcal T$.
