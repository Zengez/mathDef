A \emph{triangulated category} is an additive category $\mathcal T$ equipped with an autoequivalence $\Sigma$ and a class of distinguished triangles $X\to Y\to Z\to\Sigma X$ satisfying Verdier's axioms TR1--TR4, including rotation, completion, morphisms of triangles, and the octahedral axiom.
