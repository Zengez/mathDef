A \emph{preadditive category} is a category $\mathcal C$ whose hom-sets $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(X,Y)$ are abelian groups and in which composition is bilinear:
\[
(f+g)\circ h=f\circ h+g\circ h,\qquad k\circ(f+g)=k\circ f+k\circ g
\]
whenever the morphisms compose. A preadditive category need not possess a zero object or finite biproducts; these are additional requirements for an additive category.
