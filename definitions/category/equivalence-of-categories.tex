An \emph{equivalence of categories} is a functor $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ for which there is a functor $G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$ and natural isomorphisms $GF\cong 1_{\mathcal C}$ and $FG\cong 1_{\mathcal D}$. Under usual choice assumptions this is equivalent to $F$ being full, faithful, and essentially surjective on objects. An equivalence need not be a strict isomorphism of categories.
