% term: Equivalence of categories

% status: draft

An \emph{equivalence} $F:\mathcal C\to\mathcal D$ is a functor admitting $G:\mathcal D\to\mathcal C$ with natural isomorphisms $GF\cong1_\mathcal C$ and $FG\cong1_\mathcal D$.
