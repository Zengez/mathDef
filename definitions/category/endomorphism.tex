For an object $X$ of a category $\mathcal C$, an \emph{endomorphism} is a morphism $f:X\to X$. The set $\operatorname{End}_{\mathcal C}(X)$ forms a monoid under composition with identity $1_X$; its invertible elements are precisely the automorphisms of $X$. No invertibility is required for an endomorphism.
