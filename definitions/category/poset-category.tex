A partially ordered set $(P,\le)$ defines a \emph{thin category} whose objects are elements of $P$, with a single morphism $x\to y$ precisely when $x\le y$, and no morphism otherwise. Reflexivity gives identities, transitivity gives composition, and antisymmetry makes the resulting category skeletal. It is a special case of a preorder category.
