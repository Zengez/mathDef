Given parallel morphisms $f,g:A\to B$, an \emph{equalizer} is a morphism $e:E\to A$ satisfying $f\circ e=g\circ e$ such that for any $h:X\to A$ with $f\circ h=g\circ h$, there is a unique $u:X\to E$ such that $e\circ u=h$. If it exists, the equalizer is unique up to a unique isomorphism compatible with its structure map.
