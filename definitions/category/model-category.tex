A \emph{model category} has finite limits and colimits together with weak equivalences, cofibrations and fibrations. Weak equivalences satisfy two-out-of-three; each class is closed under retracts; cofibrations lift against trivial fibrations and trivial cofibrations lift against fibrations; and each map has both prescribed cofibration--trivial-fibration and trivial-cofibration--fibration factorizations. Here \emph{trivial} means also a weak equivalence.
