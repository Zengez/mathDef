For a fixed object $A$ of a locally small category $\mathcal C$, the \emph{covariant Hom functor} $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(A,-):\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$ sends $X$ to $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(A,X)$ and $f:X\to Y$ to postcomposition $g\mapsto f\circ g$. Dually, $\operatorname{Hom}_{\mathcal C}(-,A):\mathcal C^{\mathrm{op}}\to\mathbf{Set}$ sends $f:X\to Y$ to precomposition $\operatorname{Hom}(Y,A)\to\operatorname{Hom}(X,A)$.
