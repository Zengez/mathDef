For an object $A$ of a locally small category $\mathcal C$, the \emph{covariant Hom functor} $\mathcal C(A,-):\mathcal C\to\mathbf{Set}$ sends $X$ to $\mathcal C(A,X)$ and $f:X\to Y$ to postcomposition by $f$.
