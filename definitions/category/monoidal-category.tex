A \emph{monoidal category} is a category $\mathcal C$ with a bifunctor $\otimes:\mathcal C\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$, a unit object $I$, a natural associator $(X\otimes Y)\otimes Z\cong X\otimes(Y\otimes Z)$, and natural left and right unit isomorphisms $I\otimes X\cong X$ and $X\otimes I\cong X$. These satisfy the pentagon and triangle coherence identities. Symmetry or braiding is additional structure, not part of this definition.
