A \emph{monoidal category} is a category $\mathcal C$ equipped with a bifunctor $\otimes:\mathcal C\times\mathcal C\to\mathcal C$, a unit object $I$, and natural associativity and unit isomorphisms satisfying the pentagon and triangle coherence equations.
