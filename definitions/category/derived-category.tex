For an abelian category $\mathcal A$, let $K(\mathcal A)$ denote the homotopy category of cochain complexes over $\mathcal A$. The \emph{unbounded derived category} $D(\mathcal A)$ is its localization at the class of \emph{quasi-isomorphisms}, namely chain maps inducing isomorphisms on all cohomology objects:
\[
D(\mathcal A)=K(\mathcal A)[\mathrm{qis}^{-1}].
\]
The bounded, bounded-below and bounded-above derived categories $D^b,D^+,D^-$ use corresponding restrictions on complexes. Set-theoretic size or enhancement choices may be needed in general categories.
