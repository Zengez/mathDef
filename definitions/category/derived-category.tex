For an abelian category $\mathcal A$, its \emph{derived category} $D(\mathcal A)$ is obtained by formally inverting quasi-isomorphisms in the homotopy category of cochain complexes over $\mathcal A$. A \emph{quasi-isomorphism} is a cochain map inducing an isomorphism on every cohomology object.
