A \emph{Grothendieck topology} assigns to each object $U$ a set $J(U)$ of covering sieves such that the maximal sieve covers $U$, pullbacks of covering sieves are covering, and any sieve whose pullbacks along every arrow of a covering sieve are covering is itself covering.
