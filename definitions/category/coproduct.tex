A \emph{binary coproduct} of objects $A,B$ in a category $\mathcal C$ is an object $A\amalg B$ with arrows $i_A:A\to A\amalg B$ and $i_B:B\to A\amalg B$, such that for any arrows $f:A\to X$ and $g:B\to X$ there is a unique $h:A\amalg B\to X$ with $h i_A=f$ and $h i_B=g$. This is dual to the categorical product and is unique up to a unique compatible isomorphism.
