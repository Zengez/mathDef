For parallel morphisms $f,g:A\to B$ in a category, a \emph{coequalizer} is a morphism $q:B\to Q$ with $q\circ f=q\circ g$ such that for every morphism $h:B\to X$ satisfying $h\circ f=h\circ g$, there is a unique $u:Q\to X$ with $u\circ q=h$. If it exists, this pair $(Q,q)$ is unique up to a unique isomorphism respecting $q$.
