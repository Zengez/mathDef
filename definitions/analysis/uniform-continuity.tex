% id: analysis.uniform-continuity
% term: Uniform continuity
% related: metric.metric-space, functions.function, topology.continuity

A function $f:(X,d_X)\to(Y,d_Y)$ is \emph{uniformly continuous} if
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists\delta>0\
\forall x,x'\in X:\
d_X(x,x')<\delta\implies d_Y(f(x),f(x'))<\varepsilon.
\]

The choice of $\delta$ may depend on $\varepsilon$, but not on the point $x$.
