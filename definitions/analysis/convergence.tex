% term: Convergent sequence
% aliases: Sequence convergence

A sequence $(x_n)$ in a metric space $(X,d)$ \emph{converges} to $x\in X$ if
\[
\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists N\in\mathbb N\
\forall n\ge N:\ d(x_n,x)<\varepsilon.
\]

Equivalently, every neighborhood of $x$ contains all but finitely many terms of the sequence.
