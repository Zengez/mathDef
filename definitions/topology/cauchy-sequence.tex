A sequence $(x_n)$ in a metric space $(X,d)$ is \emph{Cauchy} if for every $\varepsilon>0$ there exists $N\in\mathbb N$ such that $d(x_n,x_m)<\varepsilon$ for all $m,n\ge N$. Every convergent sequence in a metric space is Cauchy; the converse holds exactly when the space is complete.
