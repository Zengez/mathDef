A topological space $X$ is \emph{first-countable} if for each point $x\in X$ there is a countable family $(U_n)$ of neighborhoods of $x$ such that every neighborhood of $x$ contains some $U_n$. In a first-countable space, closure and continuity can be tested using sequences; this need not hold for general topological spaces.
