A topological space $X$ is \emph{Hausdorff} (or $T_2$) if for any distinct points $x,y\in X$ there exist disjoint open subsets $U,V\subseteq X$ with $x\in U$ and $y\in V$. In a Hausdorff space, compact subsets are closed and limits of convergent nets are unique.
