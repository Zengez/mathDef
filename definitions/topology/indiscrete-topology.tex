The \emph{indiscrete topology} on a set $X$ is $\{\varnothing,X\}$ (a singleton collection when $X=\varnothing$). It is the coarsest topology on $X$. Every map into an indiscrete space is continuous, whereas a map from an indiscrete space to another space need not be continuous.
