Given a surjection $q:X\to Y$ from a topological space $X$ onto a set $Y$, the \emph{quotient topology} on $Y$ consists exactly of subsets $V\subseteq Y$ for which $q^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. It is the finest topology on $Y$ making $q$ continuous. With this topology, a map $f:Y\to Z$ is continuous precisely when $f\circ q:X\to Z$ is continuous.
