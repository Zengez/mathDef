A topological space $X$ is \emph{path-connected} if for every pair $x,y\in X$ there is a continuous map $\gamma:[0,1]\to X$ with $\gamma(0)=x$ and $\gamma(1)=y$. Path connectedness implies connectedness, but the converse fails for general spaces. The empty space is path-connected under the literal universal-quantifier convention.
