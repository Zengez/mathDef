For an indexed family of topological spaces $(X_i,\tau_i)_{i\in I}$, the \emph{product topology} on $\prod_{i\in I}X_i$ is the coarsest topology making every coordinate projection $\pi_i:\prod_jX_j\to X_i$ continuous. A basis consists of products $\prod_i U_i$ where each $U_i$ is open in $X_i$ and $U_i=X_i$ for all but finitely many indices. Allowing arbitrary restrictions in infinitely many coordinates would generally give the finer box topology.
