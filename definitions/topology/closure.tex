For a subset $A$ of a topological space $X$, its \emph{closure} $\overline A$ is the smallest closed set containing $A$, equivalently the intersection of all closed subsets containing $A$. A point $x$ lies in $\overline A$ if and only if every open neighborhood of $x$ intersects $A$. No sequential characterization is valid in complete generality without additional countability assumptions.
