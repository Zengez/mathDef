An \emph{open cover} of a subset $A$ of a topological space $X$ is a family $(U_i)_{i\in I}$ of open subsets of $X$ such that $A\subseteq\bigcup_{i\in I}U_i$. A \emph{subcover} is a subfamily still covering $A$. The definition permits repeated members, arbitrary index sets, and the empty family as a cover of $\varnothing$.
