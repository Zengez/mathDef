In a topological space $(X,\tau)$, a subset $U\subseteq X$ is \emph{open} if $U\in\tau$. The topology $\tau$ is a collection of subsets containing $\varnothing$ and $X$, closed under arbitrary unions and finite intersections. Openness is relative to the ambient topology: a subset may be open in a subspace without being open in $X$.
