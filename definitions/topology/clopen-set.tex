A subset $A$ of a topological space $X$ is \emph{clopen} if both $A$ and its complement $X\setminus A$ are open; equivalently, $A$ is both open and closed. The sets $\varnothing$ and $X$ are always clopen. A space is connected exactly when these are its only clopen subsets.
