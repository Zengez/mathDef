A \emph{topological space} is a pair $(X,\tau)$, where $\tau$ is a collection of subsets of $X$ such that $\varnothing,X\in\tau$, arbitrary unions of members of $\tau$ belong to $\tau$, and finite intersections of members of $\tau$ belong to $\tau$.

The members of $\tau$ are the open sets.
