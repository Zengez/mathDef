A \emph{topological space} is a set $X$ equipped with a family $\tau\subseteq\mathcal P(X)$ satisfying $\varnothing,X\in\tau$, closure under arbitrary unions, and closure under finite intersections. Members of $\tau$ are the \emph{open sets}. The topology determines notions such as continuity and convergence but does not necessarily arise from a metric.
