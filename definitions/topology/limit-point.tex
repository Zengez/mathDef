% term: Limit point

% status: draft

A point $x$ in a topological space $X$ is an \emph{accumulation point} (or \emph{limit point}) of a subset $A$ if every open neighborhood $U$ of $x$ contains a point of $A$ different from $x$, that is, $U\cap(A\setminus\{x\})\ne\varnothing$. This is distinct from merely belonging to the closure of $A$; an isolated point of $A$ lies in its closure but is not an accumulation point.
