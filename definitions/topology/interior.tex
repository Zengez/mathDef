For a subset $A$ of a topological space $X$, the \emph{interior} $\operatorname{int}(A)$ is the union of all open subsets of $X$ contained in $A$, hence the largest open subset contained in $A$. A point $x$ is an \emph{interior point} of $A$ if it lies in $\operatorname{int}(A)$, equivalently if there is an open $U$ with $x\in U\subseteq A$.
