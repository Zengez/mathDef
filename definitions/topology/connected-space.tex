A topological space $X$ is \emph{connected} if it cannot be written as the disjoint union of two nonempty open subsets. Equivalently, the only subsets simultaneously open and closed are $\varnothing$ and $X$. The empty space is connected under this definition, and connectedness is weaker than path connectedness.
