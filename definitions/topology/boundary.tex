For a subset $A$ of a topological space $X$, its \emph{boundary} is
\[
\partial A=\overline A\cap\overline{X\setminus A}=\overline A\setminus\operatorname{int}(A).
\]
It consists of points every open neighborhood of which meets both $A$ and $X\setminus A$. The boundary is closed and need not be contained in $A$.
