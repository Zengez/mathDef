% status: draft

A subset $A$ of a topological space $X$ is \emph{nowhere dense} if
\[
\operatorname{int}_X(\overline A^{\,X})=\varnothing.
\]
Equivalently, every nonempty open subset of $X$ contains a nonempty open subset disjoint from $A$. This is a topological property, not a claim that $A$ has measure zero.
