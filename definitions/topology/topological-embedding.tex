A \emph{topological embedding} $f:X\to Y$ is a map for which $f:X\to f(X)$ is a homeomorphism when $f(X)$ has the subspace topology from $Y$. Thus it is injective and continuous, with continuous inverse onto its image. A continuous injection need not be an embedding unless further hypotheses are imposed.
