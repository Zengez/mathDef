% id: topology.continuity
% term: Continuous function (topological)
% aliases: Topological continuity
% related: topology.open-set, topology.topological-space, functions.function
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Continuous_function

A map $f:X\to Y$ between topological spaces is \emph{continuous} if for every open subset $V$ of $Y$, its preimage $f^{-1}(V)$ is open in $X$. Equivalently, preimages of closed subsets of $Y$ are closed in $X$. Continuity concerns inverse images of open sets, not images of open sets; the latter condition defines an open map.
