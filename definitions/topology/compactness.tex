% term: Compact space
% aliases: Compactness

% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Compact_space

A topological space $X$ is \emph{compact} if every open cover $\{U_i:i\in I\}$ of $X$ has a finite subcover: there is a finite $F\subseteq I$ with $X=\bigcup_{i\in F}U_i$. Compactness does not, by definition, include the Hausdorff property. A subset $K\subseteq X$ is compact if it is compact with the subspace topology.
