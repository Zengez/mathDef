For $A\subseteq X$ in a topological space $X$, the \emph{sequential closure} of $A$ is $\operatorname{scl}(A)=\{x\in X:\text{there exists a sequence in }A\text{ converging to }x\}$. It is contained in the topological closure of $A$, but may be strictly smaller; the operation need not be idempotent.
