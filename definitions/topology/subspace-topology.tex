If $(X,\tau)$ is a topological space and $A\subseteq X$, the \emph{subspace topology} on $A$ is $\tau|_A=\{U\cap A:U\in\tau\}$. It is the coarsest topology on $A$ making the inclusion $A\hookrightarrow X$ continuous. A set may be open in $A$ without being open in $X$.
