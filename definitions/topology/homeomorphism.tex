A \emph{homeomorphism} between topological spaces $X,Y$ is a bijection $f:X\to Y$ such that both $f$ and $f^{-1}$ are continuous. Equivalently, it is a bijection that carries the topology of $X$ exactly onto the topology of $Y$. Being bijective and continuous alone does not suffice in arbitrary spaces.
