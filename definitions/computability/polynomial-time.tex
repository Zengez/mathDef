An algorithm is \emph{polynomial-time} relative to a specified input encoding and machine model if there are constants $C>0$, $k\in\mathbb N$ and $n_0$ such that its worst-case number of computation steps on inputs of length $n\ge n_0$ is at most $Cn^k$. A decision algorithm must also be correct and halt on every permitted input.
