The complexity class $\mathbf{EXPTIME}$ is the union $\bigcup_{k\ge1}\operatorname{DTIME}(2^{n^k})$: a decision language belongs to it if a deterministic Turing machine decides membership in time at most $2^{p(n)}$ for some polynomial $p$, up to the usual asymptotic constant factors. Thus EXPTIME permits any fixed polynomial exponent in the running-time exponent.
