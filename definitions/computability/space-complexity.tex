For a deterministic Turing machine $M$ with read-only input and work tapes, its \emph{worst-case space complexity} $S_M(n)$ is the maximum, over inputs of length $n$, of the number of distinct work-tape cells visited during computation. With potentially infinite computations this value may be infinite; for nondeterministic machines the maximum also ranges over branches.
