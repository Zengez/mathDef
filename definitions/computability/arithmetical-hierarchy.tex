The \emph{arithmetical hierarchy} classifies sets $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ by arithmetic formulas. For $n\ge1$, $A\in\Sigma^0_n$ if membership $x\in A$ is definable by a formula with $n$ alternating blocks of unbounded number quantifiers beginning existentially, followed by a decidable predicate; the class $\Pi^0_n$ begins universally. The class $\Delta^0_n=\Sigma^0_n\cap\Pi^0_n$ contains sets with both descriptions. Bounded quantifiers may be absorbed into the decidable predicate. In particular, $\Sigma^0_1$ is the class of computably enumerable sets.
