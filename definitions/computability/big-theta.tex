% id: computability.big-theta
% term: Big-Theta asymptotic notation
% related: computability.big-o, computability.big-omega
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_O_notation
% status: draft

For eventually nonnegative functions $f,g:\mathbb N\to[0,\infty)$, $f(n)=\Theta(g(n))$ if there exist $c,C>0$ and $n_0$ such that
\[
c\,g(n)\le f(n)\le C\,g(n)\qquad(n\ge n_0).
\]
Equivalently, $f(n)=O(g(n))$ and $f(n)=\Omega(g(n))$, with the complexity-theoretic convention for $\Omega$.
