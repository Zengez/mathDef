Fix an optimal universal partial computable machine $U:\{0,1\}^*\rightharpoonup\{0,1\}^*$. The \emph{plain Kolmogorov complexity} of a finite binary string $x$ relative to $U$ is
\[
C_U(x)=\min\{|p|:U(p)=x\},
\]
the length of its shortest generating program. Different optimal machines change this quantity by at most an additive constant independent of $x$. Plain and prefix-free complexities use different program conventions.
