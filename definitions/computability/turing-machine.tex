A \emph{deterministic Turing machine} is a finite-state computational model with a finite tape alphabet, a tape of cells and a read/write head. A transition function prescribes at most one next state, symbol to write and head movement for any current state and scanned symbol. It may halt in an accepting or rejecting state or continue without halting.
