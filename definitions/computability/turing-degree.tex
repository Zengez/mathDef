Sets $A,B\subseteq\mathbb N$ are \emph{Turing equivalent}, written $A\equiv_T B$, if $A\le_T B$ and $B\le_T A$. Their equivalence class $[A]_T$ is the \emph{Turing degree} of $A$. The degrees are partially ordered by $[A]_T\le[B]_T$ when $A\le_T B$; the least degree consists of computable sets.
