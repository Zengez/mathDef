A function $f:\mathbb N\to\mathbb N$ is \emph{total computable} if a Turing machine, given any standard encoding of $n\in\mathbb N$, halts and outputs an encoding of $f(n)$. Allowing nontermination on inputs outside a function's domain instead gives the notion of a partial computable function.
