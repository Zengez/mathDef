A \emph{graph algorithm} is a computational procedure that accepts an encoding of a finite graph, possibly with extra data such as edge weights or designated vertices, and produces an output meeting a graph-theoretic specification. The graph representation (for example, adjacency lists or matrices) forms part of the computational input model and may affect complexity bounds.
