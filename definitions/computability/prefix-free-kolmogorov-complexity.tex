For an optimal universal prefix-free partial computable machine $U$ with prefix-free domain of halting programs, the \emph{prefix-free Kolmogorov complexity} of a finite binary string $x$ is
\[
K_U(x)=\min\{|p|:U(p)=x\}.
\]
Prefix-freeness means no halting program is a proper initial segment of another. Different optimal prefix-free machines alter this complexity by at most a constant independent of $x$. It should not be conflated with plain complexity $C_U(x)$.
