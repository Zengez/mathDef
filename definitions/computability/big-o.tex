% id: computability.big-o
% term: Big-O asymptotic notation
% related: computability.big-theta, computability.time-complexity
% source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Big_O_notation
% status: draft

For functions $f,g:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$ with $g(n)\ge0$ eventually, the assertion $f(n)=O(g(n))$ as $n\to\infty$ means that there are constants $C>0$ and $n_0$ such that $|f(n)|\le Cg(n)$ for all $n\ge n_0$. This is an eventual upper bound on magnitude, not necessarily a limit of $f(n)/g(n)$.
