% term: Big-O asymptotic notation

% status: draft

For eventually nonnegative functions $f,g:\mathbb N\to\mathbb R$, $f(n)=O(g(n))$ if there exist constants $C>0$ and $n_0$ with $f(n)\le Cg(n)$ for all $n\ge n_0$.
