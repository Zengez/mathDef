The class $\mathbf{PSPACE}$ consists of languages decidable by deterministic Turing machines that halt on every input and use at most $p(n)$ work-tape cells on inputs of length $n$ for some polynomial $p$. Read-only input cells are not counted as work space under the standard model; unlike $\mathbf P$, the time spent may be exponential.
