The \emph{polynomial-time hierarchy} is
\[
\mathbf{PH}=\bigcup_{k\ge0}\Sigma_k^{\mathrm P},
\]
where $\Sigma_0^{\mathrm P}=\Pi_0^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf P$. For $k\ge1$, $\Sigma_k^{\mathrm P}$ consists of languages describable with at most $k$ alternating blocks of polynomial-length string quantifiers beginning existentially, applied to a polynomial-time decidable predicate; $\Pi_k^{\mathrm P}$ begins universally. Thus $\Sigma_1^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf{NP}$ and $\Pi_1^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf{coNP}$. The hierarchy is not known to be strict.
