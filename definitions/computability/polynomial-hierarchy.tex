The \emph{polynomial hierarchy} is the union $\mathbf{PH}=\bigcup_{k\ge0}\Sigma_k^{\mathrm P}$ of complexity classes defined by polynomially bounded quantifier alternation over a polynomial-time decidable predicate. For $k\ge1$, $\Sigma_k^{\mathrm P}$ starts with an existential block and has at most $k$ alternating blocks; $\Pi_k^{\mathrm P}$ begins universally. Conventionally $\Sigma_0^{\mathrm P}=\Pi_0^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf P$, $\Sigma_1^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf{NP}$ and $\Pi_1^{\mathrm P}=\mathbf{coNP}$.
