The class $\mathbf{coNP}$ consists of all decision languages $L$ whose complement $\overline L=\{0,1\}^*\setminus L$ belongs to $\mathbf{NP}$. Equivalently, nonmembership in $L$ has polynomial-length certificates verifiable in polynomial time, with an if-and-only-if guarantee. Whether $\mathbf{NP}=\mathbf{coNP}$ is not known.
