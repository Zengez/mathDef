Given a finite alphabet $\Sigma$, a \emph{formal language} over $\Sigma$ is any subset $L\subseteq\Sigma^*$ of the set of all finite strings over $\Sigma$. A language may be finite or infinite and may contain the empty string. No grammar or algorithm deciding membership is required by the definition.
