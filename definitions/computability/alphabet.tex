In the finite-alphabet convention common in automata and complexity theory, a \emph{formal alphabet} $\Sigma$ is a finite nonempty set of symbols. More general treatments allow infinite alphabets. The set $\Sigma^*$ consists of all finite strings over $\Sigma$, including the empty string $\varepsilon$. With concatenation as its operation and $\varepsilon$ as its identity, $\Sigma^*$ is the free monoid generated by $\Sigma$.
