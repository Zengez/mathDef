A real number $x$ is \emph{computable} if there is an algorithm that, on input any integer $n\ge1$, halts and produces a rational number $q_n$ with $|q_n-x|\le2^{-n}$. Equivalently, $x$ has a computable rational approximation with an effective prescribed error bound; merely being the limit of a computable sequence is not sufficient without an effective convergence rate.
