% id: schemes.stalk
% term: Stalk of a sheaf
% related: schemes.sheaf, topology.neighborhood
% source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/0078
% status: draft

For a sheaf $\mathcal F$ on $X$ and $x\in X$, its \emph{stalk} $\mathcal F_x$ is the direct limit $\varinjlim_{x\in U}\mathcal F(U)$ over open neighborhoods $U$ of $x$.
