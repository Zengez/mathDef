% term: Sheaf on a topological space
% related: schemes.presheaf, schemes.stalk

% status: draft

A presheaf $\mathcal F$ is a \emph{sheaf} if compatible sections $s_i\in\mathcal F(U_i)$ over any open cover $U=\bigcup_iU_i$ glue to a unique section $s\in\mathcal F(U)$.
