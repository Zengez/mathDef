% id: schemes.sheaf
% term: Sheaf on a topological space
% related: schemes.presheaf, schemes.stalk
% source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/006S
% status: draft

A presheaf $\mathcal F$ is a \emph{sheaf} if, for every open cover $U=\bigcup_i U_i$, sections $s_i\in\mathcal F(U_i)$ agreeing on each overlap $U_i\cap U_j$ are restrictions of a unique section $s\in\mathcal F(U)$. This includes the empty cover of the empty set.
