For $f:X\to S$ and a point $s\in S$, the \emph{fiber scheme} is $X_s=X\times_S\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)$, where $\kappa(s)=\mathcal O_{S,s}/\mathfrak m_s$ is the residue field at $s$, and $\operatorname{Spec}\kappa(s)\to S$ is its canonical map.
