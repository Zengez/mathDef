% id: schemes.spectrum
% term: Prime spectrum of a ring
% related: algebra.prime-ideal, schemes.zariski-topology
% source: https://stacks.math.columbia.edu/tag/01HR
% status: draft

For a commutative ring $R$, $\operatorname{Spec}R$ is the set of prime ideals of $R$, equipped with the Zariski topology whose closed subsets are $V(I)=\{\mathfrak p:I\subseteq\mathfrak p\}$.
