A \emph{closed subscheme} of $X$ is a scheme $Z$ with a closed immersion $i:Z\hookrightarrow X$; equivalently, it is locally described by surjective ring maps $R\to R/I$ on affine open subsets, with a quasi-coherent sheaf of defining ideals.
