## Important mathematical non-merges and quality issues

The lexical review also inspected tempting pairings that **must remain distinct**:

1. `left translation` versus `right translation` in a nonabelian group (gh and hg need not agree).
2. Real symmetric bilinear forms versus complex Hermitian sesquilinear forms; linearity and conjugate-linearity differ.
3. A **radical ideal** (`I=√I`) versus **the radical `√I` of an arbitrary ideal**. A property of I is not the same as taking an ideal's radical, although they are related.
4. First homology versus cohomology; different differential directions and graded objects.
5. Ascending versus descending chain conditions; neither implies the other in arbitrary posets.
6. Finite discrete support versus countably infinite support and the corresponding convergence/moment hypotheses.
7. Open neighborhoods versus arbitrary neighborhoods; a neighborhood need not itself be open.
8. A natural isomorphism versus the stronger *coherent monoidal comparison data* of a strong monoidal functor; coherence does not follow from bare invertibility.
