1. **Categorical finite presentability:** `Hom_C(X,Y)` may be a proper class in unrestricted categories; using set-valued filtered colimits requires a local-smallness or universe convention. The canonical comparison map here is built from colimit cocone arrows; its bijectivity is a defining condition, not another independently researched target. 2. **Naive cotangent complex / smoothness:** Distinguish chain-complex degrees 1,0 and the source-local induced differential `i mod I² ↦ di ⊗ 1`, versus a reusable quasi-isomorphism concept. Review whether the smoothness statement uses a single quasi-isomorphism or an isomorphism in the derived category (possibly through a zigzag); preserve distinctions between strict isomorphism and quasi-isomorphism.
3. **Snake lemma:** The commuting-square structure is a reusable prerequisite; representing an element of a cokernel by a coset is already covered by the linked cokernel. The choice-independence assertion belongs to the connecting-map construction and should receive later theorem-level verification.
4. **Nagata / universally Japanese:** Existing Japanese/N-2 contextual meaning linked in both related sources; its quantifier scope must not be confused with universally Japanese. Also linked the existing integral-closure-inside-a-specified-ring meaning for closure in finite fraction-field extensions. A prime quotient `R/p` is another occurrence of the shared unresearched general quotient-ring construction; do not count its raw candidate as a new distinct verified mathematical meaning.
5. **Formal unramifiedness:** Square-zero ideals are reusable and strictly more specific than arbitrary nilpotent ideals. A lift `g:S→A` with `qg=f:S→A/I` is a separate reusable map-lifting operation. **At most one** lift is not an existence guarantee; this must remain distinct from formal smoothness and formal étaleness.
6. **Universal closedness:** General closed maps differ from universally closed maps. A topological pullback uses the subspace topology of the product, over and above its abstract categorical fibre-product property; check duplicate candidate identities for `X×_Y X` versus `X×_Y Z`.
7. **Inertia:** An automorphism vertical over a base identity is different from a general endomorphism and from field automorphism groups. Commutation `βα=α′β` specifies the inertia category's morphisms as part of its definition.
8. **Topological coproduct:** A tagged indexed disjoint union of underlying sets must be distinguished from a partition of one set into disjoint pieces. The componentwise final-topology condition is the topology defining this particular coproduct, not an extra independently researched meaning.
