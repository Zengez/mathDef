## Mathematical cautions for continuation

- Bijectivity of the underlying function alone is **not** a
  homeomorphism, graph isomorphism or natural Hom-set equivalence.
- For a standard étale algebra, `f′` is a formal polynomial derivative,
  and its *image* must be invertible in the localized quotient; this
  is the ring's unit concept, not a property of differentiation alone.
- A polynomial splitting over K is not the minimal **splitting field**.
  Galois automorphism evaluation `σ↦σ(a)` is not a polynomial
  evaluation homomorphism. An initial topology is not merely an arbitrary
  topology of a space.
- Ideal extension `IS`, inverse-image contraction `φ⁻¹(J)`,
  prime-ideal lying-over relation, and going-up/going-down *existence
  properties* remain distinct. In particular do not merge going-up
  and going-down just because both use lying-over relations.
- Finite generators of an ideal of a commutative unital ring reuse
  finite generation of the regular R-module, **without** discarding
  source-specific number of generators (e.g. Krull dimension d).
  The ideal `I^n`, its action `I^nM`, and `M/I^nM` are different
  prerequisites.
- A square-zero ideal has `I²=0`, stronger than generic nilpotence.
  A ring homomorphism may have a non-square-zero kernel.
- Do not alter applied immutable packets or archived source documents.
