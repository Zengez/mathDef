Six exact mathematical source pages were inspected directly, and six independently phrased observations were persisted, using the preapproved `src.proofwiki` source registration:

1. [Group](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3AGroup): associative operation, two-sided identity and inverses.
2. [Abelian group](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3AAbelian_Group): a commutative group operation.
3. [Ideal of a ring](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3AIdeal_of_Ring): additive subgroup closed under both sides of multiplication; left/right variants distinguished.
4. [Prime ideal of a commutative unital ring](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3APrime_Ideal_of_Ring/Commutative_and_Unitary_Ring): a **proper** ideal obeying the product-prime condition.
5. [Module over a ring](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3AModule_over_Ring): a left ring action on an abelian group obeying distributivity and associativity. **ProofWiki's general module formulation does not impose `1m=m` universally**; retain unitality as an explicit separate hypothesis in that context.
6. [Topological space](https://proofwiki.org/wiki/Definition%3ATopological_Space): a topology of open subsets satisfying arbitrary-union and finite-intersection axioms.

**Source-quality caution:** ProofWiki is community edited; these six are candidate evidence with attribution/provenance, **not peer-reviewed or source-triangulated screened definitions**. Cross-check exact axioms against separate authoritative references in the next batch.
