## New source-level issue flagged: submersive continuous map

Independent source formulation `form.83a528850cdf6a59.submersive-continuous-map` states a continuous f:X→Y is submersive when it is **surjective** and Y carries the quotient topology for f, then says equivalently “U⊆Y is open exactly when f^{-1}(U) is open in X.” The latter condition alone **does not imply surjectivity**. Example: X={x}, Y={a,b} equipped with discrete topologies and f(x)=a. The correct if-and-only-if characterization must explicitly **retain surjectivity as an assumption** alongside the preimage-open test (or use an explicitly defined quotient-map convention requiring surjectivity).
