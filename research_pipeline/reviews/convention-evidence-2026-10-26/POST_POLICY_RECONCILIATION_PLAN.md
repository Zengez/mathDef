## Priority B — twenty component relationships

Under the approved rule, no additional project-wide decision is needed just
to allow a correctly instantiated general concept as a component prerequisite.

### Likely retain as typed component relations, after exact-live-link check

- **3, 5:** quotient rings and formal polynomial algebras can be component
  prerequisites; extension kernels and evaluation values are *extra data*.
- **36, 38:** pointwise `L¹` integrability and generic clopen predicates
  are reusable, but preserve **real versus discrete index quantifiers** and
  spectrum/idempotent specialization.
- **62, 95:** natural transformations and **additive** direct sums can support
  the parent; Yoneda correspondences and graded-module compatibility are
  additional structure.
- **172, 223, 245, 268:** generic degree-zero extraction,
  group completion, inverse limit **of rings** and chosen finite
  presentation can be components; avoid linking a definition to its own
  specialized whole construction.
- **222, 294, 323:** quotient sheaf versus pushforward; `R/J`
  versus `M/JM`; extended maximal ideal versus residue-field
  comparison; none can be collapsed into one generic quotient/map.
- **324, 405, 420:** unindexed partition blocks, prime-complement
  denominator sets, and the multiplication map `S⊗_R S→S` can be typed
  components, but distinct indexing, localized module, and
  **isomorphism predicate** data must be retained.

### Require particular attention before retaining links or dispositions

- **18:** general ideal products also make sense without commutativity
  or identity; the nilpotent source may have broader coefficient
  conventions than other parents. Do not force one global unital
  ideal-power identity or conflate symbolic with ordinary powers.
- **291:** a generic Lebesgue integral is a prerequisite, but the
  specific monomial value is the already named raw moment; avoid
  self-linking, and verify measure/interval endpoint conventions.
- **298:** the stored annihilator-based zero-divisor predicate
  includes zero in a nonzero ring, whereas the candidate describes
  nonzero zero divisors; source prose may be an explanatory warning
  rather than an intended named dependency. Do **not** alias the
  two predicates. Review whether the current occurrence needs
  a dependency at all.
- **364:** "infinitely many points of increase" needs its original
  measure/distribution convention. Infinitely many jumps and
  strict increase at every point are different conditions; finite
  support creates degenerate high-degree polynomial norms.

The reviewed *mathematical relationships* do not establish that all
twenty current scan dispositions are correct. Before any operational
classification as `retain_current`, compare live existing and missing
references, target variants, source hypotheses and actual contextual
instantiation. If valid, make **no graph change**. When not valid,
prepare an exact, separately reviewed correction proposal.
