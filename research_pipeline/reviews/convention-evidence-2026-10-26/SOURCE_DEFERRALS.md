### Mathematical checks on the corrected scopes

**Filtration:** on Ω={0,1}, let F_t be trivial before t=c and full afterward.
Choices c=1/2 and c=3/4 have identical integer samples but different F_0.6.
Consequently, a real-index filtration is not determined by its discrete
restriction. No such restriction construction is asserted by the source.

**Ext/Hom:** for left R-modules, sums of R-linear maps are R-linear, giving an
abelian group. Pointwise R multiplication need not preserve linearity when R
is noncommutative. In R=M₂(Q), M=N=R with left regular action, f=id and a=E₁₁,
the map g(x)=ax has g(E₁₂·1)=E₁₂ while E₁₂g(1)=0. Thus the corrected
component map dependency is justified without certifying an R-module of Hom.
Precomposition reverses the resolution arrows and yields a cochain complex
of abelian groups; the commutative R-module enrichment needs extra hypotheses.
Old matching-note text mentioning a Hom module is preserved historical
annotation, not evidence that the removed internal-Hom edge remains active.

**Order zero:** D is S-linear exactly when it preserves addition and S action.
This instantiates the existing generic ring-parameter definition. For k→k[t],
d/dt is k-linear but not k[t]-linear: D(t·1)=1, while tD(1)=0. The two resolved
linearity occurrences in the source must keep their different scalar parameters.

**m-primary:** if √I=m in a commutative unital local ring and ab∈I with a∉I,
then b∉m would make b a unit, forcing a∈I. Hence b∈m=√I and bⁿ∈I for some
n>0; also I is proper. The converse radical condition is included in m-primary.
The recorded Noetherian scope is preserved although this implication does not
need Noetherianity. The whole system of parameters adds a dimension-sized
generating sequence and is not the ideal predicate.

## Shared ring/module source convention and topological inverse continuity

The 13 historical source-convention deferrals remain bounded by the exact
source versions in the inventory. Ten concern scalar/identity/sheaf framework:

(400) should be adjudicated coherently without declaring all their sources
unital from attribution. Zero rings and zero modules require explicit handling;
field/domain nonzeroness is not a generic ring axiom.

The stored topological ring formulation requires continuous addition and
multiplication. Its ring prerequisite explicitly allows no identity. The
topological module formulation requires continuous addition and scalar action
but allows a nonunital action. Neither formulation explicitly requires
continuous negation. This gap is still present in the current effective graph.

**Counterexample within that broad reading:** take additive Z with the topology
of upward-closed subsets and zero multiplication. Addition is continuous:
for x+y in an upward-open U, the product of the upward neighborhoods of x
and y maps into U. Multiplication is constant 0 and continuous. Negation is
not continuous: the inverse image of {n:n≥0} is {n:n≤0}, not upward-open.
Take M=Z with the same topology and zero scalar action for the module analogue;
addition and constant scalar action are continuous, negation is not.

**Conditional remedy 1:** under a unital ring, r↦(−1)r equals negation and is
continuous by composing r↦(−1,r) with multiplication. Under a **unital action**,
m↦(−1)m gives continuous negation on M. A unit in R alone is insufficient
for the broad zero-action module convention.

**Conditional remedy 2:** retain nonunital rings/actions but explicitly require
the additive topological-group structure (including continuous inverse).
These are distinct choices of intended scope. Neither is applied or silently
chosen. Verify the cited source's actual conventions in a later authorized
source reconciliation; this is not a claim that the source itself is wrong.
Any Pass 2 deferral rationale must say which uncertainty is quarantined rather
than certifying the reconstructed broad formulation. It is not automatically
a gate blocker, and this report does not approve a deferral/gate decision.
