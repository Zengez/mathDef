### C2. Ext/Hom_R over a noncommutative R: type restriction not established

`form.788671763be693df.ext-group`, `occ.321952d7b781466d1856` → `meaning.3a0545450d7a5285.internal-hom-module`. Target describes Hom_R(M,N) as an R-module for **commutative** R. The source only says R-modules and has a legitimate unital left-module interpretation over any associative unital R. In general Hom_R(M,N) is an **abelian group**; an R-module structure by `(a·f)(m)=a·f(m)` need not preserve R-linearity when a is noncentral. E.g. for R=M₂(Q), multiplication by E₁₁ on `id_R` fails left R-linearity since E₁₁E₁₂≠E₁₂E₁₁.

**Decision:** retain **source coefficient-scope deferral plus a likely mismatched existing-edge target under general R**. The safe later alternatives require (a) evidence R is commutative in this source or (b) a typed additive Hom prerequisite suitable for arbitrary associative R. Do not claim Ext itself is wrong or automatically change all Hom targets. De Rham coefficients are a separate issue.

### C3. Local connectedness → global open basis: not literally identical, possible derived theorem

`form.ab260940ea9c523d.locally-connected-space`, `occ.cdc96f16a3e31332ec7d` → `meaning.6a3376e5ecb82bfb.topology-basis`. The source demands, **at each point x**, a cofinal system of connected neighborhoods. The existing target defines a **global family of open sets** forming a basis; a neighborhood system at a single x may include nonopen sets and is not itself the global object. Still, when the connected-neighborhood property holds for every point, connected components of each open U are open: every point y of its component has a connected neighborhood contained in U, necessarily contained in that component, with an open interior containing y. Those components supply a **connected-open global basis**.

**Decision:** **not** classify the reference as unconditionally false. It is **invalid as a literal object identity**, but might serve as a documented derived-prerequisite link if the source/reference semantics explicitly justify the all-points argument. Keep the separate correct missing local-neighborhood candidate `missing.eb8e1a62d06acf7c487c`; later edge remediation must choose direct versus derived semantics. This is lower-priority than C1/C2.

## D. Newly surfaced high-value semantic corrections/clarifications

- **Rank 400, relative de Rham complex `missing.ebf92c196007c438f394`:** for `A→B`, the de Rham exterior derivative `d:Ω^p_{B/A}→Ω^{p+1}_{B/A}` is **A-linear** and generally **not B-linear**. With A=k, B=k[t], `d(t)=dt` yet `t·d(1)=0`; assuming B-linearity produces a false complex of B-modules. Correct conceptual type: complex of **A-modules** (or underlying abelian groups), whose **terms** carry B-module structures, plus wedge products satisfying graded Leibniz. This does not invalidate relative de Rham cohomology.
- **Rank 250, `missing.659b5d5ed37a38428987`:** “n prime to characteristic” should mean `n·1_K` invertible; in characteristic zero every positive integer n qualifies. Literal `gcd(n,0)=1` is false for n>1, so avoid using that as an unqualified definition.
- **Rank 259, `missing.6e286e5d25f8e80fa9f6`:** “no greatest element” vs “no maximal element” only equivalent in a total order. In a two-point antichain, neither element is greatest but both are maximal. The Q-valued Dedekind-cut source is correctly totally ordered.
- **Rank 298, `missing.8f2dea7e2bd1211c0cf6`:** zero-divisor definitions vary on whether zero itself counts. Here the scan wording “zero-divisor phenomena” is partly explanatory, not evidence that the parent asserts existence of a nonzero zero divisor; this may eventually be a **dependency-necessity** issue as well as convention ambiguity. Do not force an existing zero-divisor resolution.
- **Rank 181:** `{a,b}` is not necessarily a doubleton; when a=b it is a singleton. In Kuratowski ordered-pair formulas the equal-coordinate case remains valid. Fix future wording without requiring distinct inputs.
- **Rank 95:** Z-graded additive decomposition generalizes an N-graded one by zero extension into negative degrees, but not conversely; graded pieces often fail to be submodules for degree-changing scalar multiplication.
- **Rank 172:** `(S_f)_0` under positive-degree homogeneous f is the specific projective-localization construction, not full `S_f`; a generic zero-degree component operation and the source's full definition must not self-resolve.
- **Rank 389:** approved compactness/FIP correction is verified as **NONEMPTY family of closed sets** in the effective formulation. Its empty-space case is consistent. Do not reopen as a new blocker.
- **Rank 420:** ring epimorphism f:R→S iff multiplication `S⊗_R S→S` is an isomorphism in commutative unital ring setting. This includes non-surjective epimorphisms such as `Z→Q`. Separate the canonical multiplication **map** from its isomorphism **criterion**; no automatic self-resolution.
