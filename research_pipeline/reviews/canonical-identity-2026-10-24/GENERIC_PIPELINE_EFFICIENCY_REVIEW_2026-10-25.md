## 3. Domain-neutral core versus domain policies

Proposed separation for future new subjects (NO new subject content/research now):

**Reusable engine** (subject-independent):
- concept / meaning / formulation / source / observation / source-rights records, multiple valid variants with explicit context;
- immutable source, formulation and occurrence IDs, exact hashes and parent/child reference graph;
- review states, typed relationship roles, uncertainty and provenance, append-only supersession;
- independent term-search and definition-text search, inline expandable nested definitions/cycle suppression, citations, suggestions/feedback, brand/theme configuration;
- adapter-controlled source retrieval, text normalization, safe rendering, tests/checkpoints, read-only review and controlled application machinery.

**Adapter or optional rule package** (subject-dependent):
- STEM mathematics notation, formula and LaTeX parsing, ring/module unit convention, category size;
- physical quantity/unit and dimensional-analysis constraints when appropriate;
- chemistry nomenclature/equation notation and domain-specific source families;
- education and discipline-based education research concepts that may use operational or empirically contingent definitions, disputed taxonomies, study-specific measurement instruments and context-bound constructs;
- trusted-source priorities, evidence confidence requirements, citation rights/format and domain-specific consistency heuristics.

Do not assume every STEM/education field admits one canonical axiomatic definition of a term. Preserve **context-specific and competing documented definitions** rather than forcing synonym collapse or silently erasing disciplines or measurement frameworks. Such support belongs in a shared schema's optional typed scope and review mechanism; specific domain interpretations belong in adapters, not global heuristics.
