## A. Polynomial roots and the quotient-field example: mathematical qualification confirmed

**Rank 40:** `missing.fa5932b42daa1a3e0307` (roots of a polynomial in a field). Current canonical context says that the root condition includes the residue class of the indeterminate in `K=F[T]/(f)` when `f` is merely **nonconstant**. One implicated source is `form.1fd51873f9f0afbd.field-from-irreducible-polynomial` with occurrence `occ.29b820e470569d95f584`.

A root of `f∈F[T]` in an extension field `K/F` is `α∈K` satisfying `f(α)=0`; evaluation is the map `F[T]→K` sending `T↦α`. For every nonconstant `f` the class `T+(f)` is a root **in the quotient ring** `F[T]/(f)`. That ring is a field **iff `f` is irreducible** over the field F. For example `F[T]/(T²)` contains the nonzero nilpotent class of T and is not a field. An arbitrary nonconstant reducible f can have a root in some extension field, but `F[T]/(f)` itself is not generally one.

**Decision:** Confirm `contextual_ambiguity_or_variant` solely for the canonical candidate *example/scope wording*. The source's explicit irreducibility qualification is correct and should not be weakened. Future safe candidate-context reconciliation should say, e.g., “includes the residue class of T in the extension field `F[T]/(f)` **when f is irreducible**,” while keeping the general root predicate independent of any quotient construction. **No current alias, candidate, source or graph change authorized**.

## B. Generated submodule in a nonunital-action module: formula needs convention

**Rank 54:** `missing.beea674f8fcd201a1af5` (submodule generated by S). The candidate context characterizes the generated submodule as **finite R-linear combinations** `Σr_i s_i` of elements of `S⊆M`, while the corpus retains both a general nonunital module-action meaning and a specialized unital-module meaning. The occurrence cited by Codex is `occ.03e7f2690d2d1f77a190` in `form.91ebb3796458d5bb.finite-generated-module`; two further parent contexts include a colimit-relation submodule and a finite-generation variant.

For a **unital R-module** (1_R m=m), the set of finite combinations `RS={Σr_i s_i:r_i∈R,s_i∈S}` contains S and is the least submodule containing S. For a general associative ring-action module, even over a ring with identity, `RS` may **not** contain S: with R=F₂, M=C₂ and identically zero scalar action, `R{m}={0}` for nonzero `m`; the generated submodule containing m is M. One general valid construction is the additive subgroup generated by S and RS, explicitly `⟨S⟩_ℤ+RS`, where finite integer sums of elements of S and finite R-linear combinations are taken. It is stable under R-action by associativity and includes S; it is contained in any submodule containing S. Equivalently, use the appropriate ring unitization R^+ and its action, with its convention spelled out. The empty generating set generates the zero submodule under both readings.

**Decision:** Keep `contextual_ambiguity_or_variant`, distinguish ordinary unital span from the general generated-submodule construction, and do not silently impose `1m=m` from the word “module.” This is mathematically consistent with the previously adopted module/ring convention deferrals. Future safe reconciliation can either constrain the formula to unital modules with source support or give a general-convention-specific variant. **No immediate source edit, alias split or graph change**.

## C. Natural transformation versus Yoneda correspondence: component identity only

**Rank 62:** `missing.a8c70a2a13320680c54c` is an ordinary natural transformation between presheaves `C^op→Sets`. The source `form.a570f5bed277bd8b.representable-presheaf-morphism`, occurrence `occ.099ea4270d9cbdccf52b`, originally had candidate `missing.7863e36c979e91435854`, aliased by `alias.global-audit.20261023.061`. Its historical reason correctly asserts that an ordinary transformation is the **prerequisite component**, whereas the whole Yoneda construction is an additional instance.

For a locally small category C, representable presheaf `h_U(X)=Hom_C(X,U)` and Set-valued presheaf G, the Yoneda correspondence is a **bijection**
`Nat(h_U,G) ≅ G(U)`.
Given `ξ∈G(U)`, define `η_X(f:X→U)=G(f)(ξ)`. Naturality follows from contravariant functoriality. Conversely `η↦η_U(id_U)`; the naturality square for f proves the inverse formula. A single natural transformation is **one element of the set** `Nat(h_U,G)`; it is not itself the bijection between this set and `G(U)`. The Yoneda statement also involves a representable presheaf, distinguished identity arrow, and naturality in the appropriate variables. Local-smallness/universe conventions remain separate.

**Decision:** Continue to classify the source as `contextual_ambiguity_or_variant` until its **whole correspondence vs component prerequisite** granularity is made explicit in an authorized context/occurrence record. The preexisting alias's **prerequisite-only** rationale may remain as recorded and should not be overstated as mathematical equality of full Yoneda data. **No automatic alias reversal, new identity or graph amendment**.

## D. Additional higher-priority edge warning: real-time versus discrete filtration

**Rank 88:** `missing.65d334caa51c845ea335` is a continuous-time filtration indexed over all `t≥0`. In `form.wave2.found85.adapted-continuous`, effective existing edge `occ.4dc2044654482ca4b966` reportedly points to an explicitly **discrete** filtration meaning.
