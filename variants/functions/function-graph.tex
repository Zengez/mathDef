For sets $X$ and $Y$, a \emph{function from $X$ to $Y$} is a subset $f\subseteq X\times Y$ such that for every $x\in X$ there exists a unique $y\in Y$ satisfying $(x,y)\in f$. The set $X$ is its domain, $Y$ is its specified codomain, and $f(x)$ denotes this unique $y$. Equivalently, the relation $f$ is total and single-valued on $X$.
