If $(X,d)$ is a metric space and $A\subseteq X$, then $\overline A=\{x\in X: d(x,A)=0\}$, where $d(x,A)=\inf\{d(x,a):a\in A\}$ and $\inf\varnothing=+\infty$. Equivalently, every open ball about $x$ meets $A$.
